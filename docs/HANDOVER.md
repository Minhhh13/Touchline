# TouchLine handover

For the people working on TouchLine: what it is, what works today, what is being built, where everything lives and how it is hosted. AI agents should start from [CLAUDE.md](../CLAUDE.md); the way we work together is in [CONTRIBUTING.md](../CONTRIBUTING.md).

## 1. What TouchLine is

A free web football game that fuses the wheel draft of **38-0** with the depth of **Football Manager**. A wheel lands on a real club in a real season, you draft one player from that squad, repeat until your side is built, then either simulate the season instantly or manage it week by week. Your side takes one real club's place in a real league season and plays the other clubs' real squads.

- Seven leagues: Premier League, La Liga, Serie A, Bundesliga, Ligue 1, Eredivisie, Primeira Liga, seasons 2008-09 to 2026-27 (about 69,000 player-seasons).
- Runs entirely in the browser, installable and playable offline. Saves live in the browser (`localStorage`). No account needed.
- Deterministic: one seed always plays out the same way on every device, which is what makes the Daily, challenge links and (soon) online leaderboards fair.

## 2. What it does today

**Modes**
- **Quick Draft** (the 38-0 mode): a 38-0 setup (Easy, Normal or Hard; ratings on or off; Squad First or Position First; Season or Prime ratings; era presets and a season slider; every formation tile drawn as a mini pitch), draft an XI and an optional bench of up to five, set the captain, penalty taker and instructions at kick-off, then watch the season game by game with a January window, three bench changes and injury cover, highlights of any game; share the result. On `season-release`: a Squad complete screen with the pundits' prediction, the January "Halfway there" panel, a manager picked from the real managers of the clubs drafted, the Cup and the Continental Cup after the league, and the 38-0 chase (win every game) through setup, playback, summary, profile and share text. On `cases-managers` (not pushed): the manager is spun (three reels at once, pick one of the three), and every casual Quick Draft ends with a case (a power-up for the Profile locker, unpacked in a later draft without the tray, or a rare Icon who can take a drafted starter's place, a non-standard season).
- **Super League** (a Quick Draft option, built on `super-league`, not pushed): the XI plays 38 games against the 19 greatest club-seasons in the data (one season per club, each at its best by the engine's team strength, full strength), then the Continental Cup against the next fifteen; the field is the same for everyone.
- **Daily Challenge**: the same wheel for everyone each day (UTC), with streaks.
- **Daily Arena** (on `casual-modes`): one arena puzzle a day for everyone (UTC), the Gauntlet and the Cup of 16 in turn on the Premier League wheel, with its own streak; one tap from Home's casual row.
- **Weekly Challenge** (on `casual-modes`): one themed quick-draft wheel a week for everyone (ISO week, UTC), across all seven leagues, nine themes in turn (teammate chain, European Nights, early years, Moneyball, hard mode, one nation, modern game, park the bus, Realistic), its own streak.
- **Season**: draft 16 to 22 players, then manage every matchweek: friendlies, live matches, a cup, a January window, a board that can sack you. Sim to the next big match (on `casual-modes`) plays the routine weeks and stops before derbies, title deciders, cup semi-finals and finals, an open run-in, the January window and a board alarm.
- **Dynasty**: Season mode that never ends: ageing, contracts, retirements, youth intake, summer draft and summer window.
- **Arena: The Cup** (on `casual-modes`): draft 16, then a knockout of 8, 16 or 32 real club-seasons in one sitting (or the one-off final, its two-club size, against a giant by default): the wheel, the champions, the giants, a decade or this season's Europe; seeds kept apart in round one, a redraw each round, four days between ties with bans that carry for every side, and the AI ties played around yours, on to a winner once you are out. Each tie managed live through the two stops and a shoot-out with your own penalty order and keeper, "Sim this tie", or Sim to the end. Its own save slot, a bracket on the hub and the summary, a share text written tie by tie and a card, Best runs on the Profile page and the cabinet's arena cups by size.
- **Arena: The Gauntlet** (on `casual-modes`): draft 14, then climb fifteen real club-seasons from the wheel, each from a stronger band than the last, away and at home in turn, until the first defeat; one lifeline (change a rung's draw), a bonus spin after rungs 2, 4, 6 and 8, managed live, by the assistant or Sim to the end. Everyone on a seed climbs the same ladder; rungs climbed go to Best runs and the cabinet's Gauntlet shelf.
- **Run-in** (on `casual-modes`): take over a real club at matchweek 28 with its real squad, a table projected from the real final one and a brief (the title, the top four, survival), four spins to strengthen, then the last ten games with Sim to the next big match; a verdict against the real club, a share text, the Profile page's Run-ins table and the cabinet's run-in shelf. Its own save slot.
- **Head-to-head**: two managers on one device draft from the same spins and play the same season.

**Draft systems**: no dead spins, eight formations, era / one-club / one-nation filters, hard mode (hidden ratings), re-spins, wheel twists every third spin (Golden, Basement, Blind, Decade), the Moneyball wheel (on `casual-modes`: a salary cap, every player priced by rating and position, never age), the Teammate Chain (on `casual-modes`: every pick a former teammate of the one before), European Nights (draft from all seven leagues), ghost-draft challenge links (`#c=...`) verified by replay.

**Management systems**: 34 roles and six team instructions, an xG match engine with live stops at half time and the 70th minute (talks, subs, touchline shouts), condition / sharpness / morale / familiarity, injuries and suspensions, training focus, squad status and playing time, player conversations, captain and set-piece takers, backroom staff, man-marking, press conferences and dressing-room events, the assistant's briefing, a domestic cup and an optional Continental Cup, a transfer market with bids, loans and part-exchange, finances, board objectives, in-season development, awards, 19 achievements, record books against real seasons, a share card, save export / import.

**Recruitment** (`fm-depth-2`, not pushed; Season and Dynasty): league knowledge and attributes shown as ranges until a full scout (one a market day or a matchweek) reveals a player and his hidden traits; scouting assignments; a recruitment focus that narrows the market and brings two graded scout reports a matchweek; contract talks with any player (agent moods, locked and excluded terms, a signing fee, a release clause AI clubs can pay, appearance and goal bonuses); an agent who finds a buyer for a 5% cut; sell-on shares on bids; board requests (funds, a backroom budget, Dynasty youth facilities) and the wage-to-transfer slider on a three-column Club tab; a data hub on the Stats tab (xG, xA, goals against xG, per 90, an analyst's note every five matchweeks). Nothing moves a seeded result unless used.

**Match day**: live matches play on a Football Manager style dashboard (both XIs as numbered shirts with live ratings, a central 2D pitch, switchable widgets such as heat map, match events, xG story, pass map, live table and latest scores, a commentary banner and our XI along the bottom with condition and morale); instructions, mentality, set-piece takers, substitutions (five in three windows, half time free) and, from 70, a shout can change the match at any minute, and the last twenty minutes play live to the whistle; the Live tab replays a finished match on the same screen; phones get the pitch first and a tab strip. On a desktop (1280 by 720 up) the board fits the window with no page scroll. The pitch has a real football carried and passed in spells of possession at a broadcast pace (1x is 3 s a match minute; 0.5x, 2x, 3x, 4x, remembered per browser), and every goal holds the clock for a celebration (the ball into the bulging net, a GOAL! banner in the club's colours, confetti for ours, a short subdued one for theirs). The Dugout remembers Watch, Auto-play or Sim result (`GameSettings.matchPlay`, optional): Watch opens the half-time and 70th-minute decisions as a centred pop-up (Play on, No changes, Let the assistant decide, Finish the match, Full sim), Auto-play lets the assistant answer both stops, Sim result goes straight to the report; Auto from here and Sim result sit on the top bar.

**Lineups**: tap a pitch slot in the Dugout for a picker (a bottom sheet on a phone) of everyone who can play there, rated there, with the occupant and To bench on top; in the Draft a player with several open slots shows one chip per slot.

**Trophy Room** (on `casual-modes`): the Profile page's cabinet counts every mode's titles, cups, 38-0s, invincibles and centurions, twelve tiered badges (bronze, silver, gold) show progress, a manager card downloads as a PNG, and Best seasons filters by mode. Older profiles keep their five trophy counts exactly; the rest is counted from their 50 best seasons.

**Help on the page**: seven one-time coach marks (draft reveal, bench picks, Squad and Tactics tabs, half time, match report, market), each gone after Got it and brought back by Show tips again on the Profile page; a Key chip on the Squad panel labels the condition, morale and sharpness bars and adds a pitch legend.

**Dugout at a glance** (`fm-ux`, not pushed; display only, no ENGINE_VERSION bump): squad rows carry a form arrow (last three ratings against the season), a last-five sparkline, a MOTM star and the morale face in place of the morale bar; the squad list switches between Overview, Form, Fitness and Contract views; a Next match tab holds the opposition report, our units against theirs and the team talk; Roles show each option's rating, a fit chip and familiarity; Fixtures open with a season calendar and the opponent's place; the Table tab draws the league position by matchweek; the Inbox has filter chips and a pinned Needs you row; a window countdown chip, a deadline-day ticker and Done deals on the Market; Space plays the week, 1 to 0 open the tabs. On the match day: Full, Extended and Key-moments highlight levels, the assistant's line at 15, 30, 60 and 80, "Since 70'" under the stats after a shout, and P to pause.

**Look**: light and dark themes (the sun and moon in the top bar flips them and remembers the choice; Auto on the Profile page follows the device live); a night-navy palette with cyan and violet-magenta neon accents (Daylight is a cool lavender); Home opens on our own cartoon drawing of a floodlit ground at night with three figures, a drawn picture on each of the four mode cards and compact tiles, with Lucide icons in place of emoji. No photos.

Full detail with formulas: [GAME_DESIGN.md](GAME_DESIGN.md). Data method: [DATA.md](DATA.md). Design research: [RESEARCH.md](RESEARCH.md).

## 5. Run it

Needs Node 22.13 or later.

```bash
git clone https://github.com/Minhhh13/Touchline.git   # private: you need collaborator access
cd Touchline
npm ci
npm run dev          # http://localhost:5173
npm test             # all Vitest suites
npm run typecheck
npm run build        # production build into dist/
npx vite preview --port 4173 --strictPort   # serve the build
```

Rebuilding the player data is rarely needed and downloads several large archives the first time: `node scripts/import/build.mjs`, with `DATA_CACHE=/path/to/datasets` to reuse a cache (default `.cache/datasets`; see [DATA.md](DATA.md)).

## 7. Known issues and limits

- **Data licensing.** Ratings come from community Kaggle datasets derived from EA Sports FC ratings (FC 24 CC0, FC 25 Apache 2.0, FC 26 CC0, FC 27 an unofficial non-commercial snapshot), the European Soccer Database (ODbL), openfootball results (public domain) and Wikidata (CC0). The ratings themselves originate with EA. Treat TouchLine as a non-commercial fan project; going commercial would need a different ratings source. Full credits in [DATA.md](DATA.md).
- **No real crests, kits, logos or player photos.** Clubs are shown as colours plus a letter code. Keep it that way. The only photos are freely licensed shots of grounds, grass and crowds in silhouette, credited in [CREDITS.md](CREDITS.md).
- **Free Supabase projects pause** after about a week idle; the online layer needs a keep-alive or a paid plan.
- Classic Premier League seasons 1992-93 to 2007-08 (on `classic-seasons`) can be chosen in Setup and are labelled, but not in head-to-head (its season picker and wheel leave them out); Ligue 1, the Eredivisie and the Primeira Liga have no real tables (no record books there).
- Fixed on `classic-seasons`: every left and right position of 2008-14 was swapped (the European Soccer Database's X coordinate read mirrored: A. Cole and P. Evra were right backs), and 23,725 rows of 2008-15 shipped `null` consistency and big-match traits (read as 0.5). Both corrected in the build and the packs rebuilt.
- About 230 players have an unknown nationality.
- Values and wages are our own model, not real figures.
- The default team name is still "Touchline XI" (set in the game logic and pinned by tests).
- In the Daylight theme a side in white kit draws near-white momentum bars and xG line on the light chart panel; on `bugfix-1` the momentum bars carry a thin outline and the chart is scaled, so they read, but the xG line is still pale.
- The match-day dashboard's heat map and its scale still use `--gold` as a heat colour (the design system keeps gold for trophies and records); a dedicated heat token would be cleaner.
- Some docs are partly stale (README's Node version, player-season count and roadmap line; DATA.md's England-only intro and totals; RESEARCH.md section 7).
