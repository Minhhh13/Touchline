# TouchLine handover

For the people working on TouchLine: what it is, what works today, what is being built, where everything lives and how it is hosted. AI agents should start from [CLAUDE.md](../CLAUDE.md); the way we work together is in [CONTRIBUTING.md](../CONTRIBUTING.md).

## 1. What TouchLine is

A free web football game that fuses the wheel draft of **38-0** with the depth of **Football Manager**. A wheel lands on a real club in a real season, you draft one player from that squad, repeat until your side is built, then either simulate the season instantly or manage it week by week. Your side takes one real club's place in a real league season and plays the other clubs' real squads.

- Seven leagues: Premier League, La Liga, Serie A, Bundesliga, Ligue 1, Eredivisie, Primeira Liga, seasons 2008-09 to 2026-27 (about 69,000 player-seasons).
- Runs entirely in the browser, installable and playable offline. Saves live in the browser (`localStorage`). No account needed.
- Deterministic: one seed always plays out the same way on every device, which is what makes the Daily, challenge links and (soon) online leaderboards fair.

## 2. What it does today

**Modes**
- **Quick Draft** (the 38-0 mode): draft an XI in 11 picks (two re-spins), simulate the whole league season in one click, share the result.
- **Daily Challenge**: the same wheel for everyone each day (UTC), with streaks.
- **Daily Arena** (on `casual-modes`): one arena puzzle a day for everyone (UTC), the Gauntlet and the Cup of 16 in turn on the Premier League wheel, with its own streak; one tap from Home's Today and this week row.
- **Weekly Challenge** (on `casual-modes`): one themed quick-draft wheel a week for everyone (ISO week, UTC), across all seven leagues, nine themes in turn (teammate chain, European Nights, early years, Moneyball, hard mode, one nation, modern game, park the bus, Realistic), its own streak.
- **Season**: draft 16 to 22 players, then manage every matchweek: friendlies, live matches, a cup, a January window, a board that can sack you. Sim to the next big match plays the routine weeks and stops before derbies, title deciders, cup semi-finals and finals, an open run-in, the January window and a board alarm.
- **Dynasty**: Season mode that never ends: ageing, contracts, retirements, youth intake, summer draft and summer window.
- **Arena: The Cup** (on `casual-modes`): draft 16, then a knockout of 8, 16 or 32 real club-seasons in one sitting (or the one-off final, its two-club size, against a giant by default): the wheel, the champions, the giants, a decade or this season's Europe; seeds kept apart in round one, a redraw each round, four days between ties with bans that carry for every side, and the AI ties played around yours, on to a winner once you are out. Each tie managed live through the two stops and a shoot-out with your own penalty order and keeper, "Sim this tie", or Sim to the end. Its own save slot, a bracket on the hub and the summary, a share text written tie by tie and a card, Best runs on the Record page and the cabinet's arena cups by size.
- **Arena: The Gauntlet** (on `casual-modes`): draft 14, then climb fifteen real club-seasons from the wheel, each from a stronger band than the last, away and at home in turn, until the first defeat; one lifeline (change a rung's draw), a bonus spin after rungs 2, 4, 6 and 8, managed live, by the assistant or Sim to the end. Everyone on a seed climbs the same ladder; rungs climbed go to Best runs and the cabinet's Gauntlet shelf.
- **Head-to-head**: two managers on one device draft from the same spins and play the same season.

**Draft systems**: no dead spins, eight formations, era / one-club / one-nation filters, hard mode (hidden ratings), re-spins, wheel twists every third spin (Golden, Basement, Blind, Decade), the Moneyball wheel (on `casual-modes`: a salary cap, every player priced by rating and position, never age), the Teammate Chain (on `casual-modes`: every pick a former teammate of the one before), European Nights (draft from all seven leagues), ghost-draft challenge links (`#c=...`) verified by replay.

**Management systems**: 34 roles and six team instructions, an xG match engine with live stops at half time and the 70th minute (talks, subs, touchline shouts), condition / sharpness / morale / familiarity, injuries and suspensions, training focus, squad status and playing time, player conversations, captain and set-piece takers, backroom staff, man-marking, press conferences and dressing-room events, the assistant's briefing, a domestic cup and an optional Continental Cup, a transfer market with bids, loans and part-exchange, finances, board objectives, in-season development, awards, 21 achievements, record books against real seasons, a share card, save export / import.

**Lineups**: tap a pitch slot in the Dugout for a picker (a bottom sheet on a phone) of everyone who can play there, rated there, with the occupant and To bench on top; in the Draft a player with several open slots shows one chip per slot.

**Trophy Room** (on `casual-modes`): the Record page's cabinet counts every mode's titles, cups, 38-0s, invincibles and centurions, twelve tiered badges (bronze, silver, gold) show progress, a manager card downloads as a PNG, and Best seasons filters by mode. Older profiles keep their five trophy counts exactly; the rest is counted from their 50 best seasons.

**Help on the page**: seven one-time coach marks (draft reveal, bench picks, Squad and Tactics tabs, half time, match report, market), each gone after Got it and brought back by Show tips again on the Record page; a Key chip on the Squad panel labels the condition, morale and sharpness bars and adds a pitch legend.

Full detail with formulas: [GAME_DESIGN.md](GAME_DESIGN.md). Data method: [DATA.md](DATA.md). Design research: [RESEARCH.md](RESEARCH.md).

## 5. Run it

Needs Node 22.13 or later.

```bash
git clone https://github.com/Minhhh13/Touchline.git   # private: you need collaborator access
cd Touchline
npm ci
npm run dev          # http://localhost:5173
npm test             # all Vitest suites
npm run typecheck
npm run build        # production build into dist/
npx vite preview --port 4173 --strictPort   # serve the build
```

Rebuilding the player data is rarely needed and downloads several large archives the first time: `node scripts/import/build.mjs`, with `DATA_CACHE=/path/to/datasets` to reuse a cache (default `.cache/datasets`; see [DATA.md](DATA.md)).

## 6. Hosting and backend

### Render (hosting)

Both sites are static sites in the Render project **TouchLine**. That Render workspace also holds an unrelated project; never change anything outside the TouchLine project.

| Service | URL | Tracks | Use |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| `touchline` | https://touchline-o5ot.onrender.com | `claude/youthful-mccarthy-a57idu` (the working branch) | The live game |
| `touchline-preview` | https://touchline-preview.onrender.com | `claude/touchline-preview` | Previews of in-progress branches (today `reel-ui` and `fm-depth` merged) |

- Build command `npm ci && npm run build`, publish directory `dist`, no `BASE_PATH`.
- Auto-deploy on every push to the tracked branch (Render's GitHub app is installed on the repo).
- Environment variables: `VITE_SUPABASE_URL`, `VITE_SUPABASE_ANON_KEY`. These are the public publishable keys; Vite bakes them into the build, so changing them needs a redeploy.
- To deploy from `main` instead, change the tracked branch in the Render dashboard once `main` is the integration branch.
- Optional: rename the service to drop the `-o5ot` suffix, and add a custom domain.
- The GitHub Pages workflow (`.github/workflows/pages.yml`) is obsolete and is being removed.

## 7. Known issues and limits

- **Data licensing.** Ratings come from community Kaggle datasets derived from EA Sports FC ratings (FC 24 CC0, FC 25 Apache 2.0, FC 26 CC0, FC 27 an unofficial non-commercial snapshot), the European Soccer Database (ODbL), openfootball results (public domain) and Wikidata (CC0). The ratings themselves originate with EA. Treat TouchLine as a non-commercial fan project; going commercial would need a different ratings source. Full credits in [DATA.md](DATA.md).
- **No real crests, kits, logos or photos.** Clubs are shown as colours plus a letter code. Keep it that way.
- **Free Supabase projects pause** after about a week idle; the online layer needs a keep-alive or a paid plan.
- No player data before 2008-09; Ligue 1, the Eredivisie and the Primeira Liga have no real tables (no record books there).
- About 230 players have an unknown nationality.
- Values and wages are our own model, not real figures.
- The product name is TouchLine, but many screens, the manifest and the share card still say "Touchline" until the redesign lands.
- Theme "Auto" always shows the dark theme.
- Some docs are partly stale (README's Node version, player-season count and roadmap line; DATA.md's England-only intro and totals; RESEARCH.md section 7).
- Saves are per browser; clearing site data loses them unless exported from the Record page (cloud saves will fix this).
- **Arena: the giants pass blocks the page.** The first arena draft of a session to finish works out the team strength of every club-season in all seven leagues (`giantsOf`, about 0.9 s on a desktop, longer on a phone) inside `finishDraft`, and the page does not respond meanwhile. It is memoised per universe, so it happens once a session.
- **Gauntlet: an era's ladder repeats a club.** A club appears once among the ladder's fifteen mains only when the pool has enough clubs: the full England wheel always does (200 of 200 ladders), but an era's smaller pool (2020-21 onwards: 140 club-seasons; 2014-15 to 2019-20: 120) falls back to an unused club-season of the band, so every such ladder has some club twice (`buildLadder`, `src/engine/gauntlet.ts`).
- **Arena: a drafted player can also line up for the opponent.** As in Season mode, an arena opponent keeps its real squad, so a man you drafted from that club-season can appear on both sides of the final. Per-player maps in the match result (`conditionLoss`, `motmId`) are keyed by player-season id and cannot tell the two apart; harmless for a one-match final; the Cup now carries the condition of a side that beats you, so a man on both sides takes the one figure twice there (rare, small, not yet settled).
