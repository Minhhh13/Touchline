# TouchLine handover

For the people working on TouchLine: what it is, what works today, what is being built, where everything lives and how it is hosted. AI agents should start from [CLAUDE.md](../CLAUDE.md); the way we work together is in [CONTRIBUTING.md](../CONTRIBUTING.md).

## 1. What TouchLine is

A free web football game that fuses the wheel draft of **38-0** with the depth of **Football Manager**. A wheel lands on a real club in a real season, you draft one player from that squad, repeat until your side is built, then either simulate the season instantly or manage it week by week. Your side takes one real club's place in a real league season and plays the other clubs' real squads.

- Seven leagues: Premier League, La Liga, Serie A, Bundesliga, Ligue 1, Eredivisie, Primeira Liga, seasons 2008-09 to 2026-27 (69,155 player-seasons), plus the Premier League's classic seasons 1992-93 to 2007-08 (8,903 player-seasons with estimated ratings, opt-in).
- Runs entirely in the browser, installable and playable offline. Saves live in the browser (`localStorage`). No account needed; an optional account adds cloud saves, the world leaderboards and online head-to-head rooms.
- Deterministic: one seed always plays out the same way on every device, which is what makes the Daily, challenge links, the online leaderboards and the online rooms fair (the server replays every ranked run).

## 2. What it does today

Everything below is on `main` and live on https://touchline.quest, through PR #69 (squash `5ddbd7b`, 2026-10-26).

**Modes**
- **Quick Draft** (the 38-0 mode): a one-page 38-0 setup (Easy, Normal or Hard; ratings on or off; Squad First or Position First; Season or Prime ratings, Prime by default; era presets and a season slider; every formation tile drawn as a mini pitch), draft an XI and a bench of up to five (one place by default), then Squad complete: the pundits' prediction (the chance of a perfect and an unbeaten season among it), a scouting report on the XI and the manager's edge. The manager is spun (three reels of real managers from the clubs drafted, pick one) or picked; the draft ends with a recap (a grade from A to D, the best pick, the one that got away, the Hindsight XI: the best XI the same spins held) and a case (a power-up for the Profile locker, unpacked in a later draft, or a rare Icon who can take a drafted starter's place, a non-standard season). Set the captain, penalty taker and instructions at kick-off, then watch the season game by game beside a live table, with three bench changes and injury cover, highlights of any game, and a January window that offers the January spin (a tiered case reel onto a wheel player), the January Upgrader (one roll on one of the XI), the transfer market or Skip. After the league come the Cup and the Continental Cup, each revealed round by round in a full bracket and ending on our own cartoon trophy lifted; the league with both cups is the Treble, with its own celebration. The 38-0 chase (win every game) runs through setup, playback, summary, profile and share text. Setup's Modes add Crazy, Hard, Underdog, Legends, Speedrun, Gamble and the Upgrader, and a power-up tray; spin odds show in every mode and always add up to 100% (marked rounded unless exact).
- **Super League** (a Quick Draft option): the XI plays 38 games against the 19 greatest club-seasons in the data (one season per club, each at its best by the engine's team strength), then the Continental Cup against the next fifteen; the field is the same for everyone.
- **Daily Challenge**: the same wheel for everyone each day (UTC), two re-spins, with streaks.
- **Daily Arena**: one arena puzzle a day for everyone (UTC), the Gauntlet and the Cup of 16 in turn on the Premier League wheel, with its own streak; one tap from Home's casual row.
- **Weekly Challenge**: one themed quick-draft wheel a week for everyone (ISO week, UTC), across all seven leagues, nine themes in turn (teammate chain, European Nights, early years, Moneyball, hard mode, one nation, modern game, park the bus, Realistic), its own streak.
- **Season**: draft 16 to 22 players, then manage every matchweek: friendlies, live matches, a cup, a January window, a board that can sack you. Sim to the next big match plays the routine weeks and stops before derbies, title deciders, cup semi-finals and finals, an open run-in, the January window and a board alarm.
- **Dynasty**: Season mode that never ends: ageing, contracts, retirements, youth intake, summer draft and summer window.
- **Arena: The Cup**: draft 16, then a knockout of 8, 16 or 32 real club-seasons in one sitting (or the one-off final against a giant by default): the wheel, the champions, the giants, a decade or this season's Europe; seeds kept apart in round one, a redraw each round, four days between ties with bans that carry for every side, and the AI ties played around yours, on to a winner once you are out. Each tie managed live through the two stops and a shoot-out with your own penalty order and keeper, "Sim this tie", or Sim to the end. Its own save slot, a bracket on the hub and the summary, a share text written tie by tie and a card, Best runs on the Profile page and the cabinet's arena cups by size.
- **Arena: The Gauntlet**: draft 14, then climb fifteen real club-seasons from the wheel, each from a stronger band than the last, away and at home in turn, until the first defeat; one lifeline (change a rung's draw), a bonus spin after rungs 2, 4, 6 and 8, managed live, by the assistant or Sim to the end. Everyone on a seed climbs the same ladder; rungs climbed go to Best runs and the cabinet's Gauntlet shelf.
- **Run-in**: take over a real club at matchweek 28 with its real squad, a table projected from the real final one and a brief (the title, the top four, survival), four spins to strengthen, then the last ten games with Sim to the next big match; a verdict against the real club, a share text, the Profile page's Run-ins table and the cabinet's run-in shelf. Its own save slot.
- **Head-to-head**: two managers on one device draft from the same spins and play the same season. **Online rooms** (online build): make a room, send the link (`#h=...`) or the code, and draft against a friend on their own device, guests welcome with no account; the server holds the seed, replays every pick and plays the season; online results go on their own Trophy Room shelf.

**Draft systems**: no dead spins, eight formations, era / one-club / one-nation filters, hard mode and blind mode (ratings hidden while choosing, shown once a player is in your squad), re-spins, wheel twists every third spin (Golden, Basement, Blind, Decade), the Moneyball wheel (a salary cap, every player priced by rating and position, never age), the Teammate Chain (every pick a former teammate of the one before), European Nights (draft from all seven leagues), classic seasons 1992-93 to 2007-08 (one Setup toggle, "Estimated ratings" labels everywhere), pick grades on a finished draft, ghost-draft challenge links (`#c=...`, short `#s=...` online) verified by replay, and the case reel with its synthesised sound (ticks timed from the strip's curve, landing sounds by rarity; the speaker toggle and Sound on the Profile page). Every rating says what it is: the overall on the badge, the number in a slot (the disc on the pitch) with its out-of-position cost, and the attribute figure in a tooltip.

**Management systems**: 34 roles and six team instructions, an xG match engine with live stops at half time and the 70th minute (talks, subs, touchline shouts), condition / sharpness / morale / familiarity, injuries and suspensions, training focus, squad status and playing time, player conversations, captain and set-piece takers, backroom staff, man-marking, press conferences and dressing-room events (a pending decision is one strip inline in the Dugout), the assistant's briefing, a domestic cup and an optional Continental Cup (Cup and Europe tabs on the same bracket), a transfer market with bids, loans and part-exchange, finances, board objectives, in-season development, awards, 35 achievements, record books against real seasons, a share card, save export / import.

**See your squad** (Season and Dynasty): the Dressing room tab (hierarchy, groups, atmosphere, happiness with a morale log), the Planner tab (depth and needs, contracts, the medical centre), personality labels, league awards on the summary, Inbox actions.

**Recruitment** (Season and Dynasty): league knowledge and attributes shown as ranges until a full scout (one a market day or a matchweek) reveals a player and his hidden traits; scouting assignments; a recruitment focus that narrows the market and brings two graded scout reports a matchweek; contract talks with any player (agent moods, locked and excluded terms, a signing fee, a release clause AI clubs can pay, appearance and goal bonuses); an agent who finds a buyer for a 5% cut; sell-on shares on bids; board requests (funds, a backroom budget, Dynasty youth facilities) and the wage-to-transfer slider on a three-column Club tab; a data hub on the Stats tab (xG, xA, goals against xG, per 90, an analyst's note every five matchweeks). Nothing moves a seeded result unless used.

**Match day**: live matches play on a Football Manager style dashboard (both XIs as numbered shirts with live ratings, a central 2D pitch, switchable widgets such as heat map, match events, xG story, pass map, live table and latest scores, a commentary banner and our XI along the bottom with condition and morale; tap a man in the strip and he is picked out on the pitch); instructions, mentality, set-piece takers, substitutions on a drag-and-drop board (five in three windows, half time free) and, from 70, a shout can change the match at any minute, and the last twenty minutes play live to the whistle; the Live tab replays a finished match on the same screen; phones get the pitch first and a tab strip. On a desktop the board fits the window with no page scroll. The pitch follows Football Manager's 2D view: phases of build-up (from the back, through midfield, the final third, counters, staged set pieces), the ball in flight over its shadow, the man on the ball's name, pass lines and xG-coloured shot lines, runs onto the ball, the camera on big chances and goal replays; 1x is a broadcast pace (0.5x to 4x, remembered per browser), and every goal holds the clock for a celebration. The Dugout remembers Watch, Auto-play or Sim result: Watch opens the half-time and 70th-minute decisions as a centred pop-up (Play on, No changes, Let the assistant decide, Finish the match, Full sim), Auto-play lets the assistant answer both stops, Sim result goes straight to the report. Full, Extended and Key-moments highlight levels, the assistant's line at 15, 30, 60 and 80, and P to pause.

**Lineups**: tap a pitch slot in the Dugout for a picker (a bottom sheet on a phone) of everyone who can play there, rated there, with the occupant and To bench on top; in the Draft a player with several open slots shows one chip per slot.

**Dugout at a glance** (display only): squad rows carry a form arrow, a last-five sparkline, a MOTM star and the morale face; the squad list switches between Overview, Form, Fitness and Contract views; a Next match tab holds the opposition report, our units against theirs and the team talk; Roles show each option's rating, a fit chip and familiarity; Fixtures open with a season calendar; the Table tab draws the league position by matchweek; the Inbox has filter chips and a pinned Needs you row; a window countdown, a deadline-day ticker and Done deals on the Market; Space plays the week, 1 to 0 open the tabs.

**Trophy Room**: the Profile page's cabinet counts every mode's titles, cups, 38-0s, invincibles and centurions, with their own shelves for the arena, the run-in and online head-to-head; twelve tiered badges (bronze, silver, gold) show progress; an honours board lists every Treble and Double season; a manager card downloads as a PNG, and Best seasons filters by mode. Older profiles keep their five trophy counts exactly.

**Online** (the live build carries the Supabase variables; every mode still works signed out and offline): create an account or sign in by email code from the top bar, a profile with a checked display name, a flag and a favourite club; cloud saves for the Quick Draft (with the Daily and both Weeklies), Season, Dynasty and head-to-head slots (arena runs and Run-ins stay on the device); the World screen with the Daily board, the weekly challenge, all-time totals and best streaks, and the 38-0 chase (Quick Draft runs on server-issued wheels, five a day, posted from the Summary), each with a country filter; short challenge links; online head-to-head rooms; the Privacy and fair play page. Every ranked result is replayed by the Edge Function; a posted claim is only compared.

**Help on the page**: seven one-time coach marks (draft reveal, bench picks, Squad and Tactics tabs, half time, match report, market), each gone after Got it and brought back by Show tips again on the Profile page; a Key chip on the Squad panel labels the condition, morale and sharpness bars and adds a pitch legend; the manager spin and the case explain what each manager and item brings. Every modal is a real dialog (focus kept inside, Escape closes, focus returns).

**Look**: light and dark themes (the sun and moon in the top bar flips them and remembers the choice; Auto on the Profile page follows the device live); a night-navy palette with cyan and violet-magenta neon accents (Daylight is a cool lavender); one typeface, Sora; Home opens on our own cartoon drawing of a floodlit ground at night, a drawn picture on each mode card that never crops its subject, with Lucide icons in place of emoji. No photos. Shell screens fit one desktop window with boxes that scroll inside themselves, the top bar folds away, and a Boxes button on most screens ticks boxes on or off.

## 7. Known issues and limits

- **Data licensing.** Ratings come from community Kaggle datasets derived from EA Sports FC ratings (FC 24 CC0, FC 25 Apache 2.0, FC 26 CC0, FC 27 an unofficial non-commercial snapshot), the European Soccer Database (ODbL), openfootball results (public domain) and Wikidata (CC0). The ratings themselves originate with EA. Treat TouchLine as a non-commercial fan project; going commercial would need a different ratings source. Full credits in [DATA.md](DATA.md).
- **No real crests, kits, logos or player photos.** Clubs are shown as colours plus a letter code. Keep it that way. The pictures are our own drawings, with no photos ([CREDITS.md](CREDITS.md)).
- **Engine 5 pauses the W44 Weekly's ranking.** The release just after 00:00 UTC on 2026-10-27 moves every replayed run, and a function verifies only its own engine's pins: W44's Weekly was pinned on engine 4 on Monday 26 October, so from the release until Monday 2 November no W44 Weekly ranks (the casual Weekly still plays; yesterday's Daily and open e4 chase attempts and rooms finish on `touchline-e4`). W45 is pinned on engine 5.
- **A Daily pinned on engine 4 just after midnight stays engine 4 all day.** A Daily is pinned by the first ranked request of the UTC day (a signed-in visitor's page load, or a post), and `touchline-e4` pins while the live site still says engine 4. Merged at 00:00 UTC, any signed-in visitor on the old site before Render serves the e5 `build.json` (CI plus the deploy, about ten minutes, plus up to five minutes of the function's cached copy, `LIVE_BUILD_TTL_MS`) pins 2026-10-27's Daily on engine 4, and no e5 run ranks that day (Home says "Unranked today", posts read "this board opened on an earlier version"). Avoid it by having the e5 site live by about 23:50 UTC (yesterday's Daily then loses its last minutes of e5 posts instead), or repair it before anyone posts (SQL in the `polish-online` notes; warm `touchline-e5` isolates keep their cached pin until they recycle). Engine 5 itself was merged at 23:43 UTC on 2026-10-26 for this reason (the main session watches the 10-27 pin); the note stands for the next engine release.
- **`room.pick` does not check the room's engine** (`roomMove` in `src/online/server/service.ts`; `room.join` does): a hand-made call to `touchline-e5` for an e4 room would replay it on engine 5. The game never sends one (the room screen refuses another version's room), so it waits for the next server change (a bundle rename and a redeploy).
