# TouchLine handover

For the people working on TouchLine: what it is, what works today, what is being built, where everything lives and how it is hosted. AI agents should start from [CLAUDE.md](../CLAUDE.md); the way we work together is in [CONTRIBUTING.md](../CONTRIBUTING.md).

## 1. What TouchLine is

A free web football game that fuses the wheel draft of **38-0** with the depth of **Football Manager**. A wheel lands on a real club in a real season, you draft one player from that squad, repeat until your side is built, then either simulate the season instantly or manage it week by week. Your side takes one real club's place in a real league season and plays the other clubs' real squads.

- Seven leagues: Premier League, La Liga, Serie A, Bundesliga, Ligue 1, Eredivisie, Primeira Liga, seasons 2008-09 to 2026-27 (about 69,000 player-seasons).
- Runs entirely in the browser, installable and playable offline. Saves live in the browser (`localStorage`). No account needed.
- Deterministic: one seed always plays out the same way on every device, which is what makes the Daily, challenge links and (soon) online leaderboards fair.

## 2. What it does today

**Modes**
- **Quick Draft** (the 38-0 mode): a 38-0 setup (Easy, Normal or Hard; ratings on or off; Squad First or Position First; Season or Prime ratings; era presets and a season slider; every formation tile drawn as a mini pitch), draft an XI and an optional bench of up to five, set the captain, penalty taker and instructions at kick-off, then watch the season game by game with a January window, three bench changes and injury cover, highlights of any game; share the result. On `season-release`: a Squad complete screen with the pundits' prediction, the January "Halfway there" panel, a manager picked from the real managers of the clubs drafted, the Cup and the Continental Cup after the league, and the 38-0 chase (win every game) through setup, playback, summary, profile and share text.
- **Daily Challenge**: the same wheel for everyone each day (UTC), with streaks.
- **Season**: draft 16 to 22 players, then manage every matchweek: friendlies, live matches, a cup, a January window, a board that can sack you.
- **Dynasty**: Season mode that never ends: ageing, contracts, retirements, youth intake, summer draft and summer window.
- **Head-to-head**: two managers on one device draft from the same spins and play the same season.

**Draft systems**: no dead spins, eight formations, era / one-club / one-nation filters, hard mode (hidden ratings), re-spins, wheel twists every third spin (Golden, Basement, Blind, Decade), European Nights (draft from all seven leagues), ghost-draft challenge links (`#c=...`) verified by replay.

**Management systems**: 34 roles and six team instructions, an xG match engine with live stops at half time and the 70th minute (talks, subs, touchline shouts), condition / sharpness / morale / familiarity, injuries and suspensions, training focus, squad status and playing time, player conversations, captain and set-piece takers, backroom staff, man-marking, press conferences and dressing-room events, the assistant's briefing, a domestic cup and an optional Continental Cup, a transfer market with bids, loans and part-exchange, finances, board objectives, in-season development, awards, 19 achievements, record books against real seasons, a share card, save export / import.

**Match day**: live matches play on a Football Manager style dashboard (both XIs as numbered shirts with live ratings, a central 2D pitch, switchable widgets such as heat map, match events, xG story, pass map, live table and latest scores, a commentary banner and our XI along the bottom with condition and morale); instructions, mentality, set-piece takers, substitutions (five in three windows, half time free) and, from 70, a shout can change the match at any minute, and the last twenty minutes play live to the whistle; the Live tab replays a finished match on the same screen; phones get the pitch first and a tab strip. On a desktop (1280 by 720 up) the board fits the window with no page scroll. The pitch has a real football carried and passed in spells of possession at a broadcast pace (1x is 3 s a match minute; 0.5x, 2x, 3x, 4x, remembered per browser), and every goal holds the clock for a celebration (the ball into the bulging net, a GOAL! banner in the club's colours, confetti for ours, a short subdued one for theirs). The Dugout remembers Watch, Auto-play or Sim result (`GameSettings.matchPlay`, optional): Watch opens the half-time and 70th-minute decisions as a centred pop-up (Play on, No changes, Let the assistant decide, Finish the match, Full sim), Auto-play lets the assistant answer both stops, Sim result goes straight to the report; Auto from here and Sim result sit on the top bar.

## 5. Run it

Needs Node 22.13 or later.

```bash
git clone https://github.com/Minhhh13/Touchline.git   # private: you need collaborator access
cd Touchline
npm ci
npm run dev          # http://localhost:5173
npm test             # all Vitest suites
npm run typecheck
npm run build        # production build into dist/
npx vite preview --port 4173 --strictPort   # serve the build
```

Rebuilding the player data is rarely needed and downloads several large archives the first time: `node scripts/import/build.mjs`, with `DATA_CACHE=/path/to/datasets` to reuse a cache (default `.cache/datasets`; see [DATA.md](DATA.md)).

## 7. Known issues and limits

- **Data licensing.** Ratings come from community Kaggle datasets derived from EA Sports FC ratings (FC 24 CC0, FC 25 Apache 2.0, FC 26 CC0, FC 27 an unofficial non-commercial snapshot), the European Soccer Database (ODbL), openfootball results (public domain) and Wikidata (CC0). The ratings themselves originate with EA. Treat TouchLine as a non-commercial fan project; going commercial would need a different ratings source. Full credits in [DATA.md](DATA.md).
- **No real crests, kits, logos or player photos.** Clubs are shown as colours plus a letter code. Keep it that way. The only photos are freely licensed shots of grounds, grass and crowds in silhouette, credited in [CREDITS.md](CREDITS.md).
- **Free Supabase projects pause** after about a week idle; the online layer needs a keep-alive or a paid plan.
- Classic Premier League seasons 1992-93 to 2007-08 (on `classic-seasons`) can be chosen in Setup and are labelled, but not in head-to-head (its season picker and wheel leave them out); Ligue 1, the Eredivisie and the Primeira Liga have no real tables (no record books there).
- Fixed on `classic-seasons`: every left and right position of 2008-14 was swapped (the European Soccer Database's X coordinate read mirrored: A. Cole and P. Evra were right backs), and 23,725 rows of 2008-15 shipped `null` consistency and big-match traits (read as 0.5). Both corrected in the build and the packs rebuilt.
- About 230 players have an unknown nationality.
- Values and wages are our own model, not real figures.
- The default team name is still "Touchline XI" (set in the game logic and pinned by tests).
- In the Daylight theme a side in white kit draws near-white momentum bars and xG line on the light chart panel (seen on the old live view; not yet rechecked on the match-day dashboard, which keeps the report's charts).
- The match-day dashboard's heat map and its scale still use `--gold` as a heat colour (the design system keeps gold for trophies and records); a dedicated heat token would be cleaner.
- Some docs are partly stale (README's Node version, player-season count and roadmap line; DATA.md's England-only intro and totals; RESEARCH.md section 7).
- One-screen gaps left after `home-fit` (desktop shell, 1280x720 and 2000x920): the head-to-head setup is a form about 1,100 px tall that scrolls inside its body (it needs a more compact layout, for example one row of formation tiles; the Setup work touches H2H.tsx), and the Dugout's Squad and Tactics boxes are only 100 to 300 px tall under the club header, briefing, tabs, opposition report and tip.
- Saves are per browser; clearing site data loses them unless exported from the Profile page (cloud saves will fix this).
- A season's full match detail passed the browser's 5 MB `localStorage` quota (4.4 MB for one Season at full time, and a parked game on top failed every write silently). Fixed on `save-compaction`: the save is compacted and compressed (about 50 K characters for a Season, under 100 K for a two-season Dynasty with a parked Season), and a refused write shows a toast instead of failing silently.
- The product name is TouchLine, but many screens, the manifest and the share card still say "Touchline" until the redesign lands.
- Quick Draft bench (`replica-release`): with a bench, the match engine's injury rate means an injured starter is covered in roughly 2 to 17 of 38 games; tune the quick season's injury lengths if that feels too busy. Injury cover only uses bench players who can play the slot, so a thin bench often leaves an injured man playing on.
