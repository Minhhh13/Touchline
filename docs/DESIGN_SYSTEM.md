# TouchLine: design system

The look of the game, written down so every screen is built the same way. The owner's brief: a proper logo, blue instead of green, a nicer design and a cleaner layout; on the Season setup screen "less text, more pictures", keeping the detail and the customisation; and the name written **TouchLine**, not in capitals. The goal is a product that looks finished and friendly on a phone and on a laptop.

Where it lives:

| What | Where |
|---|---|
| Tokens (colour, type, space, shape) and every shared component's CSS | `src/ui/styles.css` (tokens at the top, Floodlit then Daylight) |
| The name, the mark's and the lockup's geometry and SVG, colours for canvas drawing | `src/ui/brand.ts` (`APP_NAME`, `WORDMARK`, `MARK`, `LOCKUP`, `markSvg`, `BRAND_COLOURS`) |
| Logo components | `src/ui/components/Logo.tsx` (`Lockup`, `Logo`, `LogoMark`, `Wordmark`) |
| The info button with a tooltip | `Info` in `src/ui/components/bits.tsx` |
| Tier names in sentence case | `tierName()` in `src/ui/format.ts` |
| Favicon and app icons | `public/favicon.svg`, `public/icons/*.png`, regenerated by `node scripts/brand/icons.mjs` |
| The theme: Auto, Light, Dark | `src/ui/theme.ts` (`resolveTheme`, `toggledTheme`, `useResolvedTheme`), applied in `App.tsx`, painted early by the script in `index.html` |
| Icons | `lucide-react` (pinned), section 7; the per-meaning maps (`NEWS_ICON`, `TWIST_ICON`, `ACHIEVEMENT_ICON`, `AWARD_ICON`, `BriefingIcon`, `RedCard`, `YellowCard`) in `src/ui/components/icons.tsx` |

| Motion: tokens, View Transitions, count-ups, the celebration | `--dur-*`, `--ease-*`, `--stagger` in `styles.css` (the motion section); `src/ui/motion.ts`; `CountUp` in `bits.tsx`; `components/Celebrate.tsx`; section 12 |

The rule for everyone building screens: **use the tokens and the components below; no raw colours, no new one-off styles.** If a screen needs something this document does not cover, add it here and to `styles.css` first.

## 1. Principles

1. **Night football, calm, confident.** Deep night-navy surfaces, one cyan (a deep blue in Daylight) for actions, selection and focus, and a cyan-to-violet glow on the one primary button. Magenta lives only in the art. Neon is an accent, never a fill for whole panels. Status colours (good, warn, bad, gold) only ever mean status.
2. **Less text, more picture.** Badges, flags, league marks, pitch diagrams and numbers carry the information. Explanations sit behind an info icon, not in paragraphs under controls.
3. **One primary action per region.** Each panel, bar or modal has at most one `.btn.primary`. Everything else is secondary or ghost.
4. **Mixed case.** Titles and buttons are written as sentences. Capitals are kept for small labels and codes only.
5. **Phone first.** Every screen works at 390 px with no sideways scroll, and the main action is reachable with a thumb.

## 2. Name and logo

**The name is TouchLine**: one word, capital T and capital L, everywhere it is displayed (`APP_NAME` in `brand.ts`). Never "TOUCHLINE", "Touch Line" or "Touchline". Lower-case `touchline` stays only where it is a slug: the npm package, the repository, `localStorage` keys (`touchline-save-v1`), download file names and the service worker cache. Engine strings are unchanged: `shareText` keeps its `'Touchline'` default, and the UI passes `APP_NAME`.

**The mark** is the owner's sketch: a football corner flag (a white pole standing on the touchline, its foot ending at the line's top edge with nothing below it, a red pennant flying right from its top), a classic black-and-white ball resting on the line to the right of the pole, and a thin white touchline running on past both, on the blue tile. It replaced the pitch-corner mark of the first redesign pass. Everything is drawn from `brand.ts`, on a 64-unit grid (y down):

| Part | Full tier (64 px and up) |
|---|---|
| Tile | 64 × 64, corner radius 15, linear gradient `#3b82f6` (top left) to `#1d4ed8` (bottom right); no grass, the tile stays pure blue |
| Touchline | x 9 to 57 at y 51, stroke 1.6 (44 % of the first logo's 3.6), round caps |
| Pole | x 16, stroke 2.8, round caps, y 11.2 to 48.8: with its cap it ends exactly on the line's top edge (50.2); drawn as a rounded post (a slim white highlight left of centre and a soft shade along the right edge, one gradient over the white stroke) with a round finial (r 2 at y 10.6) carrying a radial highlight |
| Pennant | from the pole, top 12, bottom 27.2, tip (38.5, 19.2), the tip rounded (r 0.7); both edges carry the same wave (amplitude 1.7); drawn as cloth (see below) at strength 1.3 |
| Ball | r 11.5 at x 41.5, resting on the line's top edge (4 units of line run on past it): a true truncated icosahedron (front pentagon, five hexagons, rim panels), projected with curved seams, each panel shaded by its facing angle, a navy rim shade and a highlight at the top left |

Three detail tiers, picked from the rendered size by `markTier(px)`:

| Tier | Sizes | What changes |
|---|---|---|
| `full` | 64 px and up (app icons 192 and 512, apple-touch, the Home hero) | Everything above |
| `mid` | 22 to 63 px (the top bar, the loading screen, 24 to 48 px icons) | Flat ball: a white disc with a soft grey edge (`#c3cddb`) and black pentagons only, no seams or shading; pole 4 (standing on the line, a plain stroke, no finial), line 2.4 (half the first logo's 4.8), ball r 12 at x 41; the wave drops to 1.1 and the cloth to strength 0.6, so at 32 px it still reads as a clean red pennant |
| `tiny` | under 22 px (`favicon.svg`) | On the pixel grid (4 units are 1 px at 16 px): pole x 16 to 20 and y 8 to 48, standing on the line, which is one pixel (y 48 to 52, x 8 to 60), butt caps; a straight pennant 20 × 20 units (5 px) in flat red `#ef4444` (no folds or shade), ending clear of the ball; the colour ball (r 12 at 40, 36) carries real pentagon panels, clipped to the disc: the front one and those on the lower rim (`tinyPanels`), so the favicon still reads as a football when the browser draws it at 32 device px (one centred patch read as an eye there) while the top of the ball stays a clean white arc against the tile at 16 px (all the rim panels turned it into a grey check); the mono and inverse balls keep a single pentagon nudged up and left |

**The cloth** (`pennantCloth` and `clothStops` in `brand.ts`, colours in `MARK.cloth`). On the waving tiers and the lockup the pennant is one shape (`<path id="…-pc">`, in `<defs>`, animated once) drawn three times with `<use>`:

1. **Folds**: a linear gradient of 17 stops from the pole to the tip, slanted a little (its end 0.3 of the cloth's length below the tip) so the bands lean back from the upper edge. Each stop's light comes from the slope of the same wave the edges carry plus a ripple at twice its frequency travelling with it, so there are two or three soft light and dark bands; the red `#ef4444` is mixed toward `#ff8c7e` where the cloth faces the light and toward `#8c1212` where it turns away, with a soft knee so plain red stays between the crests, and the cloth by the pole is held flat.
2. **Light and shade**: a vertical gradient on the shape's own box (so it follows the cloth as it waves): white at 34 % along the upper edge fading out by 16 %, nothing in the middle, and `#450a0a` rising to 42 % along the lower edge (times the strength).
3. **The pole's shadow**: `#450a0a` at 50 % where the cloth meets the pole, gone 16 % of the way along.

Strength (`MARK.cloth.strength`) scales all three: 1.3 on the full tile, 0.6 on the mid tile, 1.1 on the lockup; the tiny tier is a single flat red path. The tip is rounded (`tip`, a five-point arc inside `tipX`), so it ends in a soft curve rather than a spike.

**The lockup** (`Lockup`) is the open, tile-less version: the mark's drawing at the left (the corner flag standing on a short touchline, the ball resting on it right of the pole, the line ending a little past the ball), then "TouchLine" standing on the same ground: the name's baseline is the line's top edge. There is no line under the name. Lengths are in u = height / 64 (`LOCKUP` in `brand.ts`, mirrored in the `.tl-*` rules of `styles.css`; a test checks the two agree):

The mark is sized to the name (the owner's "balanced and well ratioed with the text"; a test holds the ratios):

- The name is real text in Sora 700 at 48u from 57u (cap height 35.0u, x-height 26.4u, letter spacing 0.012em); its line box starts at 10.7u (Sora's baseline sits 0.84 of the size down a line-height-1 box), so its baseline lands at 51u, on the line. "Touch" (`.tl-touch`) and "Line" each carry a top-lit gradient clipped to the letters over a solid fallback colour: on Floodlit "Touch" `#ffffff` to `#c9d5e8` and "Line" `#9ccaff` to `#4b8df8`; on Daylight "Touch" `#2d3d5e` to `#0d1526` and "Line" `#2563eb` to `#1e3a8a` (every end clears 4.5:1 on the page). Under `forced-colors` both fall back to plain text.
- The mark, finial to baseline, is 40.8u: 1.16 cap heights. The pennant's top (14u) sits 2u above the cap tops; the ball's diameter (24u) is 0.91 of the x-height.
- The line: top 51u, 2u thick (1 px at the smallest lockup, 32 px), from the pole's left edge (7.5u) for 37u, to 4u past the ball; then 12.5u (about half a letter, 0.38 of an "o") before the name.
- Finial top (10.2u) to line bottom (53u) is centred in the 64u box (31.6u).
- The pole: x 9u, 3u wide, 13.4u to 49.5u, its round foot on the line's top edge; a rounded post like the full tile's (highlight and shade over `--tl-ink`) with a finial r 2.2u at 12.4u. The pennant: top 14u, bottom 26u, tip (37.5u, 19u), tip radius 1.3u, cloth at strength 1.1.
- The ball: r 12u resting on the line at x 28.5u, a clear 6u right of the pole and clear of the pennant at every frame of the wave. It is the flat tier below a 40 px diameter (every size in the app today), with a darker edge (`#94a3b8`), plus a soft navy rim shade, a small highlight at the top left and a contact shadow (a soft ellipse, r 0.85 × 0.14 of the ball, drawn under it) where it sits on the line.
- Width is about 317u: 515 px at 104 px high (the Home hero), 297 px at 60 (the hero on a phone), 198 px at 40 (the top bar), 158 px at 32 (the top bar on a phone).

Colours: on Floodlit the pole and the line are white; on Daylight they, and a thin ring round the ball, take `--text` (`#0a1b51`), the same navy as the name. The pennant stays red on both. All of it follows the theme through CSS variables (`--tl-ink`, `--tl-ball-ring`).

**Motion.** CSS keyframes only, no JavaScript; the resting state is the finished logo, so `prefers-reduced-motion` switches the animations off and shows it.

- **Shine:** an `aria-hidden` copy of the name lies over it (generated content, `::before { content: attr(data-text) }`, so the page text, find-in-page and copy see the name once), its text transparent, its background a narrow 110° band (pale blue edge `#93c5fd`, white core on Floodlit; `#bfdbfe` edge, white core on Daylight) clipped to the letters with `background-clip: text`; `background-position` sweeps it left to right once. Alongside it a `mask-image` wipe at the same angle and size holds the letters ahead of the band at 68 % and brings them up to full behind it, so the glint reads on white "Touch" as well as on blue "Line". The shine uses an even ease (`cubic-bezier(.45, .05, .4, 1)`) so it is seen over both halves.
- **Line:** `transform: scaleX(0 → 1)` from the left, so the short touchline draws itself out from under the pole.
- **Ball:** it fades in clear of the pole and rolls 5u along the line to its spot, turning 23.9° (distance / radius, so it rolls rather than slides). It never touches the pole or the letters.
- **Pennant flutter:** the cloth waves in a light wind, always, on every lockup (the Home hero and the top bar): a SMIL `<animate>` on the pennant's `d` steps through 8 shapes of a wave travelling along the cloth (`pennantWave`, amplitude 2.2u, a 2.6 s loop whose last frame is its first), so it runs in every browser; the edge at the pole never moves, and the pole and the ball stay still. The folds move with it: each of the fold gradient's stops carries its own `<animate>` on `stop-color` through the same 8 frames (`clothStops` at each frame's phase), and the light-and-shade gradient follows the shape's box, so the bands travel along the cloth with the wave. Under `prefers-reduced-motion` (`useReducedMotion` in `Logo.tsx`) every `<animate>` is left out and the pennant keeps its resting shape and folds. The tile mark, the favicon, the app icons and the share card never wave.
- The line and the ball share one easing (`cubic-bezier(.25, .65, .3, 1)`) and 1.2 s.
- When: on the Home hero once per page load (0.15 s delay; going back to Home later does not replay it), and on the top-bar lockup on hover (pointer devices) or keyboard focus.

Components (`Logo.tsx`):

```tsx
<Lockup height={40} animate="hover" />   // the top bar (32 px on a phone, set through --h in styles.css)
<Lockup height={104} animate="load" />   // the Home hero, inside the h1 (72 px on a phone)
<Logo size="lg" />                       // the compact lockup, tile + name: the loading screen and tight headers
<LogoMark size={72} />                   // the mark alone
<LogoMark size="md" variant="mono" title="TouchLine" />
<Wordmark />                             // "Touch" + "Line" in the accent blue, real text
```

`Lockup` takes `height` in px (it picks the ball's tier; a stylesheet may resize it through `--h`) and `animate` (`none`, `load`, `hover`). `LogoMark` sizes: `sm` 24, `md` 32, `lg` 48, `xl` 96, or any number of pixels; the tier follows the size. In the compact `Logo` the wordmark is 0.82 × the mark's size, with a gap of 0.3 × the mark.

Variants: `colour` (the blue tile; the default and the app icon), `mono` (no tile, everything in `currentColor`, the ball's panels knocked out of a solid disc: for light ink on a photo or a primary button), `inverse` (a white tile with the pole, the line and an outlined ball in the tile blue, the pennant red: on a blue or navy background, and the pattern to follow for dark ink).

Rules:

- Clear space around the mark: at least a quarter of its size; around the lockup, at least the ball's diameter (23u). Smallest sizes: the mark 16 px (the favicon), the lockup 32 px high.
- Never recolour the tile or the pennant, stretch either, outline the tile, put the colour tile on the primary blue (use `inverse`), add grass or a second ball, or set the name in capitals or with a space.
- The mark is decorative next to the wordmark (`aria-hidden`); give `LogoMark` a `title` when it stands alone. In the lockup only the name is read; the shine copy, the flag and the ball are hidden.
- SVG ids are prefixed per instance (`useId`), so two logos on a screen never share a gradient or a clip.
- After any change to `MARK`, run `node scripts/brand/icons.mjs` (Node 22.18+ and Playwright; set `PLAYWRIGHT_MODULE` to Playwright's entry file if it is not installed in the project). It writes `public/favicon.svg` (the tiny tier), `icons/icon-192.png` and `icon-512.png` (full tier, rounded tile, transparent corners), `icons/icon-maskable-512.png` (full bleed, glyph at 78 % inside the safe circle) and `icons/apple-touch-icon.png` (full bleed, opaque).
- The share card (`shareCard.ts`) draws the open lockup (the short line, the resting pennant with its folds, the pole and its finial, no line under the name) with `Path2D` from `LOCKUP`, `pennantPath` and `ballPanels`, 76 px high, in the Floodlit colours of `BRAND_COLOURS`, whatever the viewer's theme.

## 3. Colour

Two themes share every token name: **Floodlit** (dark, "Dark" in the settings) and **Daylight** (light, "Light", `[data-theme='light']`). Only use the names; the values change with the theme.

The profile's theme setting is Auto, Light or Dark (Profile screen). `App.tsx` always sets `data-theme` on `<html>` to what is on screen: the fixed choice, or for Auto the device's `prefers-color-scheme`, followed live (`useResolvedTheme` listens to the media query, so switching the phone to dark mode switches the game at once). A small script in `index.html` sets the same attribute before the app loads, so a light device never flashes the dark page. The CSS default with no attribute is still Floodlit. The sun and moon button in the top bar flips Light and Dark and stores the result, so after one tap the setting is fixed until Auto is chosen again. The browser's `theme-color` follows `--bg`.

## 4. Typography

One family: **Sora** (Google Fonts, SIL Open Font License; 400, 600, 700, 800 from one stylesheet in `index.html`) for titles, big numbers, the call to action and body text alike: a wide geometric sans with flat, squared terminals, our free stand-in for the look of a European club-competition broadcast face (the real broadcast fonts are exclusive and are never used). `--font-display` and `--font-body` both name it, so a rule can still say which role it plays; the fallbacks are `'Segoe UI', system-ui, -apple-system, Roboto, sans-serif`. `--mono` for seeds, minutes and codes. Sora is about 45 % wider than the old condensed display face, so display sizes were cut where a box is fixed (below). The share cards draw in Sora too and wait (up to 2 s) for `document.fonts` before drawing; `public/sw.js` caches the Google Fonts stylesheet and files cache-first, so the type survives offline after one visit.

## 6. Components

All of these are global classes or components already in the code; screens compose them.

### Buttons (`.btn`)

| Variant | Class | When |
|---|---|---|
| Primary | `.btn.primary` | The one forward action of a region: "Spin the wheel", "Kick off the season", "Continue →" |
| Secondary | `.btn` (or `.btn.secondary`) | Alternatives and everyday actions: "Play friendly", "Sim 5", "Download share card" |
| Ghost | `.btn.ghost` | Low emphasis and navigation: "← Back", "Sim to end", "Auto-complete" |
| Danger | `.btn.danger`, `.btn.ghost.danger` | Destructive actions, always behind a `confirm()`: "Abandon", "Release", "Delete all data" |

Sizes: default 40 px; `.sm` 30 px for actions inside rows and cards; `.big` 52 px in the display face for a screen's main call to action; `.block` is full width; `.spin-btn` is the draft's 56 px button. Labels are sentence case, start with a verb and are three words or fewer where possible; "→" only marks moving on to the next step. Disabled buttons fade to 45 % and keep their label. Never two primaries side by side.

### Chips (`.chip`)

Pills of 12 px semibold for short facts.

| Class | Meaning | Examples |
|---|---|---|
| `.chip` | A neutral fact | "28 years", "Chelsea 2019-20", "Bench" |
| `.chip.info` | A state or selection | "In the XI", "Picking", "Marked" |
| `.chip.good` | A good outcome | "fits GK", "✓ Met", a won fixture |
| `.chip.warn` | Attention | "Bidding war", "can cover CB" |
| `.chip.bad` | A problem | "🩹 3w", "Sacked", "✗ Missed" |
| `.chip.gold` | Trophies, records, the captain | "🏆", "📜 League record", "©" |
| `.chip.estimated` | A number that is TouchLine's estimate (classic seasons), dashed border in `--text-2`; only through `EstimatedChip` | "Estimated ratings", "est." |

At most three chips on a row; a chip is never a button unless it is in a chip group like the depth chart. `.chip.good` is not a selection colour: the XI's position chips in the squad list become `.chip.info` in the screen pass.

### Segmented controls (`Segmented`, `.segmented`)

For two to five mutually exclusive options with labels of one or two words. The active option takes the selection pattern (`--primary` border, `--primary-soft` fill). Put the explanation in the option's `hint` (a title) or in an `Info` beside the field label, never in a sentence under the control. On a phone the options wrap; seven leagues is the most a segmented control should carry. Beyond that, use a select or a picture grid.

### Fields: inputs, selects and toggles

```tsx
<label className="field">
  Team name
  <input className="input" … />
</label>

<div className="field">
  <span>Difficulty <Info>Calibrated scales the real clubs so an average draft finishes mid-table.</Info></span>
  <Segmented … />
</div>

<label className="toggle">
  <input type="checkbox" … /> Hard mode <Info>Ratings stay hidden while you draft.</Info>
</label>
```

- `.field` (a `label` or a `div`) stacks the label (13 px, 600, `--text-2`), the control, and at most one `.help` caption.
- Native checkboxes, radios and sliders take `--primary` through `accent-color` on `body`.
- `.input` and `select.input` are full width, 40 px, on `--field-bg` with the 3:1 `--field-border`; focus shows the `--focus` ring.
- `.toggle` is a checkbox row; checked toggles take the selection pattern. The label is one line of six words or fewer. `.toggle.era-classic` is the compact form for a panel head row (2 px by 10 px padding, 13 px text, no minimum height): the Setup Era panel's Classic toggle.
- Two fields side by side only on desktop (`.grid.two`); on a phone everything stacks.

### Info and captions: the rule for explanatory text

The Setup screen showed the problem: a helper sentence under every control. The rule now:

1. **A control gets a label, nothing else, by default.**
2. If it needs explaining, add **one `<Info>`** beside its label: a sentence or two, plain text, no links.
3. If the user needs one fact to choose well (the current value's consequence), use **one `.help` caption** of at most one line on a phone (about 60 characters). Never more than one caption per control, and preferably no more than one per panel.
4. **Never a paragraph under every control.** Paragraphs belong to How it works.
5. A panel's purpose goes in its title; a subtitle in `.panel-head` is at most eight words.
6. `.notice` is for state that changes what the user can do (today's puzzle, a challenge, the window being shut, an error), never for static explanation.
7. Show numbers instead of sentences: "Wage room £1.2M/wk", not "You have £1.2M a week of room under the wage cap".

`Info` (bits.tsx): `<Info label="About seeds">Share a seed and a friend gets the same wheel.</Info>`. A mouse opens it on hover, a tap toggles it, keyboard focus opens it; Escape, a tap elsewhere, scrolling or the mouse leaving closes it. The tip is portalled to `<body>`, positioned with `position: fixed` and clamped to the viewport, so it is never clipped by a scrolling list and never widens the page; it is `role="tooltip"` and linked with `aria-describedby` while open. `label` is the button's accessible name (default "More information"). Inside a `<label>` the button's name joins the control's name, so keep `label` short, or use a `div.field` and give the control its own `aria-label`.

### Panels (`.panel`, `.panel-head`)

```tsx
<div className="panel">
  <div className="panel-head">
    <h3>Squad · 18/23</h3>
    <button className="btn sm">Auto-pick</button>
  </div>
  …
</div>
```

- 16 px padding, 16 px radius, 1 px `--line`, flat. `.panel.tight` (12 px padding) for small side panels.
- The header is the `h3` title on the left and at most one caption or one small button on the right; it wraps on a phone.
- Hierarchy: page `h1`, then optional section `h2`, then panel `h3`, then `h4` or an `.eyebrow` label for a group inside a panel. Do not skip from `h1` to `h4`.
- An emphasised panel (the one waiting on the user, such as the half-time panel or the youth intake) takes a `--primary` border; an event takes `--warn`, and the event card keeps its inline `borderColor: 'var(--warn)'` because smoke.cjs selects on it.

### Cards (`.card`)

Items inside a panel (an offer, a staff role, a job offer): `--panel-2`, 12 px radius, 12 px padding. A chosen card takes a `--primary` border.

### Panel headers with an Info, toolbars, lined lists

- A title that needs explaining wraps it with its `Info` in `.head-title` (`<span className="head-title"><h3>Depth chart</h3><Info>…</Info></span>`), so the `h3` text stays exactly the title (smokes select on it).
- Several small controls in a header go in a `.toolbar` (wraps on a phone).
- `.plist.lined`: rows divided by a hairline instead of a box each (the Draft squad, the Dugout squad, the slot sheet, the bench, the youth intake, search results). Hover is `--bg-3`; the selected row is `--primary-soft` with a 3 px `--primary` bar on the left. Lists of three rows or fewer may keep the boxed `.plist`.
- The same idea elsewhere: role rows, instructions, staff, the draft log and the Inbox are divided by `--line` hairlines rather than cards; unread news has a 3 px `--accent` bar.
- `.stat-strip`: a screen's headline numbers as `Stat`s, four across on a desktop and two by two on a phone.
- `.club-head` and `.club-sub`: a badge, the name as an `h2`, then a line of short facts separated by middle dots (chips sit in the line without dots).
- `.empty-state`: an icon in a 56 px `--primary-soft` circle, one title line with an `Info`, at most one caption.
- `details.panel.fold`: a panel that folds, its `summary` a `.panel-head` with the title and a `ChevronDown` (`.fold-chevron`) that turns when open; the share text on the summaries. The opposition report is the same pattern under `.opposition`.
- `.steps`: a short ordered list of rules as pictures, an icon in a 40 px `--primary-soft` circle, a bold title and one line each; four across on a desktop, two at 900 px, one at 520 px (the head-to-head setup).

### The result banner (`.tier-banner`)

The season summary and the head-to-head result share it, top to bottom: `.banner-meta` (the badge and "Team as Club", then season and mode; two lines on a phone), the tier name (`tierName()`), the record in the status colours, `.banner-stats` (`Stat`s), the result blocks, `.banner-chips` (cup, Continental Cup, sacked, one nation, objectives as `Objectives 1/2`, and the real club's finish as two chips: `Real Chelsea: 10th, 52 pts` and `7 places higher` in `--good` or `--bad`, from `versusReal`), then `.banner-actions`: one primary (Copy result, or Continue in a dynasty), the rest secondary with an icon, New game ghost. On a phone the primary is full width and the others sit two by two without their icons. The head-to-head result puts the two sides in `.h2h-side` cards (the winner's ringed in gold) between the title and the actions.

### Player rows (`PlayerRow`, `.prow`)

The anatomy, left to right: the rating badge (34 px column), then the name line (flag, name, at most two flag chips such as injury or champion), then the meta line (position tags, age, club-season, at most one extra chip), then one action or status on the right (`.btn.sm` or a chip). State bars (condition, morale, sharpness) only in squad contexts. Hover is neutral (`--raised`, `--line-2`); selected takes the selection pattern; `.dim` (42 %) means unusable. Rows are 4 px apart in a `.plist`.

### Tables (`table.tbl`)

Capital 11 px headers in `--text-3`; `.num` for figures; `tr.me` (the user's row) takes `--primary-soft`; `tr.clickable` hovers on `--bg-3`. On a phone a table scrolls sideways inside itself (`display: block; overflow-x: auto`), never the page; keep tables to the columns that matter there. A table that can be wider than its panel on a desktop sits in a `.tbl-wrap` (scrolls inside the panel); a select in a row is `select.input.sm` (control-h-sm, `--fs-sm`, sized to its text); a trailing action cell is `td.end`.

### Tabs (`Tabs`, `.tabs`)

One-word labels; the active tab has a 2 px `--primary` underline, one ink bar (`.tabs-ink`) that slides from tab to tab once `Tabs` has measured them (until then, and without JavaScript, each active tab draws its own inset underline); an alert is the `.dot` in `--warn`. On a phone the tabs are one row that scrolls sideways; never let them wrap into several rows.

### Modals and sheets (`Modal`)

The title is an `h2` in a `.panel-head` with the close button on the right (`aria-label="Close"`; smoke10 clicks it). Content is a `.col`; actions sit at the end, primary last on desktop and full width on a phone. On a phone (720 px and below) every modal is a bottom sheet: full width, anchored to the bottom, 88 % of the screen at most, with a grabber bar, sliding up as it opens; on a desktop it rises 8 px and fades in. The scrim fades in on its own layer (`.modal-back::before`), so the dialog is never see-through while it arrives. Escape and a tap on the scrim close it. One exception: the live match's half-time and 70th-minute decisions (`DecisionSheet`) are a dialog centred in the window on every width, over a lighter scrim (`--scrim` at 55 %) with a `--primary` border, so the scoreboard and the pitch stay in view and the whole pop-up is visible without scrolling on a phone; Escape or a tap on the scrim minimises it to a Decisions pill rather than dismissing the stop.

### Bottom bars (`.bottom-bar`, `.cta-bar`)

On a phone the screen's main action lives in a bar fixed to the bottom (`--panel`, `--border-strong` on top, `--shadow`, padded for the safe area). The primary button comes first and widest; at most three buttons and one line of summary or briefing above them. The page gets `.has-bottom-bar` and the measured `--bottom-bar-h` so nothing hides under the bar.

### Notices and toasts

`.notice` (info), `.notice.warn`, `.notice.danger`: a 12 px-radius box on the soft status fill with a 40 % border. Toasts are a floating `--float-bg` box (12 px radius, `--shadow`) at the bottom centre that slides up as it arrives and, for its last 0.22 s, slides away (`.toast.leaving`) before it clears itself after 3.2 seconds.

### Top bar

The open `Lockup` at 40 px high (32 px on a phone), playing its shine on hover, on the left, the nav on the right: pills of 14 px semibold with a 16 px icon before each label (Home, Draft or Dugout or Summary, Head-to-head, Explore database ("Database" at 1100 px and below; the screen's title is "Explore database", the route stays `explore`), Profile, How it works; each label is a `.nav-label` span), the active one in the selection pattern. Beside it, the same size and style, the top bar toggle (`.topbar-toggle`, `data-testid="topbar-toggle"`, a chevron up, "Hide top bar"); section 13. At 1100 px and below the lockup and the switch share the first row and the nav takes a full-width second row, spread evenly, 13 px labels with their icons; on a phone (720 px and below) that row is icons only, the labels kept for screen readers (visually hidden). Nothing wraps, clips or scrolls at any width.

## 7. Imagery

"More pictures" means our own imagery, never anyone's branding. The owner's call: fun cartoon sketches, not real photos. There are no photos in the game.

- **Allowed**: SVG we draw (the cartoon scenes and the neon flow below, pitches, formation diagrams, the mark), `ClubBadge` colour discs, drawn flags, league marks (a disc in the league colour with its three-letter code, from `LEAGUE_COLOURS` in `leagueSegments.ts`), charts drawn from game data, Lucide icons.
- **Never**: photos or downloaded art, real club crests, kits, league or competition logos, player photos or likenesses, any identifiable face, sponsor boards or stadium names, screenshots of other games.
- **Never anything that could pass as UEFA or Champions League branding.** The palette is inspired by night-football broadcast colours (navy, cyan, magenta), in our own design: no starball, no star shapes, star patterns or ball-of-stars, no "UCL" or "Champions League" text, no UEFA photos or trade dress. The art uses discs, rounded points and curves, never stars (the crown's points are rounded and its gems are discs). League and club names stay as they are.

### The art (`src/ui/components/Art.tsx`)

- **`NeonFlow`**: two flowing cyan-to-magenta curves, each a blurred halo (stroke × 4 under a Gaussian blur), the gradient core and a pale highlight. ## 12. Motion

The owner asked for "some site animation" and "some nice touchup". Motion here is feedback and a sense of place, never decoration that waits on the player: short, once, and on the compositor.

### Tokens

| Token | Value | Use |
|---|---|---|
| `--dur-1` | 150 ms | Hover colours, the press-down (60 ms while held), the outgoing screen |
| `--dur-2` | 220 ms | Toasts leaving, modals and the scrim on a desktop, the old screen in a View Transition |
| `--dur-3` | 320 ms | The screen entrance, the tab ink, a phone sheet sliding up, a toast arriving, the theme icon |
| `--dur-4` | 400 ms | Rises on Home, bars growing, the tier pop |
| `--ease-out` | `cubic-bezier(0.2, 0.7, 0.2, 1)` | Everything that arrives or moves |
| `--ease-in-out` | `cubic-bezier(0.4, 0, 0.2, 1)` | Things that leave, the loading sweep |
| `--ease-spring` | `cubic-bezier(0.2, 0.9, 0.3, 1.25)` | A small overshoot for something worth noticing: the toast, the tier, the theme icon |
| `--stagger` | 60 ms | The step between items entering one after another |

`DURATION` in `src/ui/motion.ts` mirrors the four durations for the JavaScript side (the count-up runs for `DURATION.slow`).

### Rules

1. **150 to 400 ms**, on the tokens. The exceptions are named: the reel and the wheel (their timing is part of the draft, see CLAUDE.md), the live match pitch, the hero art's settle (`hero-in`) and the travelling neon light (`neon-run`, 7 s, the only loop; ambient, nothing waits on them), the lockup's own load and hover animation (section 2), the loading sweep (a 1.4 s loop, only while the packs load) and the celebration (0.9 s, once per result).
2. **Transform and opacity only.** No animated width, height, top or left, so nothing reflows. Bars grow with `scaleX`, the tab ink moves with `translate` and `scaleX`, the toast keeps its centring `translate` in every frame. The one width transition left is a bar's value changing in place (an old rule, on one small element).
3. **Once.** Entrances run when something mounts and never loop. Entrances use `animation-fill-mode: backwards`, so a finished animation never pins a transform over `:hover`.
4. **Never block.** Nothing the player or a smoke script waits for is hidden behind an animation; buttons work while things are still rising. Count-ups mark themselves with `data-counting` while they climb.
5. **Reduced motion turns it all off.** Under `prefers-reduced-motion: reduce` the motion section's last block stops every entrance, transition, hover lift and press; `withViewTransition` and `useCountUp` land at once; the celebration is not drawn; the eligible-slot pulse on the pitch stops. Add every new animated selector to that block.
6. **No dependency.** CSS first; the few helpers in `motion.ts` are plain React. No animation library.
7. **Decoration never touches the game.** No helper reads the game's RNG or writes state; the confetti is a fixed pattern (`confettiPieces`, the golden angle), not random numbers.

**Hiding the top bar.** A round button the size and style of the theme switch sits right beside it (`.topbar-toggle`, `data-testid="topbar-toggle"`, a chevron up, `aria-label` "Hide top bar"). Pressed, the bar slides up out of the way (`topbar-out`, `--dur-2`) and the screen takes the freed height; a pill takes the same top-right corner (`.topbar-restore`, `data-testid="topbar-restore"`, a chevron down and "Show top bar"), and focus moves to it. The pill brings the bar back (`topbar-in`) and focus returns to the toggle. The `\` key does either, except while typing in a field. The choice is per viewer in `localStorage` under `touchline-topbar-hidden` (inside try/catch, through `useViewerFlag`); the hidden bar is `inert` and `aria-hidden`. A shell screen's head keeps 150 px clear on the right while the bar is hidden, so the pill never covers a control. Under reduced motion neither the bar nor the pill animates.

**Naming the side.** Headers show the team name only, with the league flag and season as a chip where it helps; no header says "as <club>". The user's side wears a `TeamBadge` (its short name on `--primary`), never the badge of the club whose place it took. The replaced club appears only in Setup and on the Club tab ("You replace").
