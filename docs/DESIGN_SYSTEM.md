# TouchLine: design system

The look of the game, written down so every screen is built the same way. The owner's brief: a proper logo, blue instead of green, a nicer design and a cleaner layout; on the Season setup screen "less text, more pictures", keeping the detail and the customisation; and the name written **TouchLine**, not in capitals. The goal is a product that looks finished and friendly on a phone and on a laptop.

Where it lives:

| What | Where |
|---|---|
| Tokens (colour, type, space, shape) and every shared component's CSS | `src/ui/styles.css` (tokens at the top, Floodlit then Daylight) |
| The name, the mark's geometry, colours for canvas drawing | `src/ui/brand.ts` (`APP_NAME`, `WORDMARK`, `MARK`, `BRAND_COLOURS`) |
| Logo components | `src/ui/components/Logo.tsx` (`Logo`, `LogoMark`, `Wordmark`) |
| The info button with a tooltip | `Info` in `src/ui/components/bits.tsx` |
| Tier names in sentence case | `tierName()` in `src/ui/format.ts` |
| Favicon and app icons | `public/favicon.svg`, `public/icons/*.png`, regenerated by `node scripts/brand/icons.mjs` |

The rule for everyone building screens: **use the tokens and the components below; no raw colours, no new one-off styles.** If a screen needs something this document does not cover, add it here and to `styles.css` first.

## 1. Principles

1. **Blue, calm, confident.** Deep navy surfaces, one primary blue for actions and selection, a lighter accent blue for highlights. Status colours (good, warn, bad, gold) only ever mean status.
2. **Less text, more picture.** Badges, flags, league marks, pitch diagrams and numbers carry the information. Explanations sit behind an info icon, not in paragraphs under controls.
3. **One primary action per region.** Each panel, bar or modal has at most one `.btn.primary`. Everything else is secondary or ghost.
4. **Mixed case.** Titles and buttons are written as sentences. Capitals are kept for small labels and codes only.
5. **Phone first.** Every screen works at 390 px with no sideways scroll, and the main action is reachable with a thumb.

## 2. Name and logo

**The name is TouchLine**: one word, capital T and capital L, everywhere it is displayed (`APP_NAME` in `brand.ts`). Never "TOUCHLINE", "Touch Line" or "Touchline". Lower-case `touchline` stays only where it is a slug: the npm package, the repository, `localStorage` keys (`touchline-save-v1`), download file names and the service worker cache. Engine strings are unchanged: `shareText` keeps its `'Touchline'` default, and the UI passes `APP_NAME`.

**The mark** is a pitch corner: the touchline running up, the goal line running right, the corner arc between them, and the ball struck from the corner. It is drawn on a 64-unit grid (`MARK` in `brand.ts`):

| Part | Geometry |
|---|---|
| Tile | 64 × 64, corner radius 15, linear gradient `#3b82f6` (top left) to `#1d4ed8` (bottom right) |
| Lines | path `M14 13V50H51`, stroke 6.5, round caps and join |
| Corner arc | path `M14 38A12 12 0 0 1 26 50`, stroke 5 |
| Ball | circle at (36.5, 27.5), r 9.5 |
| Pentagon | regular, r 4, point up, knocked out of the ball; only drawn from 40 px up |

Components (`Logo.tsx`):

```tsx
<Logo size={30} />                 // the lockup: mark + wordmark; the top bar
<Logo size="lg" />                 // loading screen
<LogoMark size={72} />             // the mark alone; the home hero
<LogoMark size="md" variant="mono" title="TouchLine" />
<Wordmark />                       // "Touch" + "Line" in the accent blue, real text
```

Sizes: `sm` 24, `md` 32, `lg` 48, `xl` 96, or any number of pixels. The wordmark in a lockup is 0.82 × the mark's size, with a gap of 0.3 × the mark.

Variants: `colour` (the blue tile, white glyph; the default and the app icon), `mono` (the glyph alone in `currentColor`, no tile: on a photo, a coloured fill or print), `inverse` (a white tile with a blue glyph: only on a blue or navy background).

Rules:

- Clear space around the mark: at least a quarter of its size. Smallest size: 16 px (the favicon).
- Never recolour the tile, stretch it, outline it, put the colour tile on the primary blue (use `inverse`), or set the wordmark in capitals or with a space.
- The mark is decorative next to the wordmark (`aria-hidden`); give `LogoMark` a `title` when it stands alone.
- After any change to `MARK`, run `node scripts/brand/icons.mjs` (Node 22.18+ and Playwright; set `PLAYWRIGHT_MODULE` to Playwright's entry file if it is not installed in the project). It writes `public/favicon.svg` (no pentagon), `icons/icon-192.png` and `icon-512.png` (rounded tile, transparent corners), `icons/icon-maskable-512.png` (full bleed, glyph at 78 % inside the safe circle) and `icons/apple-touch-icon.png` (full bleed, opaque).
- The share card (`shareCard.ts`) draws the same mark with `Path2D` from `MARK` and the two-tone wordmark, in the Floodlit colours of `BRAND_COLOURS`, whatever the viewer's theme.

## 4. Typography

Two faces: **Barlow Condensed** (`--font-display`, 600 and 700) for titles, big numbers and the main call to action; **Inter** (`--font-body`, 400, 600, 700) for everything else; `--mono` for seeds, minutes and codes.

| Token | Size | Phone | Face | Use |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| `--fs-hero` | 64 px | 46 px | display 700 | The home wordmark only |
| `--fs-h1` | 36 px | 30 px | display 700 | Page title, one per screen (`h1`) |
| `--fs-h2` | 26 px | 22 px | display 700 | Section and modal titles, club names (`h2`) |
| `--fs-h3` | 19 px | 19 px | display 700 | Panel titles (`h3`) |
| (h4) | 16 px | 16 px | display 700 | A group inside a panel |
| `--fs-base` | 15 px | 15 px | body 400 | Body text, buttons (600) |
| `--fs-sm` | 13 px | 13 px | body | Captions (`.help`), field labels (600), secondary lines, small buttons |
| `--fs-meta` | 12 px | 12 px | body 600 | Chips, list meta lines |
| `--fs-xs` | 11 px | 11 px | body 600 | Capital labels: `.eyebrow`, `.stat .label`, table headers, position codes |

Rules:

- **Mixed case everywhere.** `h1` to `h4`, panel titles, buttons (the big ones too), the scoreboard and the tier banner are written in sentence case with no `text-transform`. Capitals are only for `.eyebrow`, `.stat .label`, table headers, position and club codes, chips that hold a code, and the scoreboard clock ("HALF TIME").
- **Tier names** come from the engine in capitals. Display them with `tierName(tier)` ("Title race", "38-0: The perfect season"). The share card already does; the tier banner, the leaderboard and head-to-head move to it in the screen pass.
- **Numbers that matter are big and condensed**: scores, points, prices, ratings, stat values (`.stat .value` is 28 px display). Tables use `.num` for tabular figures.
- Line height 1.45 for body text, 1.1 for titles, 1 for big numbers.
- On phones, inputs are 16 px so iOS does not zoom (already global).

## 6. Components

All of these are global classes or components already in the code; screens compose them.

### Buttons (`.btn`)

| Variant | Class | When |
|---|---|---|
| Primary | `.btn.primary` | The one forward action of a region: "Spin the wheel", "Kick off the season", "Continue →" |
| Secondary | `.btn` (or `.btn.secondary`) | Alternatives and everyday actions: "Play friendly", "Sim 5", "Download share card" |
| Ghost | `.btn.ghost` | Low emphasis and navigation: "← Back", "Sim to end", "Auto-complete" |
| Danger | `.btn.danger`, `.btn.ghost.danger` | Destructive actions, always behind a `confirm()`: "Abandon", "Release", "Delete all data" |

Sizes: default 40 px; `.sm` 30 px for actions inside rows and cards; `.big` 52 px in the display face for a screen's main call to action; `.block` is full width; `.spin-btn` is the draft's 56 px button. Labels are sentence case, start with a verb and are three words or fewer where possible; "→" only marks moving on to the next step. Disabled buttons fade to 45 % and keep their label. Never two primaries side by side.

### Chips (`.chip`)

Pills of 12 px semibold for short facts.

| Class | Meaning | Examples |
|---|---|---|
| `.chip` | A neutral fact | "28 years", "Chelsea 2019-20", "Bench" |
| `.chip.info` | A state or selection | "In the XI", "Picking", "Marked" |
| `.chip.good` | A good outcome | "fits GK", "✓ Met", a won fixture |
| `.chip.warn` | Attention | "Bidding war", "can cover CB" |
| `.chip.bad` | A problem | "🩹 3w", "Sacked", "✗ Missed" |
| `.chip.gold` | Trophies, records, the captain | "🏆", "📜 League record", "©" |

At most three chips on a row; a chip is never a button unless it is in a chip group like the depth chart. `.chip.good` is not a selection colour: the XI's position chips in the squad list become `.chip.info` in the screen pass.

### Segmented controls (`Segmented`, `.segmented`)

For two to five mutually exclusive options with labels of one or two words. The active option takes the selection pattern (`--primary` border, `--primary-soft` fill). Put the explanation in the option's `hint` (a title) or in an `Info` beside the field label, never in a sentence under the control. On a phone the options wrap; seven leagues is the most a segmented control should carry. Beyond that, use a select or a picture grid.

### Fields: inputs, selects and toggles

```tsx
<label className="field">
  Team name
  <input className="input" … />
</label>

<div className="field">
  <span>Difficulty <Info>Calibrated scales the real clubs so an average draft finishes mid-table.</Info></span>
  <Segmented … />
</div>

<label className="toggle">
  <input type="checkbox" … /> Hard mode <Info>Ratings stay hidden while you draft.</Info>
</label>
```

- `.field` (a `label` or a `div`) stacks the label (13 px, 600, `--text-2`), the control, and at most one `.help` caption.
- Native checkboxes, radios and sliders take `--primary` through `accent-color` on `body`.
- `.input` and `select.input` are full width, 40 px, on `--field-bg` with the 3:1 `--field-border`; focus shows the `--focus` ring.
- `.toggle` is a checkbox row; checked toggles take the selection pattern. The label is one line of six words or fewer.
- Two fields side by side only on desktop (`.grid.two`); on a phone everything stacks.

### Info and captions: the rule for explanatory text

The Setup screen showed the problem: a helper sentence under every control. The rule now:

1. **A control gets a label, nothing else, by default.**
2. If it needs explaining, add **one `<Info>`** beside its label: a sentence or two, plain text, no links.
3. If the user needs one fact to choose well (the current value's consequence), use **one `.help` caption** of at most one line on a phone (about 60 characters). Never more than one caption per control, and preferably no more than one per panel.
4. **Never a paragraph under every control.** Paragraphs belong to How it works.
5. A panel's purpose goes in its title; a subtitle in `.panel-head` is at most eight words.
6. `.notice` is for state that changes what the user can do (today's puzzle, a challenge, the window being shut, an error), never for static explanation.
7. Show numbers instead of sentences: "Wage room £1.2M/wk", not "You have £1.2M a week of room under the wage cap".

`Info` (bits.tsx): `<Info label="About seeds">Share a seed and a friend gets the same wheel.</Info>`. A mouse opens it on hover, a tap toggles it, keyboard focus opens it; Escape, a tap elsewhere, scrolling or the mouse leaving closes it. The tip is portalled to `<body>`, positioned with `position: fixed` and clamped to the viewport, so it is never clipped by a scrolling list and never widens the page; it is `role="tooltip"` and linked with `aria-describedby` while open. `label` is the button's accessible name (default "More information"). Inside a `<label>` the button's name joins the control's name, so keep `label` short, or use a `div.field` and give the control its own `aria-label`.

### Panels (`.panel`, `.panel-head`)

```tsx
<div className="panel">
  <div className="panel-head">
    <h3>Squad · 18/23</h3>
    <button className="btn sm">Auto-pick</button>
  </div>
  …
</div>
```

- 16 px padding, 16 px radius, 1 px `--line`, flat. `.panel.tight` (12 px padding) for small side panels.
- The header is the `h3` title on the left and at most one caption or one small button on the right; it wraps on a phone.
- Hierarchy: page `h1`, then optional section `h2`, then panel `h3`, then `h4` or an `.eyebrow` label for a group inside a panel. Do not skip from `h1` to `h4`.
- An emphasised panel (the one waiting on the user, such as the half-time panel or the youth intake) takes a `--primary` border; an event takes `--warn`, and the event card keeps its inline `borderColor: 'var(--warn)'` because smoke.cjs selects on it.

### Cards (`.card`)

Items inside a panel (an offer, a staff role, a job offer): `--panel-2`, 12 px radius, 12 px padding. A chosen card takes a `--primary` border.

### Player rows (`PlayerRow`, `.prow`)

The anatomy, left to right: the rating badge (34 px column), then the name line (flag, name, at most two flag chips such as injury or champion), then the meta line (position tags, age, club-season, at most one extra chip), then one action or status on the right (`.btn.sm` or a chip). State bars (condition, morale, sharpness) only in squad contexts. Hover is neutral (`--raised`, `--line-2`); selected takes the selection pattern; `.dim` (42 %) means unusable. Rows are 4 px apart in a `.plist`.

### Tables (`table.tbl`)

Capital 11 px headers in `--text-3`; `.num` for figures; `tr.me` (the user's row) takes `--primary-soft`; `tr.clickable` hovers on `--bg-3`. On a phone a table scrolls sideways inside itself (`display: block; overflow-x: auto`), never the page; keep tables to the columns that matter there.

### Tabs (`Tabs`, `.tabs`)

One-word labels; the active tab has a 2 px `--primary` underline; an alert is the `.dot` in `--warn`. On a phone the tabs are one row that scrolls sideways; never let them wrap into several rows.

### Modals and sheets (`Modal`)

The title is an `h2` in a `.panel-head` with the close button on the right (`aria-label="Close"`; smoke10 clicks it). Content is a `.col`; actions sit at the end, primary last on desktop and full width on a phone. On a phone (720 px and below) every modal is a bottom sheet: full width, anchored to the bottom, 88 % of the screen at most, with a grabber bar. Escape and a tap on the scrim close it.

### Bottom bars (`.bottom-bar`, `.cta-bar`)

On a phone the screen's main action lives in a bar fixed to the bottom (`--panel`, `--border-strong` on top, `--shadow`, padded for the safe area). The primary button comes first and widest; at most three buttons and one line of summary or briefing above them. The page gets `.has-bottom-bar` and the measured `--bottom-bar-h` so nothing hides under the bar.

### Notices and toasts

`.notice` (info), `.notice.warn`, `.notice.danger`: a 12 px-radius box on the soft status fill with a 40 % border. Toasts are a floating `--float-bg` box (12 px radius, `--shadow`) at the bottom centre that clears itself after three seconds.

### Top bar

The `Logo` lockup at 30 px (26 px on a phone) on the left, the nav on the right: pills of 14 px semibold, the active one in the selection pattern. On a phone the nav is one row that scrolls sideways, with a fade at the right edge.

### Pitch, ratings, positions, badges, flags

- `Pitch`: the grass, `--pitch-line` markings, navy discs with the rating; the eligible slot pulses in `--pitch-highlight`; the selected slot rings in `--gold`.
- `Rating`: the band classes `r90` to `r70` over `--rating-base`; hidden ratings show "?" on `--bg-3`.
- `Pos`: the position code coloured by group (`--pos-*`).
- `ClubBadge`: a disc in the club's approximate primary colour with its code and a ring in its secondary colour. This is the only way a club is pictured.
- `Flag`: the nation's emoji flag.

### Selection, hover, focus, disabled

- **Selected**: `--primary` border, `--primary-soft` fill, `--text`. Segments, nav, formations, player rows, toggles, chosen cards.
- **Hover**: neutral (`--raised` or `--bg-3`, `--line-2`), except buttons and mode cards, which show a `--primary` border.
- **Focus**: the global 2 px `--focus` ring on `:focus-visible`. Never remove it.
- **Disabled**: 45 % opacity, `not-allowed` cursor, the same label.

- **Allowed**: SVG we draw (pitches, formation diagrams, icons, the mark), `ClubBadge` colour discs, emoji flags, league marks (a disc in the league colour with its three-letter code, from `LEAGUE_COLOURS` in `leagueSegments.ts`), charts drawn from game data.
- **Never**: real club crests, kits, league or competition logos, player photos or likenesses, stock photos, screenshots of other games.
- **Icons**: draw them inline on a 24-unit grid, 2-unit strokes, round caps and joins, `currentColor`. Emoji are fine as status glyphs inside text (⚽ 🟨 🟥 🩹 🏆) but not as the main picture of a card or button; the mode cards' emoji give way to drawn icons in the screen pass.
