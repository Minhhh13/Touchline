# TouchLine: game design

This document describes the game as implemented in `src/engine` and `src/game`. Every number quoted here is taken from the code at the time of writing; where a system is planned but not built it is listed under Roadmap at the end and nowhere else.

## 1. Name

**TouchLine** is the product name, written as one word with a capital L and never in capitals. It is the manager's domain (the one place a manager is allowed to stand), it is short and it is brandable: it is the `<title>`, the manifest name, the wordmark in the top bar and the first line of the share text. The repository, the npm package and the save keys keep the lower-case slug `touchline`, and the default team name is still "Touchline XI". The logo, the palette and the type are in [DESIGN_SYSTEM.md](DESIGN_SYSTEM.md).

Tagline: **Spin the wheel. Draft the XI. Manage the season.**

Alternatives considered and set aside:

| Name | Why not |
| --- | --- |
| Gaffer | Good voice, but it names the person rather than the game, and it is widely used. |
| Hotseat | Captures job security, but it reads as a quiz format and ignores the draft. |
| Dugout Dynasty | Describes only one of four modes and is long. |
| Centurion | The 100-point season is one achievement among fifteen, not the whole promise. |
| Fortune XI | Leans on the luck of the wheel and undersells the management half. |

## 2. Pillars

1. **The wheel is the only way in.** You never pick from a list. Every player arrives through a spin that lands on a real club in a real season, so every squad is a story of where the wheel took you.
2. **Then you manage for real.** Roles, instructions, condition, morale, injuries, suspensions, a cup, a transfer window, a board with a target. Each system has a bounded, legible effect that the UI explains.
3. **Nostalgia with a spine of data.** Players are tied to a season and rated for how they played that year, from 2008-09 to the current 2026-27 campaign. Your drafted side takes one real club's place and plays the other nineteen clubs' real squads.
4. **Deterministic and shareable.** Every spin and every match flows from one seed, so a Daily Challenge is the same wheel for everyone and a seed can be sent to a friend. Every season ends in a share text.
5. **Legible randomness.** Luck exists (Poisson goals, variance scaled by consistency) but it is explained in match reports and tactical notes, never hidden.

## 3. Modes

| Mode | Draft | Season | Persistence |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Quick Draft | 11 picks, 1 re-spin (normal difficulty; easy 3, hard 0), each club-season usable once; an optional bench of up to 5 more picks (Setup, default 5) | Squad complete with the pundits' prediction, then the season played back game by game (simulated up front, revealed at 1.5 s a game at 1x) with a live table and position chart; the goal is 38-0; a January window after matchweek 19 (toggle, default on); fixed XI apart from one January deal and, with a bench, three changes and injury cover (below), default roles, no injuries carried without a bench, no live state; an optional Continental Cup run after a top-four finish | Result to local leaderboard and share text |
| Daily Challenge | As Quick Draft, seed `daily-YYYY-MM-DD` (UTC), every rule pinned by `dailySettings` (its original 2 re-spins, ratings shown, Squad First, season ratings, twists on, all-time, no club or nation filter, no modes) | As Quick Draft; `DAILY_SEASON` pins the January window on and the Continental Cup run off | Streak, daily result, achievement at seven days running |

Setup options (`src/ui/screens/Setup.tsx`), in 38-0's order as numbered steps (a circled number and a short title each) in two boxes, at most 1480px wide. Left box: **1 Pick your formation**, **2 Name your team**, **3 Choose your league**; right box: **4 Set the rules**, **5 Spin**. From 1000px wide the boxes sit side by side, and on a desktop window at least 600px tall the page fits the screen with no page scroll (checked at 1280x720, 1440x900, 2000x920 and 2560x1440 for every mode): the page fills the height under the top bar, step 1's pitch takes the height left in the left box, and with More options open the rules scroll inside the right box while Spin stays at its foot. Below 1000px the boxes stack, so the steps run 1 to 5 down the page, and at 720px or narrower Spin is the fixed bottom bar. On a desktop the choice cards show their titles only (the line is the tooltip and stays for screen readers). The Daily and a challenge link number their steps 1 formation, 2 name, 3 the day's or the link's rules, 4 Spin.

1. **Title and subtitle**: the mode's name and one line ("Spin real club-seasons, draft an XI, then watch its season play out." for Quick Draft).
2. **Formation** (step 1): the eight shapes (section 4) as tiles, two to a row beside the chosen shape on a desktop (picture beside the name), below it on a phone; the chosen one is drawn on a read-only `Pitch` with an empty dot per slot labelled by position (`.pitch-mini`), with its one-line description (`Formation.blurb`). A name with a word after the numbers puts the word on its own smaller line on a tile ("4-4-2" over "Diamond"), and the numbers never break at a hyphen.
   **Team name** (step 2): the `TeamNameField` (28 characters, from which a 2 to 3 letter code is derived) with **Clear** and the **Randomise** dice (below). **League and season** (step 3): the league flags, and on the step's title line a compact season select (`data-testid="season-select"`, newest first, defaulting as before; the classic seasons 1992-93 to 2007-08 are listed, tagged est., only while Classic is on), the season the game simulates; the league season is no longer in the fold; for Season and Dynasty the line "You replace: <club>" with its badge and a **Change** link that opens More options at the club select.
3. **Difficulty**: three cards, Easy "3 re-spins", Normal "1 re-spin", Hard "No re-spins, ratings hidden" (the counts read from `DIFFICULTY_RESPINS`). Quick Draft opens on Normal, Season and Dynasty on Easy.
4. **Show ratings**: two cards, On and Off ("Blind: go on names and positions"). On Hard both are disabled, Off is shown chosen and one line says "Hard always hides ratings."; back on Easy or Normal the card's own choice returns.
5. **Draft mode**: Squad First ("Spin a club, draft anyone, then place him") and Position First ("Choose a slot, then spin for a club to fill it"), section 5.
6. **Player ratings** (Quick Draft only): Season ("Rated as he was in that very season") and Prime ("Every player at his career best"). Season and Dynasty always use season ratings, since their players age and develop from the real season.
7. **Era**: preset chips All-time, 2000s+, 2010s+, Modern (2016+) (`ERA_PRESETS`), a two-handle slider over the seasons the wheel leagues hold (`wheelSeasons`, 2008-09 to 2026-27 for England: 19 stops), a chip with "N of M seasons" (`eraCount`) and one caption line ("The wheel lands on 2016-17 to 2026-27."). A chip puts the handles where `eraHandles` says (All-time and 2000s+ at both ends with today's data, 2010s+ at 2010-11, Modern at 2016-17, the right handle on the newest season); moving a handle (drag or arrow keys; either handle stops at the other) switches to a Custom chip. The handles step through the list of seasons, not through the years (`seasonStop` snaps a year to the nearest season held, the earlier on a tie), so a gap in older data is never a stop and the slider can never select a range with no seasons. The formation preview is a picture, not a control: it is `inert`, so its slot discs are out of the tab order.
8. **Advanced** (Quick Draft; **More options** for Season and Dynasty): folded by default, remembered per browser (below). Toggles first: European Nights (the wheel and the market draw from every league; shown when two or more leagues exist), twists, and for Quick Draft the January window toggle ("January window: one deal at the halfway mark", on by default, `GameSettings.january`); for Season and Dynasty the Continental Cup (a Quick Draft offers its cups after the league instead, so Setup no longer shows it the toggle). Then the team name (28 characters, from which a 2 to 3 letter code is derived; Season and Dynasty keep it above the fold in a "Your squad" panel with the squad-size slider 16 to 22 and the job-security toggle), the seed (shareable, `XXXX-XXXX`), the league played in (for Quick Draft, also the Super League, below), the league season (2008-09 to 2026-27, the in-progress one flagged), the club whose place you take (you inherit their colours, rivals and fixture slot; their real players sit the season out), the opposition (**Calibrated**, the default, or **Realistic**, section 13), one-club mode and one-nation mode (section 5; choosing one clears the other).
9. **Spin** (step 5): the summary line (for a Quick Draft `quickSetupLine`: the team, the shape and the rules or modes only, "Touchline XI · 4-3-3 · Normal · bench 5 · Crazy", never the league season or the seed, the full `summariseSetup` line as its tooltip; Season, Dynasty, the Daily and a challenge keep `summariseSetup`) and the button, **Start Draft** (Season and Dynasty: "Start drafting your squad"; a challenge: "Take on Minh"), both in the fixed phone bar.

The cards, the slider and the toggles become rules through `setupRules(base, choices)` (`src/game/logic/setup.ts`), a pure function: the base is `QUICK_RULES`, or `SEASON_RULES` with the squad size; `withDifficulty` sets the re-spins and keeps `hardMode` in step; `showRatings` is the card's choice except on hard, where it is false; `draftMode` and `ratings` are copied; the two handles are ordered and clamped to the wheel's seasons, and a handle at either end writes no bound, so a full slider is the All-time wheel and a range running to the newest season leaves `toSeason` out. Every preset therefore gives the same bounds as `eraRange`, except that 2000s+ (which starts before the data) writes none at all instead of `fromSeason: 2008`, the same wheel. An untouched Quick Draft setup equals `QUICK_RULES` with the league; `DAILY_RULES` is the same but for its 2 re-spins (tested). The Daily and a challenge link show no cards: the team name, the day's or the link's notice, the formation preview of the fixed shape and the fixed rules as chips from `describeRules` (`2 re-spins`, `Ratings on`, `Squad First`, `Season ratings`, `All-time`, `Twists on` for the Daily: a re-spin budget that is not its difficulty's own is stated without the difficulty's name).

Note on Daily: `dailySettings(u, seed)` (`src/game/logic/daily.ts`) fixes everything but the team name: the Premier League wheel and league, a complete Premier League season and host club rotated by the date (`n = YYYYMMDD`, season index `n mod seasons`, host index `7n mod clubs`), the formation (`3n mod 5` over 4-3-3, 4-4-2, 4-2-3-1, 3-5-2, 4-4-2 Diamond), Calibrated difficulty and `DAILY_RULES`, which writes out every draft rule as a literal, spreading nothing from `QUICK_RULES` or `DIFFICULTY_RESPINS` (11 picks, the Daily's original 2 re-spins with the difficulty labelled normal, ratings shown, Squad First, season ratings, twists on, each club-season once, `accomplished` fit, no era, club or nation filter), so a later change to the quick defaults cannot make two players' Dailies differ; a test fails when `QUICK_RULES` gains a key `DAILY_RULES` does not pin. The 38-0 branch had moved the Daily to Normal's 1 re-spin; the owner chose to keep 2, so `DAILY_RULES` is back to the rules the Daily had before the 38-0 options (`respins: 2`, twists on, season ratings, Squad First, no modes), and `dailyPin.test.ts` plays four dates (spin, both re-spins, the assistant's picks, the season) under `DAILY_RULES` and under the literal pre-38-0 rules and asserts the same spins, XI and season, plus the pre-38-0 date mapping for six dates.

### Two-tap start

The setup screen (`src/ui/screens/Setup.tsx`) starts a game in two taps (the mode card, then Start Draft): every card opens on the mode's defaults. Above the fold sit the formation, the team name, the league, the 38-0 cards and the era, plus the squad-size slider and "You replace" for Season and Dynasty. Everything else (job security, European Nights, twists, the January window, the seed, the league season, the host club for Season and Dynasty, the opposition, one-club and one-nation modes, the Continental Cup) sits under the **Advanced** fold (named **More options** for Season and Dynasty), collapsed by default. Its state is remembered per browser in `localStorage['touchline-setup-advanced']` (`'1'` open, `'0'` closed, read and written inside try/catch so a private window simply starts closed). The Daily and a challenge link show no fold, since nothing in it is theirs to change.

Directly above the Start button a summary line, built by `summariseSetup` (`src/game/logic/setup.ts`) from the exact settings Start would send, states every choice: `Touchline XI, 4-3-3, Premier League 2025-26, calibrated, seed K7Q2-MX9A` (the club replaced is never named in it, nor in any header: the Draft, Kick-off, the season playback, the Dugout, the Summary's banner and share card, and the share text's second line `Touchline XI, 2025-26`), followed only by what is off the defaults (a European Nights wheel, an era, one club or nation, the 38-0 cards that differ from the mode's base rules through `rulesTags`: `easy mode`, `normal mode` (a season, whose default is easy) or `hard mode`, `blind`, `Position First`, `prime ratings`; then `twists` or `no twists` when they differ from the mode's default of on for a quick draft and off for a season, the squad size, no sack, the Continental Cup; for a quick draft `no January window` when it is off and `Continental Cup run` when it is on). A `change` link opens the fold and scrolls to it. On screens of 720px or narrower the line and the button sit in a fixed bottom bar (`.bottom-bar`, padded for the safe-area inset); the page's bottom padding is the bar's measured height plus 20px (150px until it is measured), so a long summary never covers the last panel and Start is reachable without scrolling past the formation grid.

**The look of every setup (Quick Draft, the Daily, Season, Dynasty, Head-to-head).** Few words, pictures and chips, on the redesign tokens (`SetupBits.tsx`):

- Formations are main's `FormationPicker` tiles (one tile style everywhere; the position-coloured dots of `formationThumb`), beside the chosen shape's pitch preview in step 1. The Daily, a challenge link and Crazy show only the pitch preview of the one shape they play, titled "Your formation", Crazy with a line saying the seed draws it.
- Leagues are a segmented row of drawn flags (`LeagueFlag`, never emoji) and the real league name, in step 3. The options are icon cards (`ChoiceCards` with `icon`; on a desktop the icon sits beside the title and the line is the tooltip). Each section has at most one short help line; details sit in Info tips (`SectionHead`, `InfoTip`), the Modes tip listing every mode's line. On a phone the mode cards show only the icon and name, four to a row.
- The Modes row and the Power-ups switch (a card in the Modes style) open step 4; on a desktop they are one line of eight small cards, icon over name (two rows of four below 1200px), the label kept for screen readers and its Info tip beside the row.
- Step 5 shows the one-line `summariseSetup` line above Start. `setupChips` (`src/game/logic/setup.ts`) still builds the same choices as short chips (the shape, the league and season, never the club replaced, the opposition and the seed, then what is off the defaults) for any screen that wants them; the setup itself shows the line.
- Every team name field (and each head-to-head manager's name) is a `TeamNameField` with **Clear** (empties the field and focuses it) and **Randomise** 🎲, an invented name from `FUN_TEAM_NAMES` (`src/game/logic/teamNames.ts`, e.g. "Back Post FC", "Corner Flag United"; a test checks none is a club in any pack). The dice uses the browser's own randomness and never touches the seed; in Head-to-head it never lands on the other manager's name. An empty name starts as Touchline XI (Manager 1 or 2 in Head-to-head).

The default host club is `defaultHostClub`: the club whose position in `u.tables[leagueId][season]` is nearest `DEFAULT_HOST_POSITION` (10), ties to the higher place, among the clubs with a squad that season; where the table is missing it is the alphabetical middle of the list, as before. The help text says why (`A mid-table club (10th in 2025-26), so the board's target is reachable`) until the player picks another club, and the rivals count is the league size minus one rather than a fixed 19, so an 18-club league reads as 17 rivals.

The Daily's fixed settings are `dailySettings(u, seed)` (in `src/game/logic/daily.ts`, re-exported from `src/game/logic/setup.ts`): the digits of `daily-YYYY-MM-DD` form `n`; the season is the complete English seasons in ascending order at `n mod count`, the host club is that season's club ids sorted at `(7n) mod count`, and the formation is `['4-3-3', '4-4-2', '4-2-3-1', '3-5-2', '4-4-2-diamond']` at `(3n) mod 5`; the league is England, the wheel England only, the difficulty calibrated, and the draft rules are `DAILY_RULES`. This is the mapping the setup screen always used, now pinned by a test (`daily-2026-10-25` is a 4-3-3 as Swansea City in 2017-18), so a deploy never changes everyone's puzzle. `dailyGameSettings` builds the full `GameSettings` from it, which is what the Daily setup's **Start Draft** sends to `startGame`. On Home, the Daily card's body and its **Play today's puzzle** button both open that setup (the team name, today's formation shown as given, the rules as chips, the seed, Start Draft); neither starts the draft by itself. If today's Daily is already under way the button reads **Continue today's puzzle** and reopens it.

### Coach marks and the Key

Seven coach marks (`TIP_IDS` in `src/game/logic/tips.ts`; seven is the ceiling) explain a screen or mechanic the first time it is met. Each is a `Tip` (`src/ui/components/bits.tsx`): a `.tip` callout inline in the page flow, with an accent left border, one or two sentences and a **Got it** button. It is never an overlay and never disables a button, so a player who ignores it loses nothing. Got it calls `store.dismissTip(id)`, which records the ISO date in `profile.seenTips[id]` (an optional save field persisted with the profile; an older save has none, so every tip is unseen, and a second dismissal keeps the first date). The seven, in the order a new manager meets them:

| id | where | text |
| --- | --- | --- |
| `draft-first-reveal` | above the landed squad, while the XI is being filled | Green fits means his natural position; amber can cover means he plays there at a penalty, which is the number you will see on the pitch. Re-spins are a budget for the whole draft. |
| `draft-bench` | under the needs panel once the XI is full (and the wheel has not run dry) | Bench picks can be anyone un-drafted; cover every position, injuries will come. |
| `dugout-first` | top of the Squad tab | The three bars are condition, morale and sharpness. The disc number is his rating in that slot; acc. is a small out-of-position cost, awk. a big one. |
| `tactics-first` | top of the Tactics tab | A role moves a player's quality between units; rating in role is what the engine uses, and the team rating updates as you change it. |
| `halftime-first` | inside the half-time panel (not at the 70th minute) | A talk, four instructions and up to three substitutions; No changes plays on. |
| `report-first` | above the match report's tactical notes | xG is the chances each side created; the notes are the exact modifiers your instructions produced. |
| `market-first` | above the window's listings | Bids near the asking price are accepted, bids well under it annoy the club and raise the price; the asking price shows what it was on day one. |

The copy stays qualitative so it cannot drift when the engine is tuned. **Show tips again** in the Manager panel of the Profile screen (`store.resetTips`, which empties `seenTips`) brings all seven back, and the panel says how many of the seven are dismissed.

The Squad panel's header carries a **Key** chip. On, it prints `Cond`, `Mor` and `Shp` under every player's three state bars (`PlayerRow`'s `showLabels`) and a one-line legend under the pitch (`PitchLegend`): the disc is his rating in that slot; a white ring is natural, amber `acc.` is accomplished (position factor 0.86 or more, a small cost), red `awk.` is awkward or worse (below 0.86, a big cost), and a red ring is also injured or suspended. The pitch now draws its rings from `describeFit` in `src/engine/positions.ts`, the same bands as before, so the legend and the discs cannot disagree. The fit colour is the lowest-priority ring (`fitRingColour` in `src/ui/format.ts`): an injured or suspended player shows the red ring wherever he plays, and the selected (gold) and eligible (green) highlights also win over it, while the `acc.` or `awk.` label under the disc still names the fit. The Key is a per-viewer convenience, kept in `localStorage['touchline-squad-key']` (`'1'` on) inside try/catch; blocked storage reads as off and the chip still works for the visit.

| 4-4-2 | GK, LB, 2 CB, RB, LW (as LM), 2 CM, RW (as RM), 2 ST |
| 4-2-3-1 | GK, LB, 2 CB, RB, 2 DM, LW, AM, RW, ST |
| 3-5-2 | GK, 3 CB, LB and RB (as wing-backs), DM, 2 CM, 2 ST |
| 3-4-3 | GK, 3 CB, LB, RB, 2 CM, LW, ST, RW |
| 4-1-4-1 | GK, LB, 2 CB, RB, DM, LW, 2 CM, RW, ST |
| 4-4-2 Diamond | GK, LB, 2 CB, RB, DM, 2 CM, AM, 2 ST |

Every formation chooser (Quick Draft, Season and Dynasty setup, each manager in Head-to-head, the Dugout's Tactics tab) is the same `FormationPicker` (`src/ui/components/FormationPicker.tsx`): a tile per shape with a small drawn pitch, attack pointing up, and the eleven slots as dots placed by `formationThumb` (`src/game/logic/formationThumb.ts`), a linear scaling of the slot coordinates above into a 60 by 88 viewBox with a 6-unit inset. Dots are coloured by the slot's position group (goalkeeper gold, defenders blue, midfielders white, forwards orange), so a 4-4-2's wide men show as wingers because that is who the draft fills them with. The chosen tile has an accent border and a brighter pitch and reports `aria-pressed`; the picture itself is hidden from screen readers. Tests check that no two dots touch in any shape at radius 3.4, so every shape reads at phone width, where the tiles sit four to a row at 390 px (three on a 320 px phone) and the Tactics tab's tiles with blurbs sit two to a row. No images, no RNG.

Wide midfield slots are typed as LW/RW and wing-back slots as LB/RB, so position eligibility (section 6) decides who can fill them. Changing shape mid-season keeps roles whose position still matches, resets the rest to the default role and knocks 12 points off every player's familiarity.

## 5. The wheel and the draft

Rules (`src/engine/draft.ts`):

- **No dead spins.** The wheel pool is every club-season (within the era and club filters) that still contains at least one usable player. While the XI is being filled, usable means a player who can cover an open slot at the minimum fit (`accomplished`, section 6). Once the XI is full, any un-drafted human is usable for the bench.
- **A human is drafted once.** `takenPlayers` holds player ids (not player-season ids), so the same person cannot be drafted from a different club or season.
- **Quick drafts never repeat a club-season** (`uniqueClubSeasons: true`); season drafts may.
- **Spin weighting.** A season is picked uniformly from those still in the pool, so no era dominates because it has more rows. Within that season a club-season is weighted by `quality × usefulness`, where `quality = 1 + 0.06 × (21 − finishing position)` (1.6 if the season is in progress) and `usefulness = 1 + min(10, usable players) / 10`. A title winner is roughly twice as likely as a relegated side.
- **Re-spins** are a draft-wide budget set by the difficulty (quick: easy 3, normal 1, hard 0; season: 3; 1 in a dynasty summer; 0 in head-to-head). A re-spin logs the skipped club-season in the draft history.
- **Picks** go into an open slot the player can cover, or onto the bench once the XI is full. The UI suggests the first natural slot, then the first eligible one.
- **Auto-complete** lets the assistant finish: on each spin it takes the best `overall × (1 or 0.92 if not a natural fit)` into the scarcest open slot, or the highest-rated player for the bench.
- In Dynasty, agent events (section 10) and the summer grant **bonus spins**, shown as a gold chip and redeemed at the summer draft.

### The 38-0 options: difficulty, ratings, draft mode, prime and era

Four optional `DraftRules` fields, each read through a helper so a save or a link written before them keeps its meaning, plus era presets over the existing `fromSeason` / `toSeason`.

- **Difficulty** (`difficulty: 'easy' | 'normal' | 'hard'`, `difficultyOf`): `DIFFICULTY_RESPINS` is 3, 1 and 0 re-spins. `withDifficulty(rules, d)` sets the budget, keeps the older `hardMode` flag in step (true only on hard) and forces ratings off on hard. `QUICK_RULES` is normal (1 re-spin, down from the 2 quick drafts had before difficulties), `SEASON_RULES` easy (3, as before). A rule set with `hardMode: true` reads as hard whatever else it says; without the field it reads as normal. The hard-mode toggle at setup now means `withDifficulty(base, 'hard')`, and the Blindfolded achievement is won on a hard draft.
- **Show ratings** (`showRatings`, default true; `ratingsHidden(rules)` is hard, `hardMode` or the toggle off): off is blind mode, the existing hidden-ratings path (the squad list, the pitch, the bench and the draft log show `?`, and the ghost chip of a challenge is hidden). It is always off on hard.
- **Draft mode** (`draftMode: 'squad' | 'position'`, default squad). Squad First is the draft as it was: spin, then pick anyone usable and choose his slot. **Position First**: while the XI is being filled the player first chooses an open slot (`chooseSlot(u, d, slotId)`, stored as `DraftState.target`; no RNG), and `spin` refuses to run without one ("Choose a slot first"). The rules pool then counts as usable only players who can cover that slot (`canUse` and `eligibleSlots` narrow to the target), so the wheel, the spin weighting, the twists' candidate check and `canSpin` all see a pool of club-seasons with at least one undrafted player for that slot, era, club, nation and league rules applied: no dead spins. `pick` refuses any other slot ("This spin fills the CB slot you chose"), and the target clears with the pick. A re-spin keeps the slot, and the re-spun row records it (`history[].target`), as does the row "Auto-complete" skips. The one-club-season-once rule falls back as before (used club-seasons reopen when none is fresh), so a one-club wheel never empties for a slot it can fill at all; a slot nobody left can fill drops out of `spinnableSlots` and `chooseSlot` refuses it ("No club left on the wheel can fill GK"). `canSpin` before a slot is chosen is "some open slot is spinnable", so a dry one-nation wheel is still reported as dry. Bench picks, once the XI is full, are Squad First. The assistant keeps a slot already chosen and otherwise takes the first spinnable open slot in formation order. Tested over 40 seeds and four shapes with twists on, with one club (Burnley from 2020-21) and with one nation (Brazil, England and Spain). Squad First takes the same RNG draws as before, so `ENGINE_VERSION` stays `'3'`; Position First is a new rule and has no older seeds to keep. On the Draft screen, before each spin the open slots that can be filled pulse on the pitch and the spin button reads "Choose a slot" (disabled); tapping one marks it gold and the button reads "Spin for CB" (the slot's position); once the reel lands, every player in the list who can fill that slot is outlined in the accent colour with a "fits CB" or "can cover CB" chip (`.prow.fit`), and tapping one drafts him straight into it.
- **Player ratings** (`ratings: 'season' | 'prime'`, default season). Prime (`src/engine/prime.ts`) rates every player at his career best: his highest `overall` over every player-season of his `playerId` in the draft's wheel leagues (`rules.leagues`; every league in the universe when it is unset), ties to the earlier season, then the smaller player-season id, with that season's attributes. The wheel leagues, not the loaded ones: leagues load lazily, so a device that once opened La Liga holds it and another does not, and a prime over the loaded packs would rate the same England draft differently on the two and break challenge replays; the wheel leagues are always loaded for the draft, so the same rules rate everyone alike (tested: an England wheel rates every player the same with or without La Liga loaded beside it). A player with a single season in those leagues keeps that season's rating. One pass over the wheel leagues' club-seasons, cached per universe and league set in a WeakMap: 8 ms for England's 11,166 player-seasons (3,647 players), 65 ms for all seven leagues' 69,155 (19,096 players); 41,884 of those player-seasons are lifted, by 3.96 points on average and up to 29. A drafted player-season keeps its id, club, season, age, positions (slot eligibility was decided on them) and traits and takes the prime overall and attributes; `playerLookup` returns it that way under Prime, so the draft list, the pitch, team strength and the quick season all rate him there, and the assistant drafts by career-best rating. The real clubs he plays against keep their season ratings. Game-created players (Dynasty progression, free agents) are never re-rated.
- **Era presets** (`src/engine/era.ts`): `wheelSeasons(u, leagues)` lists the wheel leagues' seasons (the setup slider's stops) and `seasonStop(seasons, year)` the stop a year rests on and `eraHandles(preset, first, last)` where a preset puts the slider's handles; `eraRange(preset, first, last, custom?)` maps All-time to no bounds, 2000s+ to `fromSeason` 2000 clamped to the data's first season (2008-09 today, so it equals All-time), 2010s+ to 2010, Modern (2016+) to 2016, and a custom from/to to an ordered range clamped to the data. `eraCount(u, leagues, range)` gives the setup line: with England, All-time and 2000s+ are "19 of 19 seasons", 2010s+ "17 of 19", Modern "11 of 19" with the caption "2016-17 to 2026-27".
- **Labels.** `rulesTags(rules, base)` names what differs from the mode's defaults (`QUICK_RULES` for quick and Daily, `SEASON_RULES` otherwise): `Easy` or `Hard`, `Blind` (ratings off but not hard), `Position First`, `Prime ratings`. The Draft header, the summary line under the tier banner and a share-text line (`Hard · Position First · Prime ratings`) show them; default rules add nothing.
- **Older saves.** `normaliseRules` (`src/game/logic/save.ts`, applied to `settings.rules` and `draft.rules`) reads the difficulty from `hardMode` (hard) or the saved re-spin budget (3 or more easy, else normal), shows ratings unless hard, and fills Squad First and season ratings; the saved budget and `respinsLeft` are left alone.
- **Links.** The four fields travel in the rules diff and are checked by value; `rulesDiff` always writes `difficulty`, even at the default (`{ difficulty: 'normal' }` for default rules). A diff without a difficulty therefore comes from a link built before difficulties, when every quick draft had 2 re-spins and left `respins` out: `rulesFromPayload` opens it with `LEGACY_QUICK_RESPINS = 2` (unless the link wrote a budget out) and reads its `hardMode: true` as hard, so the friend gets the re-spins the challenger had. The replay ignores the budget, so verification is unchanged either way. Under Position First a row that drafted no one carries the slot it was filling, `[null, 'cb1']`, and the replay chooses each row's slot before its spin.
### Classic seasons (1992-93 to 2007-08)

The Premier League's sixteen seasons before the EA data starts ship as classic club-seasons (`cs.classic`, `cs.estimated`): real squads and real tables, with TouchLine's estimated ratings (data in `docs/DATA.md` section 8). They are **opt-in**: `rulesPool` skips them unless `DraftRules.classic` is true, so a draft, a challenge link or a save made without the rule spins exactly as before. They sit after every standard club-season in `u.clubSeasons` (`buildLeague` appends them, `mergeUniverses` puts every league's standard club-seasons first), so the weighted index of a standard club-season never moves. Every other pool follows the same rule: the market (`MarketContext.classic`), friendlies and the youth intake take classic players only for a game with `rules.classic`; the One-Nation scan only with `EraRange.classic`; the Continental Cup field, job offers and the dynasty's next season only in a classic league season (`isClassicSeason`); the Daily never (`dailySettings` filters them, which changes none of its output: `classicGate.test.ts` pins the first 60 Daily seeds and 200 seeded drafts to hashes captured before the gate). `RULE_CHECKS.classic` is a boolean, so a challenge link carries the rule.

- **Choosing them (Setup).** One toggle in the Era panel, under the slider: "Classic 1992-93 to 2007-08" with the compact "est." chip (`data-testid="era-classic"`), shown only when a wheel league has classic seasons (`classicFromFor`; England today) and never on the Daily or a challenge. Turning it on extends the slider's stops to 1992-93 (`wheelSeasons(u, leagues, true)`; "N of M seasons" counts them too, `eraCount` with `classic`) and puts the handles on 1992-93 and 2007-08; `setupRules` then adds `classic: true` (`SetupChoices.classic`), and the slider can widen the range into the standard seasons or run it to All-time. Off, the slider, the presets and the rules are exactly what they were, with no `classic` key. The league season select lists classic seasons too, tagged "(estimated ratings)" (`leagueSeasonChoices`): a side can play 1992-93 (22 clubs, 42 games) with a standard or a classic wheel. The setup line names "classic seasons (estimated ratings)", and a challenge banner says so.
- **Features after the gate.** Prime ratings count a classic season as a player's best only when the game opts in (`primeOf(u, id, leagues, classic)`, cached per league set and opt-in); the quick season's January listings skip classic club-seasons unless `rules.classic` (without this gate the Daily, which has the January window, would have changed); the reel's tiers rank standard and classic club-seasons apart (`tierTable`), so no standard club-season changes tier. `classicRelease.test.ts` plays seeded quick seasons (plain, Prime, bench, Position First, hard, Europe, an era) and four Dailies on England's standard pack alone and with the classic pack loaded, and requires the same game.
- **Labels.** Every estimated club-season and player-season shows an "Estimated ratings" chip (`EstimatedChip`, `data-testid="estimated-chip"`, a dashed neutral chip; "est." in a player row): the Draft's landed club-season, every `PlayerRow` (Draft, Explore, the Dugout), Explore's season header and club header, its highest-rated list and its search, and the career table. Explore's season select now starts at 1992-93 for England (classic seasons tagged "(est.)", with a one-line note over the table), and the career chart draws estimated seasons striped and fainter, with "estimated" in the tooltip. The reel's result line carries the compact chip beside the tier once it lands on a classic club-season. `isEstimated(u, ref)` decides from the club-season a player-season came from: pass the player-season itself where it is at hand (a dynasty's aged copy, `${playerId}@dyn${year}`, keeps its `clubId` and `season`, so it keeps the mark into 2008-09 and later); a bare id is read from the universe or its `${playerId}@${season}@${clubId}` shape. The share text gains a line from `estimatedNote` ("Estimated ratings: 3 classic picks", "…, a classic league season") and the share card's footer ends "· estimated ratings" when the XI or the league season is estimated; nothing changes for a standard result. "How it works" explains the estimate. The head-to-head season picker and the Dugout search still leave classic seasons out unless the game opted in.

### The Super League

A Quick Draft can be played in the **Super League** instead of a real league season (`DraftRules.superLeague`, optional, read through `isSuperLeague` in `src/game/logic/superLeague.ts`, which also needs `mode === 'quick'`, so a managed season or the Daily never plays it; challenge links play as quick drafts and carry it). The field is in `src/engine/superLeague.ts`:

- **The nineteen.** Every club-season with at least eleven players in the pool (`poolClubSeasons(u, classic)`: classic seasons only with `rules.classic`) is built as an AI side exactly as a league season builds one (`aiClubOf`: the club's style and shape, every player fresh) and rated by `aiStrength`: the auto-picked best XI (`autoPickLineup` through `aiTeamInput`) scored by `computeTeamStrength`. Each club keeps its strongest season (ties to the earlier season), the clubs are ranked by that strength (ties to the earlier season, then the club id) and the top nineteen are the league (`superLeagueField`, `buildSuperClubs`); ranks 20 to 34 are the Continental Cup field (`buildSuperRestClubs`). The ranking is cached per universe and flag (`rankedClubs`, about 0.6 s over all seven leagues). It draws nothing and depends on no seed, so everyone faces the same nineteen; it depends on the leagues loaded, so a Super League game loads all of them (`leaguesFor` in the store, `openChallenge`, and Setup's league choice). With the data of 2026-10-25, classic on or off (no classic season makes the cut): Real Madrid 2017-18, Liverpool 2019-20, Manchester City 2023-24, Bayern Munich 2016-17, Paris Saint-Germain 2025-26, FC Barcelona 2018-19, Juventus 2017-18, Arsenal 2026-27, Inter 2008-09, Manchester United 2008-09, Chelsea 2008-09, Atlético Madrid 2018-19, Tottenham Hotspur 2017-18, Borussia Dortmund 2025-26, AC Milan 2023-24, Napoli 2022-23, Aston Villa 2025-26, Roma 2017-18 and Bayer Leverkusen 2024-25 (strengths 87.7 down to 83.1). The sides are named with their season, "Real Madrid 2017-18" (`AiClub.name`, `superSideName`, `sideNamer` for the screens), and keyed by club id as in any league.
- **The season.** `quickLeague(u, settings)` gives the quick season (`startRun`), the pundits (`oddsLeague`) and the cups their AI sides and handicap: in the Super League `buildSuperClubs` with an offset of 0 (full strength; the Opposition setting does not apply, and the setup line says "in the Super League, full strength"), otherwise `buildAiClubs` and `aiRatingOffset` exactly as before. Everything after that is the ordinary quick season on the game's `Rng`: 20 sides, 38 matchweeks (`quickSeasonGames` returns `SUPER_LEAGUE_GAMES`), the January window after matchweek 19 (listings from the wheel's leagues, which for the Super League are all seven), the bench, the manager, highlights and challenge replays. The points-record achievement and the record books are a real league's and are not offered; the summary names no real champion and no "real side".
- **After the league.** No domestic cup (`quickCupStatus` reports `SUPER_NO_CUP` and the Cup card is not drawn); after a top-seven finish the Continental Cup is played against `buildSuperRestClubs(u, classic, 15)` at full strength, the same rules and fork as any quick season's run.
- **Words.** `superTierLabel` (`tierName(tier, position, true)`): "Perfect Super League season", "Super League invincibles", "Super League champions", "Bottom three" for the last three (nobody goes down), the rest as in any league; `superTierBlurb` likewise. The pundits say "Win the Super League" and "Finish bottom three"; the header, the summary banner and the final table show the Super League mark (`SuperLeagueMark`, a crown in an accent chip, `QuickLeagueFlag`); the share text reads "Touchline XI, Super League" and the share card's subtitle "Super League"; the Profile's leaderboard row keeps `LeaderboardEntry.superLeague` to say the same.
- **Setup.** One more league card in step 3, League and season, after the real leagues (`data-testid="league-super"`, Quick Draft only, two or more leagues available), a `.chip.super` with the mark and its title (the hint "The 19 greatest sides in the data"); choosing it sets the wheel to every league and loads them, puts a "One season, full strength" chip (`super-season`) where the season select sits, shows the help line under the cards (`league-help`), and hides the Opposition field in Advanced; the Quick Draft line above Spin (`quickSetupLine`) says "Super League" in place of the league count and never "Realistic", and the host chip of `setupChips` reads "Super League"; `SetupChoices.superLeague` makes `setupRules` write `superLeague: true` (no key otherwise). `describeRules` lists "Super League" for a challenge.
- **How hard.** The elite XI of `perfectChase.test.ts` taken across all seven leagues (overall 88.4) went 38-0 in 0 of 1,000 Super League seasons (never unbeaten, champions in 332, 20.7 wins on average; the pundits give 0% perfect, 43% for the title, 73 points), against 6 of 100 perfect seasons and 100 titles on the same seeds in the Premier League 2025-26. Reported, not tuned; `superLeague.test.ts` reruns a 30-seed sample.
- **Nothing else moves.** ENGINE_VERSION stays `'4'`: a game without the rule takes the same draws in the same order. `superLeague.test.ts` pins three Premier League quick seasons with their January deal and cups, three European Nights seasons with classic seasons and the Continental Cup, their pundits' odds and the Daily of 2026-10-25 to hashes taken on the commit before the Super League (57f3453), and the whole existing suite passes unchanged.

### One-Nation Draft

`DraftRules.onlyNation` holds an ISO3 code as stored in `PlayerSeason.nationality` (`'BRA'`); `'UNK'`, the code for the 1,876 player-seasons whose nationality is not known, is never offered. The rule is one predicate, `nationAllows`, applied in `canUse`, `eligibleSlots` and `pick` (which throws "`{name} is not Brazil`"), so the wheel pool, the spin weighting, the assistant's auto-draft and the head-to-head usable list all inherit it: the wheel still never lands on a club-season without an eligible player, and in the squad list players of another nationality are dimmed with a "not Brazil" chip.

- **Setup.** The one-nation select (`nationDepth`, `src/engine/nations.ts`) lists the nations deep enough for the chosen shape in the chosen wheel leagues and era: for every position in the formation, at least 2 × its slot count in distinct humans who can cover it at `accomplished` fit (the draft's own predicate), and at least `squadSize + 6` distinct humans in all (11 + 6 for a quick draft). It is one pass over the loaded player-seasons (`nationCover`, about 160 ms for all seven leagues, memoised on wheel leagues, era and formation) and a cheap filter per squad size (`nationDepthFrom`), so the squad-size slider never rescans. With the 2026-10-24 data a 4-3-3 across all seven leagues offers 71 of the 161 known nations (68 at squad size 18, 65 at 22); England alone offers 26 (21 at 18). Côte d'Ivoire, with Premier League players in every era but no goalkeeper who can cover the position in the Premier League data (two across the seven leagues), is left out; Brazil counts 1,141 distinct players across the seven leagues and 107 in England. The option label reads `🇧🇷 Brazil · 1141 players`. The Daily ignores the rule.
- **Running dry.** The depth check covers year one; in a Dynasty the rule is copied into every summer draft, so bonus spins can exhaust a thin nation over the years. `canSpin(u, d)` is the pool-not-empty check: the store refuses to spin and toasts "The wheel has run dry for Brazil" instead of surfacing an engine error, `autoDraft` stops instead of throwing, and the Draft screen replaces the spin button with a notice. When the XI is full the draft may close short of the squad size (`finishDraft` accepts `picksMade >= 11` once `canSpin` is false); with slots still open the side cannot start and the notice says to go home and pick another nation.
- **Market and academy.** `MarketContext.onlyNation` travels from `settings.rules` into the January window, the Dynasty summer window, scouting trickle and every approach. `openWindow` samples only club-seasons that hold someone of the nation and takes only that nation's players from each, with a sampling budget of 60 tries per listing instead of 20 because repeats are frequent for a small nation; without the rule its random draws are unchanged. `enquire` answers an approach for anyone else, from the shortlist or the search box, with "The board wants a Brazil squad" and lists nothing. `buildYouthIntake` keeps only youngsters of the nation.
- **Record.** Winning the league with the rule on unlocks `one-nation-champions` ("International"), in both the season and the quick path. The share text gains the line `One nation: 🇧🇷 Brazil` before the seed, the summary shows the same chip, and the leaderboard entry stores `nation` and shows the flag beside the team name.

### Wheel twists

`DraftRules.twists` (on in `QUICK_RULES` and therefore in the Daily, off in `SEASON_RULES`; `startH2H` passes `twists: false`; a saved draft without the field reads as off) makes every third spin a **twist spin**: spins 3, 6 and 9 of a quick draft, so three of its eleven. `Twist = 'golden' | 'basement' | 'blind' | 'decade'`:

| Twist | Wheel | Where it is empty |
|---|---|---|
| 🥇 Golden | title-winning club-seasons only (`cs.champions`) | leagues without a table that season (`position` 0) |
| 🪣 Basement | the bottom three of their league that season: `position >= clubsThatSeason − 2`, with `clubsThatSeason` the row count of `u.tables[league][season]` (20 in England, Spain and Italy, 18 in the Bundesliga) | the same |
| 🙈 Blind | the plain wheel; ratings are hidden in the squad list, on the pitch and in the draft log for that pick (the same hiding as hard mode) | never |
| 📅 Decade | club-seasons from the decade of the last pick (`Math.floor(season / 10)` of the last history row with a `pickedId`) | before the first pick |

Inside `spin()`: `d.twist` is cleared, and when `rules.twists && (d.spins + 1) % 3 === 0` the four twists are tried against `wheelPool(u, { ...d, twist })`, the ones with an empty wheel are dropped, and `rng.pick` chooses among the rest, one extra RNG draw on a twist spin and none on the others. Blind is always possible, so a twist spin always has a twist; in Ligue 1, the Eredivisie and the Primeira Liga, whose club-seasons all have `position` 0 and no `champions` flag, only blind and decade ever come up (tested over 30 seeds). `wheelPool` applies the twist as one more filter beside era, club, nation and league, so the spin weighting, the usefulness count, `canSpin`, the assistant and the reel all see the twisted pool: a golden spin runs the reel through the whole league that season with only the champion lit. The twist is recorded on the history row (`history[].twist`) and cleared by `pick()` and `respin()`; a re-spin on a twist spin logs the twist on the skipped row and lands on a plain spin number (spin 4 after re-spinning spin 3), which is left as it is rather than carried forward. "Auto-complete" clears a pending spin's twist with the spin it discards. `spin()` builds the rules pool (era, club, nation, league and the `canUse` check over every squad: 380 club-seasons in England, 2,526 across the seven leagues) once per spin and narrows it per twist, so the candidate check costs four cheap filters on a twist spin rather than four more passes over the squads; `wheelPool` returns exactly that narrowed pool.

The twist draw is part of the seed, so `ENGINE_VERSION` moved from `'1'` to `'2'` with this change: a seed drafted with twists on lands on the same first two clubs as before and diverges from the third spin. The Draft screen shows a `TwistCard` above the wheel (icon, name, one-line rule; a dashed "Twist spin next" card before a twist spin), the reel shows the twist's name beside the season drum ("Golden spin"), and the Setup screen has a toggle under Wheel rules (default on for Quick Draft, off for Season and Dynasty; the Daily is always on). The share text adds a line with the icons of the twists the picks went through, `Twists 🥇 🙈 📅`.

### The reel

The wheel is drawn as a Counter-Strike case opening (`src/ui/components/CaseReel.tsx`, used by the Draft and the head-to-head screen). It only presents a draw the engine has already made: `spin` picks the season first (uniformly over the seasons in the pool) and then a club inside it, and the reel shows them in that order. Nothing on it feeds the game. The filler cards and the stop jitter use `Math.random`; `dealReel` (`src/game/logic/reel.ts`, pure) takes the random source as an argument, so the tests pass a seeded one.

- **Stage 1, the season (1.0 s).** A drum rolls for 850 ms through the seasons the wheel could land on this spin (the seasons in `wheelPool` with the twist applied, counting up to the drawn one) and stops on the drawn season, written `2017-18`. The label beside it reads "Season", or the twist's name ("Golden spin"). The strip is veiled while the drum rolls, and only real seasons from the pool ever pass through the drum; once it stops, the drawn season is the only one shown.
- **Stage 2, the club strip (5.8 s, from 1.0 s to 6.8 s).** 70 cards, every club-season of the drawn season in the draft's leagues (`reelLeagues`: the rules' leagues, as `wheelPool` filters them, or every loaded league when the rules name none; head-to-head reads the same rules): the whole league that year, or with several leagues all of them, each card tagged ENG, ESP and so on. They are dealt in shuffled rounds, outwards from the landing card, so a one-league strip shows every club of that season and no card appears twice in a row. The drawn club-season is card 60. Cards the wheel could stop on are lit: `wheelPool` filtered to that season (`draftReelPool`), or in head-to-head the pool `h2hSpin` draws from (`h2hReelPool`: the club-seasons useful to both managers still drafting, else the first picker's wheel). The rest are greyed out, so a golden spin runs through the whole league with only the champion lit. The strip slides on one curve, `reelPosition` in `src/ui/reelCurve.ts`, sampled into the animation's keyframes (linear between them, one every 16 ms or so): a deceleration on `cubic-bezier(0.05, 0.7, 0.65, 1)` (`STRIP_CURVE`) for 5.6 s that runs past the stop point by `OVERSHOOT` (12% of a card) and comes to rest there, then a half-cosine ease back onto the stop point over the last 200 ms, so position and speed are continuous and there is no jitter. It stops anywhere up to 0.45 of a card either side of the card's centre (`REEL_JITTER`, `Math.random`, the near miss), so the marker can sit near an edge; the club is the engine's, only the offset is cosmetic.
- **Measured** (unit test over 68 and 84 px cards, windows 258 to 720 px and the full jitter range; production build in Chromium). Ten to fifteen cards pass in the first quarter second and half the distance in the first second; the last 1.5 s creeps over 1.8 to 3 cards and the overshoot's return. The ticks (section on sound in `docs/DESIGN_SYSTEM.md`) go from 6 to 15 ms apart at the start (capped at 15 ms when played) to about 400 ms at the end, four in the last 1.5 s, the last in the ease back. After the strip comes to rest the reveal waits 400 ms (`FULL_TIMING.hold`): the reveal and the pick come about 7.2 s after the tap (`SETTLE_MS`), measured 7.6 to 8.4 s on a loaded machine.
- **Stage 3, the landing (0.5 s).** After the 400 ms hold at rest the card pops with a ring and glow in its tier colour, the reel window's edge flashes the same colour, and a gold card gets a sweep of light. The line under the reel shows the club, the season and the tier chip ("Gold · Elite"). The Draft opens the pick at the landing, not on a timer.
- **Speed.** Re-spins always run `QUICK_TIMING` (a 450 ms drum, the strip from 0.5 s, a 2 s slide with a 150 ms ease back, a 150 ms hold: about 2.7 s to the reveal), detected as a run that replaces another drawn club-season with no idle reel between; so does every run when the viewer turns on Reel speed: Quick in Profile (`touchline-quick-spin`, `src/ui/viewerPrefs.ts`). Auto-complete runs no reel at all. A skip still lands at once, with no hold.
- **Skip.** A tap or click on the reel, the "Tap to skip" button, or Space or Enter while the reel runs jumps both stages to the landing. Space and Enter are left alone when they are meant for a field or another enabled button.
- **Reduced motion** (`prefers-reduced-motion: reduce`). No slide and no roll: the season shows at once, and the landed card with its glow 0.5 s later.
- **Resizing.** The stop is measured from the layout, so a `ResizeObserver` on the reel window and the landing card measures it again whenever either changes size (a phone turned, a window dragged, the cards growing from 68 px to 84 px at the 420 px container breakpoint): mid-slide the slide retargets with `setKeyframes`, and after the landing the strip moves so the marker stays on the drawn club. With reduced motion the strip stays where it is until the landing, so a resize never shows the drawn card early. Before this, resizing the window from 1280 to 1000 px wide after a landing left the marker ten cards away, on a club that was not drafted.
- **Screen readers.** One polite live region says the season once when the drum stops ("Season 2017-18, Golden spin") and the club and tier once at the landing ("Manchester City 2017-18: Gold, Elite"), never the passing cards. The strip itself is `aria-hidden`.
- **One run per spin.** Each spin mounts its own run, keyed by the spin number and the club-season, so nothing of the previous spin (its landed card, its season, its squad) can show at the start of the next, and a re-spin onto the same club-season still runs (at the quick timing). The run is measured and started from zero in a layout effect before the first paint, and moves with the Web Animations API (`element.animate`). The pick opens at the reveal, the hold after the slide's `finished` promise resolves, exactly once per run; unmounting or a new spin cancels the animations and the reduced-motion timer. The Draft and head-to-head screens derive "spinning" from whether the reel has settled on the current `draft.current` object, so a new spin reads as spinning in the very render the store hands it over, and Re-spin is disabled before a second tap can land. Before this a double tap on Re-spin burned both re-spins, the first spin after the Draft mounted was the only one that slid in full (the reset and the slide were folded into one React render), and the hub showed a random year from 1990 to 2026 while the reel ran.

#### Rarity tiers

`src/game/logic/rarity.ts`. A club-season's strength is the mean overall rating of its eleven best players (season ratings; `clubSeasonStrength(cs, rating?)` takes another accessor, for a future prime-ratings mode). `tierTable(u, leagues)` ranks every club-season of the draft's leagues across all seasons by strength, ties broken by the club-season key, and cuts the ranking into fixed shares. It is memoised per universe, league set and rating accessor, and it never sees the draft, so a club keeps its colour from the first spin to the last. A one-league draft is ranked within its league; a multi-league draft across all of its leagues.

| Tier | Share | Colour | Label |
|---|---|---|---|
| Gold | top 5% | `#e4ae39` | Elite |
| Red | next 10% | `#eb4b4b` | Great |
| Pink | next 20% | `#d32ce6` | Good |
| Purple | next 30% | `#8847ff` | Decent |
| Blue | last 35% | `#4b69ff` | Underdog |

The colours are `--tier-gold` to `--tier-blue` on `:root`, in the reel section of `styles.css`. Text on a tier colour uses `--tier-<tier>-ink`: black, or white on purple, each at 4.5:1 or better (gold 10.4, red 5.6, pink 5.3, purple 4.8, blue 4.8), the same in both themes because the chip carries its own background. Every reel card has a rarity bar along its bottom edge and a faint glow in its colour. Once the reel has landed, the Draft and head-to-head squad headers show the tier chip after the record line, and each row of the draft log carries a dot in its tier colour. With the 2026-10-25 England data, 17 of the 18 finished title winners are gold or red (Leicester 2015-16 is purple), and Manchester City 2017-18 is gold.

### Spin odds

`src/engine/draft.ts` (`spinOdds`, `spinWeight`), `src/engine/h2h.ts` (`h2hSpinOdds`, `h2hWeight`), `src/game/logic/tierOdds.ts` (`tierOdds`, `h2hTierOdds`, `formatOdds`), `src/ui/components/SpinOdds.tsx`. Before every spin in every mode (Quick Draft, the Daily, Season and Dynasty drafts, head-to-head), and before a re-spin while a spin is showing and re-spins are left, a panel under the reel (`data-testid="spin-odds"`) gives the exact chance of landing in each rarity tier: a bar of tier-coloured segments and the percentages in the tier colours (one decimal under 10%, "<0.1%" for a chance too small to print but not zero). It is computed from the same steps `spin` takes, in the same order, on the real wheel pool: the Icons roll (Gamble only), the twist (a Crazy golden surprise, or each twist whose wheel is not empty, equally likely, on a twist spin), a season uniformly among the twisted pool's seasons, then each club-season of that season by `spinWeight` (the weight `spin` itself now calls, unchanged). Under Position First before a slot is chosen the panel reads "Next spin, any open slot". The tiers are the reel's (`tierTable` over the draft's leagues). `gamble.test.ts` checks that the chances sum to 100% in every state of many drafts (plain, twisted, Crazy, Gamble, Underdog, Position First) and that 2,500 seeded spins from one state land in each tier within four standard errors of the panel, on a plain spin and a twist spin, and the same for head-to-head.

### Quick Draft modes

`src/engine/modes.ts` (cards, toggling, words, `crazyFormation`), `src/engine/draft.ts` (the rules), `src/ui/components/SetupBits.tsx` (`ModeCards`). Setup's **Modes** panel (Quick Draft only, never the Daily or a challenge, which carry their own rules) is a row of icon cards that switch on and off, several at once: Crazy 🌀, Hard 💀, Underdog 🐶, Legends 👑, Speedrun ⏱️, Gamble 🎲 and Upgrader ⬆️. Five are flags in `DraftRules.modes` (`QuickMode[]`, optional, left out when none is on, read through `modesOf`/`hasMode`); Upgrader is its own rule (`DraftRules.upgrader`, with a `RULE_CHECKS` entry); Hard is the existing difficulty, so tapping the Hard card sets the Difficulty cards to Hard and tapping it off (or choosing Easy or Normal) clears it (`toggleModeCard`, `withModes`, `setupRules` with `choices.modes`). Underdog and Legends exclude each other (switching one on switches the other off). The draft header shows the cards as chips (`data-testid="draft-modes"`), the summary line under the tier banner and the share text carry `modesLine` ("🌀 Crazy · 💀 Hard · ⏱️ Speedrun", Hard then dropped from the tag line), the setup line says "crazy mode" and so on, and the fixed-rules pills of a challenge name each flag. `modes` is in `RULE_CHECKS` (known flags, each once), so a challenge link carries it in its rules diff and replays it.

- **Crazy.** `nextSpinTwisted` is true on every spin (`withModes` also turns `twists` on, so the twist card shows), so every spin draws a twist from the game's stream as a twist spin always has. The formation is drawn at Start by `startFormation` (`logic/setup.ts`), which calls `crazyFormation(seed)` from a stream seeded apart from the game's (`${seed}:crazy-formation`) and then travels in the settings and a link's `f`, so a replay never draws it again; Setup shows "Crazy: seed X draws a 3-5-2" in place of the formation grid. After every pick that leaves the draft open, one draw from `DraftState.crazyRng` (seeded by `newDraft` from `${seed}:crazy-surprises`, never the game's stream) lands a surprise with chance `CRAZY_SURPRISE_CHANCE = 0.3` (tested within 0.1 over 60 drafts) and a second draw picks it: a **free re-spin** (`respinsLeft + 1`), a **golden spin** (`forcedTwist = 'golden'`: the next spin is golden without a twist draw; offered only when champions are on the wheel at the roll, so it is never announced under Underdog or in a league without tables, where it would do nothing) or a **forced position** (`forcedSlot`, only while the XI is filling with two or more open slots some club can fill: the next pick must go there, the wheel lands only on clubs that can fill it, the bench is shut and `chooseSlot` refuses another slot; under Position First it is also the chosen slot, so a re-spin row names it). `targetSlot` returns the forced slot first. The Draft screen shows "Surprise!" and the forced slot on the pitch.
- **Underdog.** `rulesAdmit` keeps only `bottomHalf` club-seasons: below half the places of that season's final table; a league-season without a table (France, the Netherlands, Portugal) ranks its clubs by the mean of their eleven best ratings (ties by club id) and the lower half count, cached per universe in a `WeakMap`. Golden twists then never qualify, so the twist draw offers the others.
- **Legends.** `rulesAdmit` keeps only club-seasons with a player rated `LEGENDS_RATING = 85` or more that season (`hasLegend`).
- **Gamble** 🎲 (`modes: ['gamble']`, `src/engine/icons.ts`). Every spin until it has come draws once from the draft's own Icons stream (`DraftState.gambleRng`, seeded `seed:gamble-icons` by `newDraft`); with chance `ICONS_CHANCE = 0.01`, and only when an icon fits this spin (an open slot, the chosen slot, the nation rule; anyone undrafted for the bench), the spin lands on the **Icons collection** instead of a club-season: `d.current` is `{ clubId: 'icons', season: 0 }`, the game's stream is not drawn at all, and no twist applies. At most once per draft (`iconsUsed`: landed on, re-spun or picked from). The collection (`iconsCollection`) is the `ICONS_SIZE = 60` best players of all time on the draft's wheel leagues, each at his prime season (`primeSeasons`: highest rating, ties to the earlier season, then the smaller id), one season per player; with European Nights that is all seven leagues. Classic seasons (estimated ratings) are never in it, Classic on or off. The reel lands on a gold-star Icons card in its own colour above gold (`--tier-icons`, aqua), with a shimmer and a star burst; the odds panel lists Icons at 1% above gold; the squad list shows the icons with their clubs and seasons; the draft log marks the row with a star. Gamble also brings the Player Upgrader.
- **Upgrader** ⬆️ (`DraftRules.upgrader`, also on with Gamble; `src/engine/upgrader.ts`, `src/ui/components/Upgrader.tsx`). Once per draft, from the draft screen's Player Upgrader panel: choose a drafted player and a boost of +2, +5 or +10. The chance is shown as a whole per cent on a dial in the rarity colours before confirming (`upgradeChance`: 80%, 55% or 30% for a player rated 60 or less, falling linearly to a quarter of that at 100, clamped to 1-95%), and it is the exact chance: the roll is 0-99 from the draft's own `upgradeRng` (seeded `seed:upgrader`, forked once by the player-season id and the boost, so it is the same whenever in the draft it is made, and replaying the seed with "Run it back" after seeing one choice's needle tells nothing about another choice) and wins when `roll < chance`. A win raises his rating by the boost (never above 99), a loss drops it by half the boost rounded up (1, 3 or 5); he stays in the side either way. The result is `DraftState.upgrade` (`{ id, boost, chance, roll, won, delta }`, kept with a quick game's draft); `playerLookup` moves that player's overall and every attribute by `delta` (`boostedPlayer`, a cached copy; the universe is never changed), so the draft pitch, team strength and the whole quick season see it. A challenge link carries it as `g: [row, boost]` (the row of `p` whose pick was upgraded), and the replay makes the same gamble after the draft, which lands the same. In-game only: nothing is bought, there is no currency, and the odds are always shown first.
- **Power-ups** 🧰 (`DraftRules.powerUps`, a toggle under the Modes cards, with a `RULE_CHECKS` entry; `src/engine/powerups.ts`, `src/ui/components/PowerUps.tsx`, `PowerUpButton.tsx`, `src/game/logic/powerups.ts`). A tray under the reel of six power-ups, each usable once per draft, each with an icon, a state line and an Info tip (`InfoTip`): **Upgrader** ⬆️ (the Player Upgrader above, moved into the tray; power-ups turn it on); **Re-roll season** 🔁 (while a spin is showing: keep the club and draw another of its seasons on this spin's wheel, twist and rules applied, each equally likely and listed with its chance before confirming; one draw from the draft's own stream `powerRng`, seeded `seed:powerups` by `newDraft` and forked by the spin, so the game's stream does not move; the spin count does not change; never on an Icons spin); **Peek** 👁️ (between spins: the season the next spin lands on, worked out by spinning a copy of the draft with a copy of the game's stream, so nothing is drawn; while it shows, the Position First slot cannot change and Swap waits, so the spin lands where the peek said); **Swap** 🔀 (between spins, no peek showing: move a starter to another slot he can cover, exchanging with the player there when he can cover the first slot; no RNG, no re-spin spent; a Position First slot it fills is cleared); **Captain's armband** ©️ (+2 to one drafted player's overall and attributes for the season, applied with the Upgrader's change by `playerLookup` through `draftBoost`); **Lock** 🔒 (before the Upgrader: an Upgrader loss on the locked player leaves his rating as it was, `DraftUpgrade.locked`, the roll unchanged). Uses are kept in `DraftState.powerUps` (kind and the history row `at`), listed in the draft log (`powerUpLog`) and as one icon line in the share text (`powerUpsShareLine`, "🧰 Power-ups ⬆️+5 🔁 👁️"). A challenge link carries them as `w` (`['r', row]`, `['p', row]`, `['s', row, from, to]`, `['a', pickRow]`, `['l', pickRow]`, each kind once, checked by `isChallengePowerUps`; a peek no spin followed, because the bench closed while it showed, changed nothing and gets no row); `replayDraft` makes the swap before its row's spin, the peek before it and the re-roll after it, then the replay locks, upgrades and gives the armband, so the same picks land on the same squad.
- **Speedrun.** The Draft screen runs a `SPEEDRUN_SECONDS = 20` clock per pick (a chip in the header, red from 5 s; paused while the reel runs; reset by every pick). It is the UI's alone: on expiry the store's `speedrunPick` calls `assistantTurn`, which chooses a slot under Position First, spins when no spin is showing (from the game's stream, as any spin) and makes `assistantChoice`'s pick (the choice `autoDraft` makes, now shared), so the draft history is an ordinary one and a link replays it.

No mode changes a draw of a draft without it, and with no flag `DraftRules` has no `modes` key at all, so `ENGINE_VERSION` stays `'4'` (the assistant's choice moved into `assistantChoice` unchanged; every seeded test passes as before). `modes.test.ts` checks every landed club-season of 30 Underdog and 30 Legends drafts, a twist on every Crazy spin, the surprise rate and kinds, 200 assistant drafts over five mode sets, both draft modes and a bench without a dry wheel, and verified replays of links with modes (a Crazy Position First draft included).

### Ghost drafts: challenge links and run-it-back

A finished quick draft can be packed into a link and replayed on another device, because the wheel, the picks and the quick season all run on the one seeded `Rng`.

- **Payload** (`src/engine/challenge.ts`, `ChallengePayload`, `v: 1`): the data stamp (`LoadedUniverse.generatedAt`) and `ENGINE_VERSION` it was built on; the challenger's name, cut to `CHALLENGE_NAME_MAX = 24` characters with control characters stripped; `s` = seed, formation, league, wheel leagues, host club, league season, difficulty and the draft rules as a diff against `QUICK_RULES` (`leagues` always travels as `wl`); `p` = one row per draft history row, `[playerId, slotId]` with the real player id (`s200458`) rather than the player-season id, and `[null, null]` for a re-spin or a discarded spin (`[null, slotId]` under Position First); `c` = the claim (points, won, drawn, lost, tier); `k` = `historyKey`, the cyrb53 hash in base 36 of the club-seasons the wheel landed on, in order; `x` (optional, `ChallengeSeason`) = the quick season's own choices when any differ from a default season: `t` the first-half tactics (the whole season without a window) as the instructions off `defaultTactics` plus `captain` and `penalties` player-season ids, `t2` the second-half tactics when they changed at the January pause, `j` the January deal as `[incoming player-season id, slot id]`, `s` the Quick Draft bench changes as `[first matchweek, slot id, incoming player-season id]` (at most three, section 12), `m` the Quick Draft manager's id (section 12, "Managers"). A default season writes no `x`, so its link is byte for byte what it was before. `encodeChallenge` writes it as UTF-8 JSON in base64url after `#c=`: about 600 to 650 characters for an eleven-pick England quick draft with default rules, 28 of them the always-written `difficulty` (a full player-season id per pick would be about twice that). `decodeChallenge` checks every field, the formation id, the tier and the rules diff key by key (only the seventeen known rules, each with its type: `squadSize` an integer from 11 to 18, `respins` 0 to 10, `benchPicks` 0 to 5, `minFit` one of the four fits, `difficulty`, `draftMode` and `ratings` one of their values, the flags booleans, `leagues` never, since it travels as `wl`), and throws "Not a Touchline challenge link" (or "comes from a newer version" when `v` is above 1) rather than returning a partial payload. `openChallenge` then refuses a link naming a league this build does not ship, or a host club that did not play that league season.
- **Replay** (`replayDraft` in the engine, `replayChallenge` in `src/game/logic/challenge.ts`): `createGame` from the link's settings, then for every row one `spin`; a null row decrements `respinsLeft` and logs the row the way `respin` does without spinning again, a pick row rebuilds `${playerId}@${season}@${clubId}` from the club-season the wheel landed on and calls `pick` (which takes no RNG). The replay must end complete (or, on a one-nation wheel that has run dry, with eleven and no spin left), with as many history rows as `p`, and with `historyKey` equal to `k`; then the season from `x` (the tactics applied; with `t2` or `j` the window opened, the second-half tactics set, the deal found among the listings or the replay refused; otherwise `runQuickSeason` in one go) and the result is compared with the claim figure by figure. The outcome is `verified: 'yes'` when it matches, `'no'` with the reason ("Spin 4: no player s000001 in the burnley 2025 squad", "the picks replay to 70 points (20-10-8), not 73", "The wheel did not land on the same club-seasons") when the picks or the claim were tampered with, and `'unverified'` with the reason when the data stamp or the engine version differ, in which case nothing is replayed beyond a best-effort draft so the ghost picks can still show. Nothing throws. A replay costs one quick season, about 300 ms in the test suite.
- **Every spin is in the history.** "Auto-complete" used to drop a pending spin (`draft.current = null`) that had already consumed RNG, so a link built from such a game would drift on replay; it now pushes the spin as `{ pickedId: null, slotId: null, skipped: true }` (with its twist) and the draft log shows it as "skipped". `respin` already logged the spin it discarded.
- **Opening a link.** `App.tsx` reads `location.hash` once the default league is loaded (and on `hashchange`), clears it with `history.replaceState` so a reload does not reopen it, and calls the store's `openChallenge`: decode, refuse a league this build does not have, `ensureLeagues` for the link's league and wheel leagues, replay, then `pendingChallenge` (transient, never saved) and the Setup screen. `sw.js` serves `index.html` for navigations, so the link works offline and under a base path. Setup's daily lock is generalised into a `fixed` object (daily or challenge) that hides the seed, the wheel rules, the league, the host, the season, the difficulty and the formation and shows the banner "Challenge from Minh: 70 pts as Leeds United 2025-26, 4-3-3 (TOP-SIX FINISH, 20-10-8). Same seed, same wheel, calibrated difficulty, twists on. Seed …" with the verification line under it; an unverified or unverifiable link still plays. The game starts with `mode: 'quick'` whatever seed the link carries, a daily seed included, so the Daily streak and `dailyResults` are untouched, and `GameSettings.challenge` (`ChallengeSettings`: name, the picks resolved to player-season ids with their club-season, the claim, `verified`, `reason`, data and engine stamps) travels with the save; older saves have no field and read as a plain game.
- **The ghost.** The Draft header reads "vs Minh (70 pts)", and once the wheel settles a chip under it shows the challenger's row for the same spin number: "Last time here: K. De Bruyne" when the wheel landed on the same club-season, "Minh's spin 5: K. De Bruyne (Manchester City 2017-18)" once the drafts have diverged (a different pick changes `takenPlayers` and the pool, so later spins land elsewhere), "Last time here: Minh re-spun" for a null row. The chip is hidden under hard mode and absent when the picks could not be resolved on this data.
- **Summary.** "Head-to-head · vs Minh": both XIs on two pitches with points, tier and record, the verification line, and a verdict, `⚔️ Beat Minh by 4 points: 74 to 70`, `⚔️ Minh wins by 1 point: 90 to 89`, `⚔️ Level with Minh on 90, fewer defeats`, `⚔️ Dead level with Minh: 90 points each`, which also goes into `extraLines` of the share text. "Challenge a friend" (any quick or daily result, since quick mode keeps `game.draft` after the season) packs this game with the manager name from the Profile page (the team name when there is none), copies the link and shows it in a field; "Run it back" starts the same wheel at once with this result as the ghost named "your ghost", verified without a replay because it is this device's own record; "Draft again" drops the challenge with the seed. The leaderboard entry stores `challenge: { name, points }` and the Profile page shows "vs Minh 70" beside it, green when beaten.
- **Best on this wheel.** The Profile page groups the quick and Daily entries of the leaderboard by seed: with two or more runs on one seed the best carries a gold "Best on this wheel · N runs" chip and the others "2nd of N on this wheel". Season and Dynasty entries are never grouped: a dynasty writes one entry per year under one seed, which is not the same wheel twice.

Links break on an engine bump (`ENGINE_VERSION`) or a data rebuild, by design: the stamps make that an "unverified" banner instead of a silent mismatch.

## 6. The player model

A **player-season** is the unit the wheel drafts: one person at one club in one season, with the rating he earned that year (`src/engine/types.ts`, `PlayerSeason`). Its id is `${playerId}@${season}@${clubId}`, so a mid-season move produces two drafts of the same human, of which you may take only one.

Fields: name, nationality, age, club, season, up to three natural positions (primary first), thirteen attributes (eight classic, five depth), four hidden traits, overall, value (GBP millions, today's money), weekly wage (GBP thousands), preferred foot, height, and accolades (`Champion` when his club won that league).

### Positions and attributes

Ten positions: GK, CB, LB, RB, DM, CM, AM, LW, RW, ST, grouped as GK, DEF, MID, ATT.

Eight attributes on 1 to 99: pace, shooting, passing, dribbling, defending, physical, mentality (decision making, composure and leadership rolled together), goalkeeping (only meaningful for keepers). They decide ratings, roles and fit.

Five depth attributes on 1 to 99, the Football Manager layer (`src/engine/depth.ts`): **stamina** (condition lost per match, the fade after the 70th minute, who comes off, the injury roll when tired), **strength** and **jumping** (set pieces and the aerial mismatch between one side's attack and the other's defence), **aggression** (cards, a little bite in the press) and **work rate** (how well pressing and counters run, how fast a player learns the system). They never enter `overall` or a role rating, so a 78 is still a 78; they change what happens to him over ninety minutes and a season. The data packs carry them (docs/DATA.md); an older pack has them derived from the eight: stamina from physical and age, strength from physical and height, aggression from defending and mentality, jumping from height and physical, work rate from mentality.

Four hidden traits in 0 to 1: consistency, injury proneness, big-match temperament, professionalism. They are never shown as numbers: the attribute panel draws big match, consistency and professionalism as a bar with a word (low, average, high) and injury proneness as a 1 to 5 **Injury risk** (`ceil(proneness × 5)`: robust, sturdy, average, fragile, glass). They shape variance, injuries, big games, discipline, morale and development.

The attribute panel (`src/ui/components/AttributePanel.tsx`, in the Dugout's player modal and Explore's career view) groups them FM-style: Technical (shooting, passing, dribbling), Physical (pace, stamina, strength, jumping), Mental (mentality, aggression, work rate, big match, consistency, professionalism), Defending, Goalkeeping, Fitness (injury risk and, for a squad player, condition). The squad list has a per-browser toggle that adds each player's stamina as a chip.

### Position weights and fit

`POSITION_WEIGHTS` gives each position a relative attribute mix (for example ST: shooting 0.38, pace 0.18, physical 0.16, dribbling 0.14, mentality 0.14). `overall` is the weighted mean at the primary position; in the shipped data it is the source rating from the dataset, with the weighted mean only as a fallback.

Out of position, a multiplier applies. Natural is 1.0. Anything not in this table is **unsuited** at 0.62, and any outfield player in goal (or keeper outfield) is 0.35.

| --- | --- |
| CB | DM 0.88, LB 0.86, RB 0.86 |
| LB | RB 0.90, CB 0.86, LW 0.84, DM 0.82 |
| RB | LB 0.90, CB 0.86, RW 0.84, DM 0.82 |
| DM | CM 0.93, CB 0.86, LB 0.80, RB 0.80 |
| CM | DM 0.92, AM 0.90, LW 0.80, RW 0.80 |

Fit bands: **natural** 1.0, **accomplished** ≥ 0.86, **awkward** ≥ 0.75, **unsuited** below. The draft requires at least accomplished; a player with several natural positions uses the best factor among them.

### Roles and the rating formula

Thirty-four roles (`src/engine/roles.ts`), each with its own attribute weights, a split of the player's quality between the team's units, and flat tactical effects. The rating of a player in a role at a slot is:

```
roleRating = (0.55 × overall + 0.45 × roleWeightedMean) × positionFactor
```

The 55/45 blend anchors to the rating people recognise while letting the role decide who suits the job.

### Live modifiers

In Season and Dynasty each squad player carries live state (`SquadPlayer`): condition, morale, sharpness, familiarity (all 0 to 100), the last six match ratings, injury, suspension, yellow cards and season statistics. The effective rating in a slot is `roleRating` multiplied by:

| Modifier | Formula | Range |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Condition | 1 at ≥ 85; `1 − (85 − c) × 0.003` down to 70; `0.955 − (70 − c) × 0.0045` below | about 0.66 at 5, 1.0 at 85+ |
| Morale | `1 + ((m − 65) / 35) × 0.04` | 0.937 to 1.04 |
| Sharpness | 1 at ≥ 75; `1 − (75 − s) × 0.0012` below | 0.934 to 1.0 |
| Familiarity | `0.95 + (f / 100) × 0.07` | 0.95 to 1.02 |
| Form | `1 + clamp((avg of last 5 ratings − 7) × 0.02, −0.03, +0.03)` | 0.97 to 1.03 |

Quick Draft and Daily pass no state, so all five are 1.

Starting state: drafted players begin at condition 96, morale 70, sharpness 70, familiarity 35. Signings arrive at familiarity 20 and sharpness 65. AI squads start at familiarity 70 and sharpness 85.

## 7. Team strength, chemistry and balance

`computeTeamStrength` (`src/engine/ratings.ts`) turns the eleven into four unit ratings.

1. Each slot's effective rating is split into GK, defence, midfield and attack by its role's contribution. Each unit's rating is the contribution-weighted mean of what flows into it.
2. **Numbers.** Defence, midfield and attack are each scaled by `0.88 + 0.12 × clamp(share / baseline, 0.5, 1.5)` against baselines of 4.0, 3.2 and 2.8 contribution shares, so a shape that starves a unit pays for it and one that floods it gains a little.
3. **Fill.** Units are multiplied by `filled slots / 11`; a missing goalkeeper multiplies goalkeeping by 0.4.
4. **Chemistry** is added to every unit. Over the 55 pairs in the eleven: +1 for two players from the same club-season, +0.4 for the same club within two seasons, +0.2 for a shared nationality (unknown nationality excluded). `linkPoints = min(1.5, score / 55 × 5)`. Average familiarity with the system adds `((avgFam − 50) / 50) × 1.2`, so chemistry runs from −1.2 to +2.7 rating points. The Dugout lists the top three club-season links.
5. **Balance** is `1 − (max unit − min unit) / 35`, clamped to 0 to 1.
6. **Overall** is `(0.18 GK + 0.30 DEF + 0.26 MID + 0.26 ATT) × (0.96 + 0.04 × balance)`.

`autoPickLineup` fills the hardest slots first (GK, then by scarcity of natural players), in two passes (fit ≥ 0.86, then anything), scoring `roleRating × condition × sharpness`, and benches the next nine by overall. It drives AI clubs, the Auto-pick button, the start of each season (the strongest eleven regardless of where the draft put people) and lineup repair before kick-off.

### The slot picker

Apart from Auto-pick, the Dugout has one way to change the XI: tap a pitch slot and a picker opens (`SlotSheet` in `src/ui/screens/Dugout.tsx`, built on `Modal` with `sheet`: on screens of 720px or narrower a bottom sheet docked to the bottom edge, 80vh at most, rounded 16px at the top and sliding up in 0.32 s, as every phone sheet does, unless reduced motion is set; wider, an ordinary centred modal). It replaced the old two-tap swap (tap a slot, then a player or a second slot) on every screen size; tapping a player in the squad list or the depth chart now always opens his details. Its contents come from the pure `slotSheet(game, u, slotId)` (`src/game/logic/slotSheet.ts`):

- **On top**, the man in the slot with a **To bench** button, or a note that the slot is empty.
- **Can play there**: every other squad player with `canCover(p, position, 'accomplished')`, a position factor of at least `ACCOMPLISHED_FACTOR` (0.86), which is exactly the line `autoPickLineup` draws for its first pass (the literal 0.86 there and in `describeFit` is now that one constant). Sorted by `ratingAt(p, position)` (attributes weighted for the position, times the fit factor), each row has the state bars, a fit chip (fits, acc.), his whereabouts (`Starting at CM`, `Bench`, `Unused`) and on the right the number his disc would show there, `discRating` = `round(overall × positionFactor)`, the same function the pitch now uses.
- **Out of position**, folded by default: everyone else (awkward or unsuited, the pitch's awk.), with the penalised numbers.
- Injured or suspended men (`isAvailable` false, the filter auto-pick applies and the two cases `lineupProblems` reports) are dimmed and cannot be tapped, so the sheet never offers a man the pre-match check would then refuse.

Tapping a row calls `store.playInSlot(id, slotId)`: `assignPlayer` as before (a man from the bench or unused pushes the occupant to the bench; a man starting elsewhere swaps slots with him, which is the old two-tap swap), then a toast worded by `assignmentNote` from the lineup as it stood: `Saka in at RW, Bowen to the bench`, `Grealish in at RW, Salah to LW`, `Saka in at RW` into an empty slot, `Saka to the bench, RW is empty` from To bench. Names are surnames with their particles (`De Bruyne`, `van Dijk`); a position the shape repeats reads `left CB`, `right CB` or `left`, `centre`, `right` for three (`slotLabel`). The squad list's chip for a man neither starting nor on the bench now reads **Unused** rather than Out, so it is not read as injured.

In the Draft, a player with more than one eligible open slot no longer shows a "Choose a slot on the pitch" notice: the notice carries one chip per slot from `draftSlotChoices(d, p)`, which is exactly `eligibleSlots(d, p)`, reading `left CM 77 · fits` or `RW 73 · can cover` (`round(ratingAt)` in that position, `?` under hard mode or a blind spin), and tapping one makes the pick; the eligible slots on the pitch still pulse as a second way in. On screens of 720px or narrower the squad panel scrolls into view (`scrollIntoView`, smooth, to the top) each time a spin is revealed, since the stacked columns put it under the wheel.

## 8. The match engine

`simulateMatch` (`src/engine/match.ts`) produces expected goals per side, draws goals from a Poisson distribution and then dresses the result with a minute-by-minute report. It is played in three segments with two stops (1-45, 46-70, 71-90): `beginMatch` plays the first, `advanceMatch` the next two, `finishMatch` whatever is left plus the report, and `simulateMatch` is exactly `beginMatch → advanceMatch(70) → finishMatch`, so a week the assistant sims and a week played by hand run one path (a test holds them identical event for event, through a JSON round trip of the paused state). See "The 70th-minute window" below for the segment shares.

### Expected goals

```
BASE_XG = 1.32
K       = 0.055
stretch(x)      = x + max(0, x − 80) × 0.8
attackPower(s)  = stretch(0.62 × attack + 0.38 × midfield)
defencePower(s) = stretch(0.55 × defence + 0.28 × goalkeeping + 0.17 × midfield)

λ_home = BASE_XG × exp(K × (attackPower(home) − defencePower(away)))
         × home.tactical.attack × away.tactical.defence × 1.12 × homeTeamTalk
λ_away = BASE_XG × exp(K × (attackPower(away) − defencePower(home)))
         × away.tactical.attack × home.tactical.defence × 0.90 × awayTeamTalk
```

Neutral venues (cup final, friendlies) use 1.0 for both. The stretch means the gap between a 90 and an 87 counts for far more than the gap between a 72 and a 69, so an all-time XI feels like one. Training and one-off event boosts multiply λ next (section 10).

Then:

- **Big matches.** When importance > 1, each side's λ is multiplied by `1 + (avgBigMatch − 0.5) × 0.14 × (importance − 1) × 2.5`. Importance is 1.4 for a cup final, 1.3 for a title decider (matchweek 28 onwards, you in the top two, them in the top three), 1.25 for a derby or cup semi-final, 1.1 for any other cup tie, 0.9 for a friendly.
- **Variance.** `λ × exp(N(0, σ))` with `σ = 0.06 + 0.12 × (1 − avgConsistency)`. A metronomic side swings about 6 per cent; a flaky one about 18.
- **In the air.** A player's aerial power is `(strength + jumping) / 2`. Each side's λ is multiplied by `1 + 0.04 × clamp((attackAerial − oppDefenceAerial) / 8, −1, 1)`: the attacking side's power weighted by each man's attack share (plus 0.3 so everyone counts) against the defending side's weighted by defence share, so a mismatch of eight points or more moves expected goals by 4 per cent each way (`aerialOf`, `mismatchFactor`). On top, every side gets a set-piece term from its three most powerful outfielders on the pitch: `((top3 aerial − 72) / 100) × 0.04`, clamped −0.03..+0.04 (`aerialBonus`), beside the designated takers' bonus below. Measured on a mid-table XI with the same seed: all men at 90 strength and jumping against all at 55 gives ×1.0475 and ×0.9535.
- **Legs.** After the 70th minute every starter still on has his effective rating cut by `0.10 × legs(stamina)`, `legs = clamp((75 − stamina) / 50, 0, 1)` (nothing at 75 and above, 5 per cent at 50, 10 at 25); the side's fade is the mean cut over the eleven on the pitch, substitutes and the goalkeeper counting as fresh, and the 71-90 segment's λ is multiplied by `(1 − ownFade) × (1 + oppFade)` for each side (`applyLateFade`). Over 600 seeded matches a side at stamina 35 scores 23.7 per cent of its goals after 70 against 24.0 for the same men at 90. Keepers are exempt from every legs effect (the fade, the stamina factor on condition loss and the tired-legs injury term) because their stamina in the data reads low by convention (36 on average in the Premier League pack against 67 for outfielders): he does not run, so the number says nothing about his last twenty minutes.
- **Short-handed.** Minutes played with fewer than eleven (red cards, injuries with no replacement) reduce own attack by up to 30 per cent and lift the opponent's by the same.
- λ is clamped to 0.12 to 5.8. Goals are `poisson(λ)`. Shots are `max(goals, poisson(7.5 λ))`, on target `max(goals, min(shots, poisson(3 λ)))`.
- Possession: `50 + 1.1 × (home midfield − away midfield) ± 4 per short or direct passing side + 2 for home + N(0, 3)`, clamped 28 to 72.

### Team talks

Chosen before each match (default Encourage). Effects are a multiplier on your own λ with a line in the report:

| Talk | Outcome |
| --- | --- |
| Calm | ×1.02 if you are underdogs by more than 3 rating points or on a 55% roll, else ×0.99 |
| Encourage | ×1.025 on a 60% roll, else ×1.0 |
| Demand | Favourites by more than 4: ×1.03 (60%) or ×0.985. Otherwise ×1.02 (35%) or ×0.975 |
| Fire up | Professional squad (avg professionalism > 0.6): ×1.035 (50%) or ×0.98. Otherwise ×1.03 (60%) or ×0.97 |

### Discipline

Per outfield player, probability of a yellow card over the match: `(0.07 + 0.09 × defensive share + 0.02 if physical > 82) × pressing (1.25 high, 0.9 low) × 1.2 if derby (importance ≥ 1.2) × (1.15 − 0.3 × professionalism) × (0.7 + 0.6 × aggression / 100)`, rolled once per segment at the segment's share of the match (`(to − from + 1) / 90`: 0.5, 0.278, 0.222). A booked player has a `0.09 × share × aggression factor` chance of a second yellow in each later segment. Straight red: `(0.004 + 0.004 × defensive share) × derby factor × share × aggression factor`. The aggression factor runs from 0.7 for the meekest to 1.3 for the most combative; over 500 seeded matches a side with every man at 99 collects 1.65 times the cards of the same side at 10 (737 against 447), and because outfield aggression averages about 68 the engine's card rate rose 15 per cent when it arrived (2.81 a match over the pinned 2,000-match rotation, from 2.45). A booking is stamped with its minute, so a second yellow, a straight red or a substitution for that player never comes earlier than the booking. A sent-off player stays in his slot for the second-half numbers (the man disadvantage is costed once, by the short-handed factor) and still counts as having been on the pitch before his dismissal. Suspensions count down one match for every match the player's side plays without him, for your squad and for every AI club.

### Injuries

Per player not sent off: `0.021 × (0.5 + injuryProneness) × conditionFactor × fatigueFactor × share` per segment, where condition below 55 doubles the risk, below 70 is ×1.45 and below 80 is ×1.1, and `fatigueFactor = 1 + 0.3 × clamp((70 − stamina) / 40, 0, 1) × clamp((80 − condition) / 40, 0, 1)`: tired legs on an outfielder with little stamina add up to 30 per cent (stamina 30 or less at condition 40 or less), fresh legs, a durable player or a keeper add nothing. Over 1,500 seeded matches at condition 40 a side at stamina 30 suffered 636 injuries to 490 for the same men at 90; at condition 95 the two are level (245 to 249). Injury proneness is shown as the 1 to 5 Injury risk. Severity:

| Roll | Injury | Weeks out |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 55% | Knock, dead leg, tight hamstring, bruised ribs, twisted ankle | 1 to 2 |
| 30% | Hamstring, calf or groin strain, sprained ankle, hip problem | 3 to 5 |
| 12% | Torn hamstring, knee ligament damage, broken foot, shoulder dislocation | 6 to 10 |
| 3% | Ruptured ACL, broken leg, Achilles rupture | 12 to 24 |

Injured players are replaced from the bench if a fit, unsuspended substitute exists (scored by `overall × positionFactor × (condition / 100 + 0.5)`), otherwise the side finishes short.

### Substitutions, goals and ratings

- Up to five substitutions; up to three routine changes around the 68th minute (σ 9, between 50 and 88), drawn once at the restart and tiredness-driven: a starter wants off if `condition − 8 (when tactical fatigue > 4) − 8 (if aged 33+) + 0.3 × (stamina − 65) + N(0, 6)` is below 72, or on falling odds of 75, 55 and 30 per cent for the first three candidates, the candidates ordered by that score so the low-stamina men go first (over 300 seeded matches with half the XI at stamina 40 and half at 90, the 40s came off 631 times to the 90s' 246). A change due after the 70th minute is decided then (the replacement is chosen and reserved) but parked in `pendingSubs` and made when the last segment is played, so the pitch at the 70' stop is the one the manager sees; a red card or injury to the man coming off, or a used substitute, cancels it.
- Each goal picks a minute (bias to late in each segment; a fifth of the last segment's goals fall in stoppage time, about 4.7 per cent of all goals, as 9 per cent of a whole second half's did), then: 2.5 per cent own goal by a defender; 9 per cent penalty, taken by the best shooting plus mentality; otherwise a scorer weighted by `(attack share × 1.6 + 0.08) × (shooting / 100)^2.2`, with an assist 78 per cent of the time weighted by midfield and attack share, passing squared and role creativity.
- Keepers are credited with a save only for a chance the log records as saved; a summary match without a log tallies saves at three of every eight chances, so AI keepers' ratings are comparable.
- Player match ratings start at 6.6, move 0.22 per goal of margin (capped ±0.8), +0.9 per goal, +0.5 per assist, +0.5 for a clean sheet or up to −0.6 for goals conceded (defenders, DM and GK), GK saves +0.12 each (max +0.9), `(effective rating − 75) / 100 × 1.2` for quality, Gaussian noise scaled by `0.45 × (1.1 − 0.7 × consistency)`, −1.2 for a red, −0.15 for a yellow, damped for cameos under 30 minutes, clamped 4.5 to 10. Man of the match is the highest rating with at least 20 minutes.
- Condition lost per player: `(26 + tactical fatigue + 5 if 33+ or 2 if 30+ + (100 − physical) / 10 + 2 if fast tempo) × (1.25 − 0.5 × stamina / 100) × minutes / 90`: the stamina factor is 1.0 at 50, 0.8 at 90 and 1.1 at 30 (a keeper always takes 1.0), so a side at stamina 35 loses nearly a third more than the same men at 90 (365 against 283 points over the XI per match in the seeded test).

The match screen plays the match on the match-day dashboard (see "The match-day dashboard" below), then shows the report: scoreboard, goals, the momentum bar and the xG graph story, the shot map, possession and territory, xG (per attempt), possession, shots (on target) and the expected goals before kick-off, all four the dashboard's own numbers at the whistle, team ratings, tactical notes with the attack and defence multipliers, the half-time talk and the shout as they landed, player ratings with rating bars for both sides, substitutions, both sides' shapes with their instructions and the opponent's unit ratings.

### Watch, Auto-play and Sim result

`GameSettings.matchPlay` (optional; unset reads as `'watch'`, so every save written before it plays as before; Season and Dynasty only: `matchPlayOf` returns `'watch'` for Quick Draft and the Daily) is how the Dugout's play button plays the next match, changed in one tap on the Dugout's "Match:" chips. The store's `playWeek` calls `playMatchweekAs` (`src/game/logic/assistant.ts`):

- **Watch**: the match stops at half time and at 70 minutes and the dashboard's stop panel (`StopPanel.tsx`, test ids `half-time-panel` and `late-panel`) opens as a pop-up (`DecisionSheet`: a dialog centred over the board on every width, phone included, over a light scrim, at most 86 per cent of the window high and scrolling inside itself with its action row always visible; focus moves in and back; Escape or ✕ minimises it to a "Decisions" pill and the match stays paused). It holds the talk or shout, the four instructions and the substitutions (the top bar's drawers edit the same pending changes), and five actions. The substitutions list the stop's pending changes, each with Undo, and "Make a change" (`stop-make-change`) turns the pop-up into the same substitution board as the top bar's sheet (see Substitutions below), inline: drag, tap or key a substitute onto a starter, then "Make changes" sends the staged list through the hand's `submit` into the stop's pending changes, exactly as the sheet does at a stop, and the pop-up comes back with them listed; Back or Escape leaves without them. On a desktop the board sits inside the pop-up with no scroll (the sheet widens to 940 px and the pitch takes what its 86 per cent, 720 px cap leaves); on a phone it is full height. Changes at the break still spend no stoppage. The actions: Play on (your changes), No changes, Let the assistant decide (`assistantCall`), Finish the match (your changes now, the assistant takes the later stop and the match plays on the pitch to the whistle) and Full sim (your changes now, then straight to the whistle and the report). While watching, the top bar's "Auto from here" hands the stops still to come to the assistant and "Sim result" (while a stop is still to come; in the last twenty minutes "Skip to full time" does the same) finishes the match with him at once. From 70 the assistant's call, like yours, holds the last twenty minutes live (`assistantAdvance(true)`) so they play on the pitch to the whistle.
- **Auto-play**: the whole match is watched, and each stop is answered by the assistant 0.7 s after it is reached, with no pop-up (a note says the assistant is making the calls; the wait is keyed on the stop and the switch, so a re-render never restarts it). A match reloaded mid-way under Auto-play carries on with the assistant (`assistantTakesStops`, read when the save loads); a cup tie that follows the league match goes back to the saved preference, whatever "Finish the match" or "Auto from here" asked for the league match.
- **Sim result**: the match is played to the whistle at once with the assistant at both stops (`simLiveMatches`, the user's cup or European tie that follows too) and the screen opens on the report.

`assistantCall(game, u)` is pure and draws nothing. At half time: a talk from the score and the XI's mean morale (calm when ahead, encourage when level, fire up when behind with morale at 65 or more, encourage otherwise), and a change only for a man whose estimated condition is below 50 (a man injured in play has already been replaced by the engine when a fit substitute was free, so the assistant never sees him on the pitch). At 70: a shout from the score (all-out attack when behind, shut up shop one or two goals up, keep shape when level or three up) and changes for the injured and the tired, estimated condition below 65 (his kick-off condition less the engine's own cost per ninety for his minutes: age, physique, tempo, the side's fatigue and stamina), the most tired first; a keeper is never taken off for tiredness. Each is replaced by the best man on the bench for the slot: fit and unsuspended, not yet used in the match or this call, a natural fit before anyone else, then by rating in the slot times condition; never more than the changes left (three at the break, five in all). Instructions are left alone, because a change there would be written into the saved tactics. The calls go through `advanceLiveMatch`, the path a human's take, so the engine sees nothing new: Sim result and Auto-play give the same result, squad and generator state for the same save (tested over 35 matchweeks), and Watch with "No changes" at both stops reproduces results pinned before this change (six seeds, eight matchweeks, the final generator state included). `ENGINE_VERSION` did not change. Switching to a parked game, or starting a new one, sets the assistant switch from that game's own preference, never from the last game's. A live league match followed by a cup tie now shows the league result (the 71-95 replay) before the tie, which it skipped before.

### The 70th-minute window and touchline shouts

A live match stops twice: at half time and at the 70th minute. `MatchInProgress.clock` is 45, 70 or 90 (a paused match saved before the window existed has no clock and reads as 45, so it resumes at half time). `advanceMatch(mp, lookups, changes, to)` plays from the stop to 70 or to 90:

- **Segment shares.** Each segment takes `(to − from + 1) / 90 × (0.94 in the first half, 1.06 in the second)` of the match's expected goals: 0.47 for 1-45, 0.294 for 46-70 and 0.236 for 71-90 (0.53 for the second half, as before; Poisson additivity keeps the full-match goal distribution exact). Cards and injuries take the plain minute share (0.5, 0.278, 0.222); the two-half engine hard-coded 0.5 per segment, which three segments would have inflated by half. Pinned on the old engine over 2,000 seeded matches (seed `window-baseline`): 3.982 goals, 2.449 cards and 0.3745 injuries a match; the three-segment engine measures 4.027, 2.446 and 0.3755, within 3 per cent, and the last twenty minutes carry 19 to 26 per cent of the cards.
- **At half time** (clock 45): the human side's talk, instructions and up to three substitutions, as before; the AI side two goals down goes attacking (pressing low → mid), two up drops from attacking to balanced.
- **At 70** (clock 70): the human side's instructions, whatever is left of the five substitutions (minute 70, reason `70th minute`) and one shout for the last twenty minutes. All-out attack multiplies own expected goals by 1.3 and the opponent's by 1.35 and adds 4 to the side's fatigue modifier; shut up shop is ×0.55 own and ×0.72 opponent; keep shape is ×1 both ways. The shout is read at 70 only, the talk at 45 only, and the multipliers apply to the 71-90 segment alone. The AI side trailing at 70 goes attacking with a fast tempo; one goal up it goes defensive with a deep line; level or two or more up it keeps what it has. Both rules live in `advanceMatch`, never in the UI, so a simmed match reacts the same way. If the manager makes a substitution at 70, his side's parked routine changes are dropped; the AI's always go ahead.
- Strengths, modifiers and man-marking are recomputed at both stops, and the substitutions the engine made at a stop are written back into the lineup and the bench (only those: a man who has already played cannot come back on, so the panel at 70 does not offer the men taken off at the break, and a change the engine refuses never reaches the lineup). The report's `halfTimeEffect` and `lateEffect` carry the two interventions' text.
- The store action is `advanceLive(changes)`; `advanceLiveMatch` in `week.ts` plays to 70 and returns with `game.live` intact, or finishes the match and carries the week on. `finishLiveMatch` remains as "play to the whistle": the changes apply at the current stop and the side plays on unchanged. Because every match now consumes the stream differently, `ENGINE_VERSION` became `'3'`: challenge links built on `'2'` verify as unverified rather than failing. The depth attributes moved it to `'4'` (every λ change moves how many draws a Poisson takes), and the pinned means over the same 2,000-match rotation are now 4.122 goals, 2.812 cards and 0.3935 injuries a match (from 4.027, 2.446 and 0.3755); a seeded elite-against-mid match (`depth-pin`) is pinned at 1-0 with 25 events and 5 cards so any later change to the draws is noticed.

### Match detail and the pitch views

Each segment, once its goals, chances, cards and substitutions are decided, forks a stream off a copy of the generator (`Rng.fromState(rng.getState()).fork('detail-<from>')`) and fills in the detail the pitch views need (`src/engine/matchDetail.ts`). Because the fork is taken from a copy, the match's own stream, and the week's fixtures after it, never move: a pinned test (seed `detail-chances`, 100 matches) holds the goals, the chances and saves and the caller's final generator state to the engine without the detail, and the detail never moved `ENGINE_VERSION`. The pin was taken at 404 goals and 774 chances and saves on engine `'3'`; the depth attributes (engine `'4'`) moved it to 432 and 769, the same with the detail calls switched off, so it was re-pinned at those values.

- **Shot positions.** Every goal, penalty, own goal, chance and save carries `position: { x, y }`: x 0 to 100 along the pitch towards the goal the side attacks, y 0 to 100 across (105 by 68 metres, so the box edge is x 84.3 and the box spans y 20.4 to 79.6). Penalties are at the spot (89.5, 50). Own goals are at x N(96, 1.5) clamped 92 to 99. Headers, corners, tap-ins, six-yard and on-the-line efforts (read from the event text) are at x N(95, 2) clamped 90 to 99, y N(50, 7) clamped 30 to 70. Otherwise one effort in five (and every "edge of the box" one) is from outside the box, x N(79, 3.5) clamped 68 to 84, y N(50, 11) clamped 25 to 75; the rest are in the box, x N(90, 3.5) clamped 84.3 to 98.5, y N(50, 11) clamped 22 to 78. Over 150 seeded matches more than 60 per cent of shots are in the box and 10 to 30 per cent outside it.
- **Momentum.** `momentum` holds eighteen values, one per five-minute bin (1-5 to 86-90, stoppage time in the last), each the home side's share of the pressure, 0.08 to 0.92. A bin starts from `λh / (λh + λa)` for its segment (the expected goals the engine used, so tactical modifiers, shouts and a man down are already in it), adds the mentality and pressing lean (attacking +0.03, defensive −0.03, high press +0.015, low −0.015, home minus away), a chase term for the side behind (`0.05 × min(2, goal difference)`, ×1.2 from the 46th minute and ×1.6 from the 71st), ±0.12 for a goal in the bin and ±0.05 for one in the bin before, ±0.04 per chance or save in the bin and an N(0, 0.06) wobble, and then carries 35 per cent of the bin before. A match paused before the detail existed fills its missing bins with the base share.
- Both live in `MatchInProgress` (so a paused match carries the segments played) and in `FullMatchResult`. The team report adds `appearances` (each man's slot, minutes on and off, a red or an injury) and the final `tactics`, for the shape views. All fields are optional; older results simply show no shot map or pitch. Only a full-detail match (the user's league, cup, European and friendly matches) carries them: a summary match (the other fixtures of a week, every match of a quick season or a head-to-head) skips the detail, since nothing draws its pitch and every one is kept in the save's fixtures. With the detail on all 380 fixtures a season's save grew from about 9.8 to 14.2 KB per played fixture and passed the browser's 5 MB storage quota before the last matchweek; skipping it moves no result, because the detail never draws from the match stream. Since then the save also compacts every older stored result to its score (section 15).

The pitch views are pure UI (`src/ui/match/` and `src/ui/components/LivePitch.tsx`, `MatchCharts.tsx`):

- **Placement.** A slot's point is its formation coordinate, +4 along the pitch for an attacking mentality and −4 for a defensive one, the back line (slots below y 36) +7 for a high line and −6 for a deep one, wide slots (more than 22 from the centre) spread ×1.14 for wide and ×0.8 for narrow; the keeper never moves. The away side is mirrored through the centre spot. Opposing slots closer than six metres are pushed apart, and a substitute takes the point of the slot he fills.
- **The ball.** `buildBallPath` (`src/ui/match/possession.ts`) lays out keyframes on one continuous timeline from a seed of its own (`ball-<seed>-<from>`, never the game's generator), one stretch per spell of the match path (`epochs.ts`): a kick-off from the centre spot at 0 and 46, and every other spell (71, the minute after a change) carries on from where the last one ended (`start`, no keyframe of its own), then cycles of a carry (the man on the ball runs 1.5 to 5 units with it, mostly forwards, over 0.16 to 0.34 of a minute) and a pass, most passes 10 to 25 units (weight `exp(-((d - 16) / 13)^2)`, forward ×1.5, within 48 units), or an interception by the nearest opponent with probability `0.03 + 0.45 × (1 - share)²` from the momentum bin, so a side keeps the ball for a spell of six or seven passes at even pressure and far longer when it is on top (the momentum test averages twenty seeds). Every hop lasts its length over `PASS_SPEED` = 46 units a minute (at least 0.22), shots fly at 120, and nothing may land after the next shot's run-up, so the ball never covers half the pitch in a frame. Passes over 34 units are lofted (peak 0.55 to 0.8), a quarter of the 24-to-34 ones too, every hop bends by up to 8 per cent of its length, crosses into the box by 12 per cent and a keeper's kick after a save is a high ball. The k shots of a minute m start at m + (i + 0.55) / k and are then spread apart within their minutes (never before m + 0.05 or after m + 0.95; `strikeTimes`) so that the ball has time to get from one strike to the next: 0.35 of a minute for the restart plus the distance between the two shots at 70 units a minute, 0.3 plus the distance from the centre spot after a goal, and the first strike of a range leaves time for its kick-off. After a goal, when the scorers strike again within a minute and a half, the side kicking off does not play it about first; after a save or a miss the restart leaves the next move the time it needs; a hop squeezed to fit before the next strike may be at most 1.8 times its own speed or it does not happen, and a last ball into the shooter that still has to be quick goes long and in the air. Over 30 real matches no more than one hop in four matches is faster than 3.5 times the pass speed (the worst about 6×, against 15× before; tested). Then: a build-up of up to 1.8 minutes before, a turnover if needed, a pass towards the box when there is time, then the ball into the shooter's stride at the event position (he runs onto it); after it the net and a kick-off by the other side, the keeper's hands and a long kick, or wide of the post, fetched and a goal kick. `ballAt` moves the ball along a cubic Hermite curve through the keyframes (`smoothPoint`): the velocity through a keyframe is the mean of the hops in and out (no faster than twice the slower), shared by both, so the ball never stops dead at a touch nor turns on the spot; through a strike, a save, a restart or a kick-off each hop keeps its own; each hop adds its sideways bend. It reports its height `4·loft·f(1-f)` and the man in control (the carrier through a carry, the receiver as a pass arrives: someone is on the ball about 42 per cent of the time, against 3 before).
- **The football.** `BallArt` in `LivePitch.tsx`: a white sphere with a radial shading gradient, a gloss overlay and black pentagon patches drawn as a repeating tile inside a circular clip; the loop slides the tile the way the ball travels (55 per cent of the distance, at most 28 per cent of a tile a frame so it never strobes), so it rolls. In the air it rises (up to 4.2 SVG units) and grows (up to 1.4×) while its ground shadow shrinks and fades. Radius 1.5 SVG units lying down, 1.75 standing up on a phone, where bigger, fewer patches keep it reading as a football.
- **Motion.** One `requestAnimationFrame` loop reads the continuous clock (`timeline` in `src/ui/match/clockStore.ts`) and eases everything on screen towards its target, frame-rate independently (`approach`): the ball by 14 ms at 1x (38 at 2x, 85 at 4x, where a hop lasts a few frames and the ball rounds the path's corners instead), the players by 150 ms (up to 300 at 4x), so a change of possession, a kick-off, a substitution or a skip never makes anyone jump. Each man's target is the one movement formula the heat maps sample (`dotPosition`, `movement.ts`: the blocks lean with the ball, the nearest drift to it, everyone sways, the man on the ball has it and the receiver runs onto it, a keeper dives at a save), and what is on screen eases towards it, so nobody twitches with each pass; during a goal's celebration the scorer and his team-mates are pulled off it. A substitute walks on from the touchline by the halfway line (eased three times slower for his first 1.5 s). Dots are keyed by side and player-season (`dotKey`), because the same player-season can turn out for both sides when you drafted him from today's opponent: before, one of the two dots stood frozen all match and the other ran as your scorer. Where everything stood on the last frame drawn is kept per match (`frameFor`, the pitch seed), so the next segment (46, 71, the replay) and any re-render of the pitch carry on from there rather than snapping back to the formation; a pitch that starts earlier than the last one (watching again from the kick-off) starts afresh. A new segment that finds the ball far from its kick-off rolls it to the spot over 0.9 s (eased by 160 ms) rather than snapping it there. Time moves things only while the clock runs: stood at a stop or paused, a redraw moves nobody, and the first frame after a sleep takes no time step. Just past a goal's hold the ball is held on the centre spot for the kick-off. React renders only when the whole minute changes, a goal is revealed or a celebration starts (the clock's 40 ms timer sets the store only when one of them changes), never inside the loop; markings and nets are memoised and each dot keeps one ref callback. With prefers-reduced-motion the loop is off: the dots are still and the ball jumps from keyframe to keyframe.
- **The clock** (`useMatchClock.ts`, `clockStore.ts`). The dashboard owns a continuous anchor (`at` minute at real time `since`, speed, period, the goal holds) and the pitch and the scoreboard clock read a fractional minute from it every frame (`timeline`), so the ball never waits on a late timer at a minute boundary; a 40 ms timer only refreshes the whole minute React shows, the goals revealed and the celebration on. A stretch ends at the end of its stop minute (46, 71, or the minute after the last of stoppage time). 1x is a broadcast pace: a match minute takes 3,000 ms (`PITCH_PERIOD`; 110 ms for the bare ticker), so a 20-unit pass takes a little over a second, a carry half a second to a second and a half about two and a quarter minutes; 0.5x takes 6 s a minute, 2x 1.5 s, 3x 1 s and 4x 0.75 s. Pause, resume and speed changes re-anchor where the picture stands (`reanchor`), so nothing jumps at any speed; "Skip to half time", "Skip to 70 minutes" and "Skip to full time" jump the anchor to the end. The speed is a per-viewer convenience kept in `localStorage` under `touchline-match-speed` (read inside try/catch, 1x when missing or unreadable). When the tab is hidden the clock pauses (it runs on real time) and resumes when the tab is back, unless you had paused it yourself. A change in play re-decides the rest of the match: the new goal holds and whistle are taken in without moving the picture.
- **The goal.** When the clock reaches a goal's strike moment it holds (`goalHolds`): 2,800 ms for the user's goal, 1,700 ms for one against him, 2,600 when neither side is the user's, 1,500 ms with reduced motion. In real time during the hold: the ball flies from the shooter's foot into the net (520 ms, `GOAL_FLIGHT_MS`, a slight lift and curl); as it crosses the line the goal reaches the dashboard (the panels, the commentary and the scoreboard read the minute before the goal's until then, and the board flashes while it is celebrated: nothing shows the goal before the ball goes in), the net bulges with a damped wobble (`netBulge`), the pitch flashes white (0.42 opacity decaying over 170 ms, 0.16 for a goal against) and, for a goal of ours, shakes for 380 ms; the "GOAL!" banner over the pitch (GOAL! (PEN) and OWN GOAL! with the defender's name and "(og)") bursts out with the scorer, the minute and the new score in the scoring club's colour, with 42 pieces of confetti in that colour, white and a contrast colour; the scorer runs towards the corner flag with a pulsing ring in his colour and his team-mates converge on him (`celebrationEnvelope` rises over half a second and is back to nothing 150 ms before the end, so nobody jumps at the restart); the ball fades out of the net and back in on the centre spot, and play resumes with the kick-off. A goal against is short and subdued: a smaller, dimmer banner, a smaller bulge and flash, no shake and no confetti. Reduced motion: no flight, no shake, no confetti, a still banner for the 1,500 ms hold. All of it is presentation of events the engine already produced; nothing draws from any game generator.
- **Charts.** The momentum bar draws each bin up (home) or down (away) as the clock reaches it, with goal markers. The xG graph story steps each side's expected goals up at every attempt (per-attempt xG, "Match day data" below), with a marker on every goal. (The older xG race, `xgSeries`, shared the match xG over the ninety minutes by the momentum; it is kept with its tests but no longer drawn.) Territory on the report is the mean of the momentum series. The match card (`renderMatchCard` in `src/ui/shareCard.ts`) draws the score, the shot map and the xG story on a 1080 by 1350 canvas, its line on a 0..90 axis with stoppage-time attempts on the ninetieth minute.

### The match-day dashboard

The live match and the replay of a finished one play on one dashboard (`src/ui/matchday/`), built after Football Manager's match screen. The shell (`MatchDashboard.tsx`) builds the match feed (`feedFromLive` / `feedFromResult`) and, once per decision rather than per tick, everything derived from it: the epochs and ball path (`matchPath`), the stats series, the sampled dot positions for the maps, the xG story and the commentary. Per minute it works out the ratings, the round's other scores and the live table. Nothing here draws from the game's generator.

- **The clock** runs from the start of the segment the engine has decided (0, 46 or 71) to the stop (45, 70) or to the whistle (90 plus the added time), as described under "Match detail and the pitch views", with the goal holds of every spell. A reload resumes at the later of the segment's start and the manager's last change in play, so nothing shown before a change is played again with a different ending. At 70 "Play to the whistle" holds the last segment live (`advanceLive(changes, true)`); the whistle calls `completeLive()`, then the report shows. A cup tie that follows a league match is held back until the league report has been seen.
- **Layout at 1200 px and wider**: a slim line naming the competition and round; the top bar (the scoreboard with club codes on club colours, the score at the clock, the minute, "(+n)" once the board is up, HALF TIME, 70 MINUTES and FULL TIME at the stops and the whistle, a flash for a goal; Pause or Resume, 0.5x 1x 2x 3x 4x and "Skip to half time", "Skip to 70 minutes" or "Skip to full time"; in a live match also "Auto from here" and, while a stop is still to come, "Sim result"); three columns, `minmax(240px, 0.9fr) minmax(0, 2.6fr) minmax(240px, 0.9fr)`. Each side column holds a formation panel on top and a widget slot below that scrolls inside itself (L, R). The centre holds three widget slots (A, B, C), the live pitch flanked by both sides' stats and the momentum strip. Under them run the commentary banner and the squad strip. The board fits the window with no page scroll from 1280 by 720 to 2560 by 1440: the shell measures what stands above and below it and sets its height to `calc(100dvh − that)` (`--md-fit`, again on resize), the widget row takes `clamp(112px, 19vh, 236px)`, the strip, banner and top bar keep their size, and the pitch row takes the rest, the pitch sized by whichever of the row's width and height runs out first (a size container: `min(100cqw, 100cqh × 111 / 74)`). The side columns share that height: each formation pitch is sized by its room and the widget slot under it takes 40 per cent. In a short window (940 px high or less, 1280 by 720 and 2000 by 920 among them) the momentum strip gives its room to the pitch (it stays a widget), the widget row drops to `clamp(96px, 15vh, 160px)` and the squad cards slim down. Panels scroll inside themselves; the page never does, and the stop's pop-up and the substitutions sheet stay inside the window (checked at 1280 by 720, 1920 by 1080, 2000 by 980, 2000 by 920 and 2560 by 1440). At a stop the half-time or 70-minute decisions open as a pop-up over the board (see "Watch, Auto-play and Sim result"); the widget row stays.
- **Formation panels**: each XI on its own pitch standing up, attacking up for both sides, at its slots' points under the instructions in force: a shirt in club colours (the keeper in a contrasting kit) with the man's number, his live rating, the role code and surname; card marks, a sub arrow on a changed slot, and a greyed slot for a man sent off or injured with nobody to replace him. Outfield men stand no deeper than 80 per cent of the panel (`PANEL_DEEPEST`), so a back three on a deep line never sits on the keeper's shirt, and each name is capped at the gap to the nearest team-mate level with him (`labelRoom`), so two centre-halves' names never run into each other at 1280 px. Clicking a man (or his card in the strip) puts him in the heat map.
- **Stats flanks**: shots, on target, xG, distance covered, pass completion, corners, tackles, fouls, yellow cards and possession at the clock (`statsAt`), with "View stats" opening the full table in slot B.
- **Widgets**: every slot's header is a dropdown of every widget, remembered per browser under `touchline-matchday-widgets` (validated against the ids, never in the save). Defaults: A heat map, B match events, C xG graph story, L live table, R latest scores; a friendly puts matchday info in L and the dugout in R. Match events lists goals with assists, cards, injuries, substitutions, the whistles and every change of instructions, home left and away right. Commentary is the full feed to the clock. The heat map follows one man (both XIs and the substitutes in a picker) with a Low to High legend, attacking up. Average positions shows both sides or either. Pass maps join one side's men at their average positions, weighted by the passes between them on the ball path. Action zones gives one side's share of the ball by third and channel. The shot map sizes each attempt by its xG. Match momentum names the spells of two or more bins with one side at 60 per cent or more. Match stats adds passes attempted and completed, offsides, red cards and territory. Latest scores shows the round's other matches with their scores, the minute, HT or FT, the last scorer and penalties at FT in a knockout. The live table counts every match of the round at its current score, with movement arrows against the table before the round and a dot on the sides playing (a knockout tie shows the league table as it stands; a friendly has none). The dugout lists the bench (ready, on, unavailable), the changes made, subs and stoppages left, the talk and shout as they landed and the assistant's notes. Matchday info gives the competition and round, the venue, the expected goals before kick-off, both sides' unit ratings and their positions and form before the round, an earlier meeting this season and our captain.
- **The commentary banner** shows the latest line by priority (a goal over a card over an injury, a substitution, a change, a goal elsewhere, a chance, the whistles, the board and a line of play); the moments slide in and are announced politely to screen readers, the lines of play change quietly.
- **The squad strip**: our XI by starting slot with the position code (GK, DC, DR, WBL, DM, MC, AMC, AML, STC and the rest), the shirt and number, the surname, a condition heart that empties as the legs go (live only: a replay has no starting condition), the rating pill, a morale face (live only), card marks and a sub arrow with the minute. The Squad control before it counts subs left of five and, in play, stoppages left of three, and opens the substitutions drawer. Tapping a card puts the man in the heat map (and offers him first when making a change).
- **Under 1200 px** (`CompactLayout.tsx`, `PhoneTabs.tsx`): the pitch first (standing up on a phone at 720 px and narrower, lying down on a tablet and capped at 860 px wide), the commentary banner, a pulse row that is always on (the momentum strip and the xG graph story), then a tab strip (class `md-tabs`, a proper tablist: arrow keys, Home and End) with every panel: Events, Stats, Line-ups, Table, Scores, Heat map, Avg positions, Pass map, Shot map, Zones, Momentum, Dugout, Info and Commentary (a friendly has no Table or Scores). The tab is remembered per browser under `touchline-matchday-tab`, read and written inside try/catch and validated. A tablet shows the chosen panel and the one after it side by side. Our XI runs along the bottom as a scroll-snap sideways scroller; every sideways scroller sits in a box that may shrink, so the page itself never scrolls sideways. The top bar puts the scoreboard on its own row; the tools and playback wrap under it. The page scrolls on a phone; the stop's pop-up is centred in the window all the same, so it is in view without scrolling.
- **States**: a friendly (no table, no other scores), a cup tie (the league table frozen, the round's other ties), an old save (attempts without positions worth 0.31 each, no timeline lines), the replay (a "Replay" chip; everything moves the same). With prefers-reduced-motion nothing slides or flashes; the numbers still follow the clock.

### In-play changes

The manager can change the match at any minute, not only at the two stops. A literal split of a segment cannot keep a seed's match identical: `playSegment` draws each segment as totals (one Poisson goal count spread over the segment, one card and injury roll per player scaled by its length, at most four chances, the routine substitutions drawn once at 46, one detail fork), so stopping 46-70 at 58 would draw everything twice. Instead the engine decides ahead and cuts on change (`changeInPlay` in `src/engine/match.ts`):

- **Decide ahead.** The three canonical segments are played exactly as before, ahead of the clock. The dashboard's clock can stand anywhere; an untouched match never calls anything new, so it is identical to `simulateMatch` by construction, and a change that changes nothing is refused (`no-change`) before the match is touched. `ENGINE_VERSION` did not move for this: built on `'3'` it stayed `'3'`, and merged onto the depth attributes it stays `'4'`, since no existing draw moved (the 2,000-match window baseline, the detail pin, `{ goals: 404, chances: 774, state }` on `'3'` and `{ goals: 432, chances: 769, state }` on `'4'`, and the 40-seed stop identity test are unchanged, and a new test checks 40 seeds × ten minutes through JSON round trips).
- **The late fade and a cut.** The depth attributes' late fade (`applyLateFade`, the starters' legs going after 70 by stamina) is applied once, when `advanceMatch` plays 71-90: it cuts the effective ratings of the men on at 70 and moves both sides' expected goals for the segment. A full-detail match stores each side's fade on its snapshot (`TeamSnapshot.lateFadeAt70`, optional); a cut in 71-89 re-applies that stored value to the replay's expected goals (after the shouts, in the same order as the canonical call) and never cuts the legs again, so a man on since kick-off keeps the effective rating the fade left him however many changes follow (tested over 20 seeds and three cuts each). A man brought on in a cut is fresh, as one brought on at 70 is. A match held at 90 from before the field existed falls back to the fade computed on the pitch as it stands.
- **Cut on change at minute k.** The segment in play is rolled back to the end of minute k: every event after k is removed and undone (goals, assists, saves, cards, injuries, substitutions), except the half-time whistle and a substitution replacing a man whose injury at or before k stands; a routine change removed goes back into the parked changes at its minute. The manager's change is applied at k (instructions and set-piece takers; substitutions at k with reason `manager` and the text "Change on k minutes: X replaces Y."; a shout from 70), strengths, modifiers, marking and expected goals are recomputed by the same helper the stops use (with the shouts in force re-applied after 70), and k + 1 to the segment's end is replayed from `Rng.fromState(mp.rngState).fork('cut-<k>')`. The match's own stream never moves, so the next segment and the report draw what they would have; the momentum keeps every bin up to the one holding k; a first-half cut rewrites the half-time score. A cut is close to a fresh segment of the remaining length (Poisson additivity for goals, card and injury rates pro rata, the four-chance cap and a fifth of the last stretch's goals in stoppage time applying again; injuries fall in minutes 3 to 88 as always, so a cut at 88 or 89 replays none rather than landing one at or before the change), and it only touches matches the manager touched. The same change at the same minute always gives the same match.
- **Rules.** Full-detail matches only (`summary-match`); a save made before in-play changes has no `segment` and the change waits for the next stop (`no-segment`, with `nextStop`); no changes in stoppage time (`too-late`, minute 90 on); a minute outside the segment in play, the stops 45 and 70 included, belongs to `advanceMatch` (`outside-segment`); a minute before the side's last change is `in-the-past`. A shout before 70 is refused on its own (`shoutRefused`) while instructions with it still apply. A substitution is refused at minute 0 (`before-kick-off`: the team is picked before the match, so the starters stay the eleven who kick off), when the man coming off is not on the pitch at k or came on that same minute, the man coming on has already played or is injured or suspended, the side has made five changes (`MAX_SUBS`; every change counts, routine, injury and the manager's) or the manager has used his three windows (`MAX_WINDOWS`: a window is a minute other than 45 at which he made at least one change, the 70 stop included; half time is free; more changes at the same minute spend no new window). The instructions of a change apply even when its substitutions are refused. A manager's substitution drops his side's parked routine changes, as at 70. The AI reacts only at 45 and 70, never to an in-play change.
- **The last twenty minutes.** `advanceLiveMatch(game, u, changes, autoManage, hold)` with `hold` plays 71-90 into the paused match and keeps `game.live` (clock 90) so the manager can still change it; `completeLiveMatch` writes the report and carries the week on. Held and then completed, the game ends JSON-identical to the unheld path. `changeLiveMatch` makes a change for the user's side and writes the substitutions made into the lineup and bench and the instructions into `game.tactics`; the store actions are `advanceLive(changes, hold)`, `interveneLive(change)` and `completeLive()`. The match-day dashboard holds it: the 70th-minute panel plays on with `hold` and the whistle completes the match.
- **What is kept.** Full-detail matches carry `segment` (the canonical segment last played and the index of its first event), `timeline` (kick-off instructions for both sides and every change since: the manager's at the stops and in play, the AI's at the stops, with only the fields that changed, set-piece takers, the shout and the number of substitutions) and `seedKey` (read off the match stream at half time, never drawn), and the result keeps `timeline`, `seedKey` and `neutral` (when true). The result's timeline is compacted (`compactTimeline`): a side's kick-off entry keeps only the instructions a later entry changes, and goes when nothing is left, since the final tactics hold the rest; the match feed reads it back whole. `TeamSnapshot.shout` holds the shout in force from 71 so a later cut plays on under it. The timeline and the key come to well under half a kilobyte a result (about 250 bytes over 60 seeded matches, never 512); summary matches carry none of it. A whole season's save grows by about 0.2 per cent (11 KB on 5.8 MB in a 38-matchweek probe).

### In-play controls (the manager's side)

The top bar and the squad strip are the manager's hands on the match (`TopBar.tsx`, `drawers/Drawers.tsx`, `SquadStrip.tsx`, the pure helpers in `controls.ts`, the shared state in `useManagerHand.ts`).

- **The top bar.** On the left Tactics (the shape, each slot's position code, role and man, and the man-marking job: read-only, since formation and roles cannot change mid-match, the lineup being keyed by slot), Instructions (tempo, width and passing in possession; pressing and the defensive line out of it, with the usual help lines), a Mentality dropdown that applies the moment it changes, Shouts (all-out attack, shut up shop, keep shape: from the 70th minute only, "Touchline shouts open at 70 minutes" before) and Tweaks (penalty, free-kick and corner takers among the men on the pitch, or the best on the pitch). In the middle the scoreboard with a running mm:ss clock (the t-th minute plus its fraction reads (t − 1):ss, "44:07"; "(+n)" once the board is up; HALF TIME, 70 MINUTES, FULL TIME). On the right the playback.
- **Where a change lands** (the chip under the tools): "Changes apply now" (in play: one `interveneLive` at the clock's minute, applying from the next); "At the break: changes apply when you play on" and "At 70 minutes: …" (at a stop the drawers edit the stop panel's own pending changes, which the panel then sends); "Changes wait for half time" or "for the 70th minute" (a save from before changes in play, or a summary match: the change is queued into the next stop's pending changes and shows there when the stop comes); "Too late for changes" (stoppage time); "Replay" (a finished match: every control but Tactics is off).
- **Drawers.** Opening one pauses the clock and closing it resumes it if it was running (switching between drawers keeps the first one's choice). Apply sends everything changed as one change and closes. A drawer takes the focus, keeps Tab inside, closes on Escape or a click outside and gives the focus back to the control that opened it; on a phone it is a bottom sheet.
- **Substitutions.** The Squad control (and the dugout's "Make a change") opens the substitutions sheet, a substitution board (`drawers/SubBoard.tsx`, its pure model in `src/ui/matchday/subBoard.ts`): our XI on a small pitch in its formation slots (shirt number, surname, role code, condition) and the bench men who can still come on (`benchAt`) as cards beside it, under it on a phone, where the sheet is full height. Drag a bench card onto a starter (or a starter onto a bench card) to stage a change, with pointer events so a mouse, a pen and a finger all work; or tap one and then the other (the man selected on the strip starts picked); or use the keyboard (Enter or Space picks up, the arrows move to the nearest slot that way, or along the bench, Enter drops, Escape lets go), each step announced politely. While a man is held every slot glows for how he fits it, the slot picker's own split: green for his natural position, amber for accomplished cover (`canCover`), red for out of position, with his `roleRating` in that slot's role on it. Dropping a man on one already staged swaps the incoming man without spending another change; a man staged cannot be staged twice, and nobody who has played comes back on. The staged changes list as "on ↔ off" with Undo, and the board previews the team rating (`computeTeamStrength` of the lineup after them, the men's live condition read in) and, over a slot, both men's role ratings there; a save that hides ratings keeps the colours and hides the numbers. The sheet stages at most what the window takes (`subCap`: the subs left in play, the stop's room at 45 and 70) and nothing in play once the three stoppages are spent, unless a change was already made at that minute (its window is open); it shows the counters after the staged changes. "Make changes" sends the staged list, in one stoppage, through the same `submit` as before (`interveneLive` in play, the stop's pending changes at a stop), so the engine's rules and refusals are untouched. The counters are the engine's: subs left `MAX_SUBS − subsMade` up to the clock (every change counts, the assistant's routine ones and injury replacements too, each from its minute: a routine change the engine has decided for the 62nd minute is not spent at 50, because a cut at 50 would roll it back; the strip and the drawer count the same ones), stoppages left `MAX_WINDOWS − managerWindowsUsed` (a minute other than 45 at which the manager made a change; half time is free, up to three changes at the break as before). A fourth stoppage, a sixth change, a man who has already played, a substitution at kick-off and the rest of the engine's refusals come back as a toast naming the reason; a change made is confirmed with a toast and shows on the pitch, in the strip and in Match events from its minute.

### Match day data

Everything the match-day dashboard shows is derived from one feed (`feedFromLive` / `feedFromResult`, `src/engine/matchFeed.ts`: the decided events sorted, the momentum, the timeline, both sides with everyone who has appeared, and the key) without touching any generator; anything random comes from a stream keyed by the match and the minute.

- **Added time.** Two minutes, plus one for every three goals, cards, injuries and substitutions in the second half, at most six, and always enough to reach the last event; the first half has none. The full-time whistle in the feed stands at 90 plus that.
- **Tactics at a minute.** Formation and roles as the side stands; instructions and set-piece takers from the kick-off entry and every later entry before the minute (an entry at k applies from k + 1). A result without a timeline reads its final tactics throughout.
- **Shots and xG** (`src/engine/matchStats.ts`). Shots are the side's goal, penalty, chance and save events; on target goal, penalty and save; own goals count for the score, never as a shot. xG per attempt: a penalty 0.76, an own goal 0, an attempt without a position (an older save) 0.31, otherwise `min(0.92, 1.55 × q0)` with `q0 = 1 / (1 + exp(1.1 + 0.11 d − 1.6 θ))` from the distance d in metres and the angle θ the goal mouth subtends, × 0.65 for a header. The constant is fitted on 400 seeded full matches (seed `xg-calibration`): 1.545, pinned at 1.55, gives 1.870 xG a side against 1.865 goals (own goals aside) and 1.892 pre-match expected goals. A 600-match probe found 5.8 attempts and 3.5 on target a side, 53 per cent of attempts close in, 28 in the box, 16 outside and 3 penalties; sharing each segment's expected goals over its attempts was rejected because 37.7 per cent of side-segments log none.
- **Possession and territory.** The base is the engine's possession formula without its noise: `clamp(50 + (home midfield − away midfield) × 1.1 + passing (short +4, direct −4, home minus away) + 2 at home, 28, 72)`. The home share of minute m is `clamp(base + 35 × (momentum − 0.5), 20, 80)`, and possession to t is the mean over minutes 1..t. Territory to t is the mean momentum over the minutes played, so at the whistle it is the report's territory.
- **Modelled per minute** from `new Rng('stats-<key>-<minute>')`, home then away: passes `Poisson(10.4 × share × tempo (slow 1.12, fast 0.9) × passing (short 1.15, direct 0.8))`, each completed with `clamp(0.8 + (mean outfield passing − 72) × 0.006 + short 0.04 / direct −0.07 + slow 0.015 / fast −0.025 − their high press 0.03 / low −0.02, 0.55, 0.94)`; tackles `Poisson(0.2 × opponent share / 0.5 × press (high 1.3, low 0.78) × defending factor)`; fouls one per card and per penalty given away plus `Poisson(0.1 × press (high 1.2, low 0.85) × aggression × 1.15 in a derby × √(opponent share / 0.5))`; corners one per attempt from a corner plus `Poisson(0.05 × (pressure / 0.5)^1.5 × mentality × width)`; offsides `Poisson(0.022 × their line (high 1.6, deep 0.6) × tempo × passing × pressure / 0.5)`; distance 0.055 km a minute for the keeper and `0.119 × work rate × press × tempo × late fade × (1 + N(0, 0.03))` for each outfield man (stamina, work rate and aggression come from the data when it has them, else from physical, mentality and professionalism). A man coming on at k plays from k + 1 and the man he replaced plays minute k, so a minute's numbers depend only on that minute and a change at k leaves every minute up to k as it was shown. Over 200 even seeded matches a side averages about 520 passes at 79 per cent, 16 tackles, 12 fouls, 5 corners, 2.2 offsides and 104 km. The foul and distance bases were 0.115 and 0.113 while the data had no depth attributes (the proxies stood in); with the real aggression, work rate and stamina of format 3 packs (and engine `'4'`'s extra cards) they read 13.9 fouls and 99 km, so both were retuned on the merge to keep the per-ninety ranges. These numbers come from keyed streams of their own and never touch a match's draws.
- **Ratings and condition** (`src/engine/liveRatings.ts`). In play, the engine's rating formula on the events so far without its noise (the clean-sheet bonus pro rata, a substitute under thirty minutes damped); over 200 matches the whistle's live figures sit 0.24 on average from the engine's. A replay leans each man towards his final rating by his share of his own minutes, so at his last minute it is exactly the report's. Condition is the starting condition less the engine's condition loss pro rata, equal to it at the whistle.
- **The rest of the round** (`src/game/logic/matchday.ts`). The other fixtures are decided before the user's match is shown; `latestScores` reveals each by its own goal minutes at the user's clock (HT while the user's match stands at the break, everything and penalties once it reaches its whistle) and `liveTable` counts every match of the round from its first minute at its current score, the user's at his, with arrows against the table before the round. A knockout round shows its other ties and the league table as it stands; a friendly has neither.
- **The pitch's spells** (`src/ui/match/epochs.ts`). One placement per spell of unchanged instructions (kick-off, 46, 71 and the minute after every change) and one ball path through them, each spell's ball carrying on from where the last left it (the second half kicks off from the centre spot). Heat maps, average positions, pass networks and action zones (`heat.ts`) sample the same path through the same dot formula the live pitch draws (`movement.ts`). The commentary (`commentary.ts`) uses every event's text, the timeline's changes in words, goals elsewhere, the added-time board and a line of play for each quiet minute from that minute's passes, its template picked by a hash of the key and the minute. Shirt numbers (`shirts.ts`) are classic by position (keeper 1, right back 2, the first striker 9, the bench from 12), roles carry short codes of up to three letters (CF, DLP, BPD) and slots Football Manager position codes (GK, DC, WBL, DM, MC, AMC, ML, AML, STC); a keeper wears the first of gold, purple, black and orange that stands apart from both kits.

## 9. Tactics and instructions

On the Tactics tab each shape is a tile with its mini pitch (`FormationPicker`), each instruction is a label and its control with what the current setting does behind an info button, and each role row shows the rating in role as a badge with the role's blurb behind an info button.

## 10. Season systems

### Setup

Your club replaces the host club in the chosen real season. The other nineteen are built from their real squads, each with live state so they too suffer injuries and suspensions. Fixtures are a double round robin (circle method, rounds shuffled by the seed): 38 matchweeks, 380 matches. Board confidence starts at 60.

### Pre-season

Up to three friendlies against a random club-season that finished 8th or lower (or is in progress), at a neutral venue with importance 0.9. Each friendly costs 60 per cent of normal condition, adds 9 sharpness and 8 familiarity to those who played, then everybody recovers 14 condition. Statistics are not counted; injuries are.

### The weekly loop

`playMatchweek` (`src/game/logic/week.ts`) runs in this order:

1. If the assistant is managing (Sim 5, Sim to end), any pending event is resolved with its second option and the team is re-picked; otherwise the lineup is repaired only if it has problems (empty slot, duplicate, injured or suspended starter).
2. Your match, with the training focus and any one-off boost applied to λ, then the other nine fixtures.
3. Any cup tie scheduled after this matchweek.
4. Training injuries, weekly recovery, wages paid out of the budget.
5. Board confidence update and, with job security on, a possible sacking.
6. Transfer window open, advance or close.
7. A 42 per cent chance of a dressing-room event (not after matchweek 38).

Your match's other fixtures are already decided when it starts (step 2 plays them in the same loop that begins yours; a live cup tie starts after the round's other ties), so the match-day views reveal them minute by minute by their own goal minutes rather than simulating anything. With `hold`, the last twenty minutes of a live match stay open (clock 90, `game.live` kept) for changes with the ball in play until `completeLiveMatch`, which does exactly what the unheld path does; a held match left open is completed first by the next `playMatchweek`. When the last match of the season is played live, the match screen keeps the final whistle until you continue; the season closes (`seasonEnding`) when you leave it, or when the assistant plays on. Substitutions made at half time and at the 70th minute are written back into the lineup, so the kick-off XI is snapshotted (`onAt === 0`) before the result is applied, for the appearance and promise bookkeeping.

### The assistant's briefing

`weeklyBriefing(game, u)` (`src/game/logic/briefing.ts`) is a pure, RNG-free function that turns the game state into at most `BRIEFING_CAP` (6) lines, each a `BriefingItem` with a level (`block`, `warn`, `info`, `ok`), a sentence and an optional action (a dugout tab to open and, where needed, an op: repair the lineup, open a player, scroll to the pending decision, play a friendly). The dugout renders it inside the next-match panel in place of the old chip row; it is empty once the season is over or the manager is sacked.

In season the lines come in this priority order: the pending decision (`block`); each `lineupProblems` entry by name with an Auto-pick button, collapsed into one `n lineup issues: ...` line when there are three or more; starters below `BRIEFING_CONDITION` (60) condition or, failing that, below `BRIEFING_SHARPNESS` (50) sharpness, opening the player (injured and suspended starters are left to the lineup line); a starter whose yellow cards stand one short of a multiple of `YELLOWS_PER_BAN` (5); a promised start who is not in the XI; the window as `info` (`January window open, day 3 of 7 · 2 offers for your players`, or `Deadline day ...` on the last day) opening the Market tab; the midweek tie (`Fourth round v Luton midweek. Rotate, you play again Thursday`) opening the Cup or Europe tab; each board objective at risk by `objectiveRisk` (home form one defeat before the limit or past it, a derby lost, a cup exit short of the round, and from `PACE_FROM_MATCHWEEK` (8) a goals or youth-starts projection, `(goals so far / matchweek) × matchweeks` rounded, below the target); and finally one `ok` line, `Training Balanced, team talk Encourage for Arsenal (1st)`, opening the Tactics tab so those knobs are seen to exist. `ok` lines are dropped as soon as any `warn` or `block` line exists, then the cap is applied, so the briefing can only grow with real problems.

In pre-season the same function returns a checklist with `done` marks: three friendlies played (with a Play friendly action), a captain named (the action opens the suggested leader, `suggestedCaptain`: the highest `(mentality − 75) × (0.6 + 0.8 × professionalism)`, plus 10 for a starter, which is the engine's `leadership` modifier with its pivot at `LEADER_MENTALITY` (75), so a man under 75 mentality is never suggested over one above it), roles reviewed (any `tactics.roles[slot]` differing from `DEFAULT_ROLE`), staff hired (the line states the wage room under the cap and that the cheapest hire, a senior physio, costs £15k a week), set-piece takers named, and in Dynasty the youth intake answered. Each line is a short label ("Friendlies 0/3", "Captain", "Roles", "Staff 1/4", "Set pieces", "Youth intake") with its explanation in `hint`, shown behind an Info button; the friendlies line has no button of its own because the panel's Play friendly button does it. Done lines are `ok`, undone lines `info`, so nothing hides them.

On screens of 720px or narrower the panel's Play, Sim 5 and Sim to end buttons (and Play friendly in pre-season) move into a fixed bottom bar (`.cta-bar`, padded for the safe-area inset) with the briefing's headline (`briefingHeadline`: the first undone line) beside them, so the reason a disabled Play button is disabled is next to it. The page carries `has-bottom-bar` and its bottom padding is the bar's measured height plus 20px, so the last panel and the event card's choice buttons are never under the bar; the bar renders only in the dugout (phases `preseason` and `week`) while the season is live, never on the match screen.

### Condition, sharpness, familiarity, morale

| System | Rule |
| --- | --- |
| Condition recovery | `(31 + (physical − 70) × 0.12 + training recovery) × 0.93 if 30+ or 0.85 if 33+` per week, capped at 100. A week's rest covers one match for a fit player; two matches in a week do not |
| Sharpness | +12 for a start, +6 for a substitute appearance, −6 for a week without a match (floor 30), −10 on return from injury (floor 40), plus the weekly training delta (clamped 20 to 100) |
| Familiarity | +4 for a start, +2 as a sub, each multiplied by `clamp(1 − (55 − work rate) / 100, 0.8, 1)` and rounded (a start is +3 at work rate 35, +4 from 55 up; a sub stays +2), plus the training delta; −12 for everyone on a formation change mid-season |
| Morale after a match | +5 win, 0 draw, −5 defeat, plus `(match rating − 6.8) × 2`, clamped 10 to 100 |
| Playing-time morale | Weekly drift against the squad status (next section): short of the expected share by more than 0.15, `(gap + 0.15) × 12 × (1.3 − 0.6 × professionalism)`; above it by more than 0.15, `(gap − 0.15) × 4`; clamped ±5 a week. This replaced the flat bench penalty |
| Promises | A promised start kept: +5; broken: −16 and an inbox item |
| Suspensions | Every fifth yellow card: one match. Red card: three matches if the match rating was under 5.5, otherwise two. Served one match at a time while he sits out |
| Injuries | Countdown one week per matchweek; cup weeks do not count extra |

### Squad status and playing time

`src/engine/status.ts`. Every contract carries a status, and a player's morale follows the minutes he gets against what the status promised.

| Status | Expected share of minutes | Wage multiplier on signing |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Key player | 0.85 | ×1.25 |
| First team | 0.65 | ×1.10 |
| Rotation | 0.35 | ×1.00 |
| Backup | 0.15 | ×0.90 |
| Prospect | 0.10 | ×0.80 |

- **Defaults.** When a season begins (`beginSeason`, after the opening eleven is auto-picked) every contract without a status gets one by rank in the squad: the top four by overall are key players, the next seven first team, the next six rotation, the rest backup; anyone aged 21 or under who rates below the squad median is a prospect. A player outside the opening eleven starts one step lower than his rank says (a second striker rated fourth in the squad is first team, not a key player), because the shape, not his rating, decides how much he can play. A contract written before statuses existed reads as rotation.
- **Minutes.** `SquadPlayer.recentMinutes` keeps the last six matchweeks for the user's squad only (AI clubs do not track it). League and cup minutes in the same week add to one entry, so a two-match week can exceed 90; a week without an appearance records 0; a bye week records nothing. The share is `min(1, total / (90 × weeks recorded))`.
- **Drift.** In the weekly recovery, with at least one week recorded: `gap = share − expected`. Below −0.15 the player loses `(gap + 0.15) × 12 × (1.3 − 0.6 × professionalism)` morale; above +0.15 he gains `(gap − 0.15) × 4`; the weekly change is clamped to ±5. A key player who does not play loses 5 a week; a backup who starts every match gains 2.8; a rotation player on anything from 20 to 50 per cent is left alone. The flat bench penalty this replaced is gone, not stacked under it. On seed `status-flow` (Burnley 2025-26, 4-3-3, 20 auto-managed weeks) the mean squad morale is 94.7 against 92.2 before; the drain now lands on the one 85-rated striker stuck behind a 90 rather than on whoever last missed two matches.
- **Events.** The wants-to-talk event also fires for a key or first-team player whose share over the last three recorded matchweeks is more than 0.25 under his expectation, at weight 6 instead of 3, with the shortfall spelled out in the text and a fourth choice, "tell him he can leave": +6 morale and the transfer list. The contract event now offers three answers: renew as first team (a key player stays key) on +10 per cent wages (+10 morale), renew as rotation on the same money (+8 morale for a rotation or lower player; a key or first-team player refuses with probability `0.7 − 0.5 × professionalism` and runs his contract down, otherwise signs at −6), or let him go.
- **Market.** A bid carries a status (the market suggests the one his rating would earn in your squad). The player refuses rotation or lower when he would be among your four best, backup or lower when he is at or above your median, and prospect past the age of 21; the refusal names the lowest status he would take (`counterStatus`). His wage is the listed wage times the multiplier, and the wage-cap check uses that figure. A signing's wage is written into `customPlayers`, the pattern renewals already used; loans keep the parent club's wage and sign as rotation. The Club tab changes a status at any time: a promotion is +6 morale and the wage scaled by the ratio of the two multipliers, a demotion −6 morale with the wage kept.
- **Dynasty and academy.** Statuses travel through the summer in all three contract branches; after ageing recomputes the wage from the new value the status multiplier is applied again. Academy signings arrive as prospects.

### The cup

Sixteen of the twenty clubs are drawn (you are always in). Rounds are played midweek after matchweeks 6 (labelled Fourth round), 14 (quarter-final), 24 (semi-final) and 36 (final, neutral venue). Drawn ties go to penalties: each kick scores with probability `0.72 + (team overall − 70) × 0.003`, five each then sudden death. Cup matches use your current lineup, so rotation is on you; knocked-out teams get free midweeks.

### The Continental Cup

Optional at setup for Season and Dynasty (`settings.continental`), and earned by right in later Dynasty seasons by finishing in the top four or lifting the trophy (`game.qualifiedContinental`). `buildContinentalClubs` takes up to four clubs per other loaded league in the same season (nearest season if that league lacks it), ordered by final position then the average of their top fourteen ratings, tops up from the strongest remaining clubs when fewer leagues are loaded, and keeps the fifteen strongest. You are the sixteenth entrant. Rounds fall on the midweeks the domestic cup leaves free: after matchweeks 10, 19, 29 and 33 (scaled for 34-matchweek leagues). Ties are single-leg with penalties, the final is neutral, and importance runs 1.15 (European night), 1.35 (semi-final) and 1.5 (final). Prize money by round reached: £4M, £8M, £15M, £25M, £45M for winners. Winning unlocks Kings of the Continent; league, cup and Europe together unlock The Treble. The knockout flow in `week.ts` is shared by both competitions (`Competition = 'cup' | 'continental'`), with `clubOf` resolving an opponent from the league or the continental field.

### Man-marking

`markTarget` on the user's match input (set from the opposition report, cleared after the match). At kick-off the target is flagged as marked and his effective rating drops by three; while the flag holds, his weight as a scorer and as a creator from attack is multiplied by 0.6 and from midfield by 0.85 (the shares themselves are untouched, so a substitute who takes his slot inherits a clean copy), the opponent's expected goals are multiplied by 0.93 and the marking side's attack modifier by 0.97. The report notes it, and the modifier is re-applied after the half-time recompute.

### Press conferences

Three media events join the deck, rolled like any other after a matchweek: a post-match conference after a league win (single out the man of the match or scorer: +7 for him, −3 for up to two unprofessional stars; credit the squad: +2 all; promise more: +2 board confidence) or defeat (take the blame: +3 morale, −2 confidence; call it unacceptable: +2 for professionals, −5 for the rest, +2 confidence; blame the referee: 40 per cent chance of a £0.5M fine), and the job question from matchweek 12 when you sit more than four places below the board's target with confidence under 45 (claim the board's backing: a coin flip between +6 and −6 confidence).

### Captain and set pieces

`captainId` is passed into the match engine. A captain on the pitch contributes `leadership = clamp((mentality − 75) / 100 × (0.6 + professionalism × 0.8), −0.25, 0.25)`: his side's expected goals are multiplied by `1 + 0.06 × leadership` and the opponent's by `1 − 0.04 × leadership`. Off the pitch, a captain with professionalism ≥ 0.6 and mentality ≥ 72 lifts every squad player below 70 morale by one a week (two below 50).

`tactics.setPieces` names a penalty taker (he takes every penalty while on the pitch), a free-kick taker and a corner taker. The specialists add `((passing − 72) / 100) × 0.06` for corners and `((shooting − 72) / 100) × 0.05` for free kicks to the side's expected-goal multiplier, clamped to −0.04..+0.07, only while they are on the pitch. AI clubs use the automatic choice.

| Praise | form < 6.5 | −4: he thinks you are being sarcastic |

### In-season development

`src/game/logic/development.ts`, run after matchweeks 5, 10, 15 and so on (never the last). Growth per block for a player who played every minute: +0.9 at 20 or under, +0.55 to 23, +0.2 to 26, 0 to 29, −0.15 to 32, −0.3 from 33. Positive growth is scaled by the square root of the share of available minutes he played, by `1 + 0.25 × coach tier`, and capped at `potential − overall` with `potential = overall + max(0, 27 − age) × 1.3`. Each attribute moves by the probabilistically rounded delta, the five depth attributes with the rest (keepers: goalkeeping, mentality, passing, physical, stamina, strength, jumping); pace, physical and stamina lose an extra point sometimes when fading. The overall is recomputed and value scales by 1.21 per point. The developed player is stored in `customPlayers` under the same id, so lineups and saves stay valid, and the modal shows the change since the start of the season. A development report lands in the inbox.

### Youth intake

`src/game/logic/youth.ts`, Dynasty only, built in `beginSeason`. Candidates are real player-seasons aged 20 or under rated 58 to 76 within the era filter and not already owned: up to two from any of the host club's real squads across the seasons (tagged Homegrown), topped up to three from the wheel leagues. Signing one costs nothing and gives a three-year contract; passing records the decision. The offer lasts through pre-season.

### Club legends

`finishSeason` accumulates every squad player's appearances, goals, assists, man-of-the-match awards, seasons and titles into `game.careerStats` keyed by the real player id, so a player who ages through several dynasty seasons keeps one record. The Club tab lists the top twelve.

### Board objectives

`src/engine/objectives.ts`. Two per season, drawn at kick-off from a pool shaped by the board target: a cup quarter-final (semi-final for a top-four target), a league goals tally (1.5 × matchweeks, 1.9 for the ambitious), at most four home defeats (two), ten league starts for a player aged 21 or under, a Continental Cup quarter-final when entered, and no derby defeats when the host has rivals in the league. Progress shows live in the Club tab; at the end of the season each one met adds £5M to the budget and +8 confidence, each one missed costs 6 confidence, and the inbox gets the verdict.

### Board, confidence and the sack

- **Target.** Count the AI clubs whose (calibrated) overall exceeds yours minus 1.5, add one, clamp to 1 to 17. The inbox welcome message states it.
- **Confidence** per matchweek: +2.5 for a win, −3 for a defeat, 0 for a draw; from matchweek 6, +1.5 if you are at or above the target, else `−min(4, (position − target) × 0.8)`. Clamped 0 to 100.
- **Sacked** if job security is on, matchweek ≥ 10, confidence < 12 and the season is not over. The season ends immediately with tier RELEGATED and no prize money.
- Dropping below 20 at any point and surviving unlocks the Survivor achievement.

### Finances

- Starting budget is `clamp(0.3 × host club's real squad value, 30, 250)` GBP millions.
- Wage cap is the larger of the club's natural bill, `clamp(5 × squad value, 900, 4500)` GBP thousands a week, and your drafted squad's wages with 15 per cent headroom, so the board always underwrites the squad you drafted. In Dynasty the cap is recomputed each summer; the budget carries over and the season's income, spending and wages-paid counters reset.
- Weekly cash flow: match-day and broadcast income is assumed to cover the wage cap, so `(wage cap − wage bill) / 1000` GBP millions is added to the budget every matchweek. Under the cap the budget grows; over it the budget drains.
- Prize money at season end: `100 − 3.2 × (position − 1)` GBP millions, plus 12 for the cup. Nothing if sacked.
- Squad cap 23. Releasing a player is free and returns nothing.

Reasons: injury-prone (0.45+, very from 0.6), condition under 80, low stamina on tired legs, 240+ minutes over the last three recorded weeks (shown as a load warning: the engine feels minutes through condition), fitness training. Injured players are listed apart. The briefing warns about the riskiest high-band starter when his condition is under 80. Over 600 seeded matches high-band starters are hurt more than 1.5 times as often as low-band ones (about 3.6 times in the test) |
| Squad planner (`planner.ts`) | For each of the ten positions: everyone who can play there (natural or accomplished), rated there, best first; who is next in (the best available player outside the XI); a need on a formation position with fewer natural players than `max(2, places + 1)`, all naturals 30 or older, or all of them out of contract |
| Contracts overview (`contractsOverview`) | Years left, wage, status; expiring (the last year and not renewed, declined, or a loan) first; the wages leaving in the summer and the wages committed |
| Personality (`personality.ts`) | The first rule that holds: model professional (professionalism 0.70+, consistency 0.62+), temperamental (professionalism under 0.46, consistency under 0.5), mercurial (big match 0.68+, consistency under 0.5), big-game player (big match 0.66+), professional (professionalism 0.68+), steady (consistency 0.68+), fickle (consistency under 0.43), casual (professionalism under 0.46), driven (mentality 80+), balanced. Shown in the player modal and the hierarchy |
| League awards (`leagueAwards.ts`) | `computeAwards` over the user's squad and every league rival's squad states (all competitions), at the summary: player of the season, golden boot, playmaker, golden glove, young player and the team of the season, the user's players marked. | Inbox actions (`inboxActions.ts`) | `NewsItem.playerId` (optional) names the player an injury, ban, promise or player event is about (team-wide events such as press conferences name nobody). Monthly awards (manager and player of the month) are not built: they need a per-matchweek rating record the states do not keep.

### Inbox

A rolling feed of up to `NEWS_CAP` (120) items, newest first: results, injuries, suspensions, board, transfers, awards, events, cup, training, info. Every item goes through `postNews` (`src/game/logic/news.ts`), which trims the feed to the cap and counts the item in `game.newsTotal`, the number of items ever posted this season (an optional save field; an older save starts it from the feed's length). `game.newsSeen` (optional) is `newsTotal` when the Inbox tab was last opened; `beginSeason` sets `newsTotal` to the opening items and `newsSeen` to 0, so a new season's opening news is unread, and a save without `newsSeen` counts as everything read. The unread count is `newsTotal − newsSeen`, measured on the counter rather than the feed's length because the feed stops growing at the cap. The Inbox tab shows that count as a badge (`99+` above 99; it takes the place of the pending-event dot). Opening the tab writes `newsSeen` (only when something is unread or the field is missing, so a visit to a read inbox never rewrites the save), marks the unread rows, says `n new since your last visit`, and draws a divider under the last new item when older items follow it; a batch that arrives while the tab is open (a sim run from it) moves the marking to that batch.

### While you were away

Sim 5 and Sim to end (`store.simWeeks`) end on a digest of the run, a transient store field (`digest`, never saved) shown as a modal over the dugout. Before the mutation the store takes `digestSnapshot(game)`: plain numbers read from the saved state, never an Immer draft (the last matchweek played, `newsTotal`, the league position from `computeTable`, board confidence). After the loop `buildDigest(before, game)` (`src/game/logic/digest.ts`, pure and RNG-free) collects:

- the results: every league fixture of yours with `matchweek` in (from, to], then every cup and continental tie of yours with a result and `afterMatchweek` in that range, labelled by round, a level tie with its penalty score; sorted by matchweek with the league match before the midweek tie. League results are W, D or L; a knockout tie is W when you went through and L when you went out;
- the position before and after, the league points of those weeks only, and confidence before and after;
- the new inbox items, `news.slice(0, newsTotal − before.newsTotal)`, flagged `truncated` when more were posted than the 120-item feed still holds, split by kind into injuries, suspensions, transfers and development (the training reports);
- the decisions: when the assistant resolves a pending event during a managed week (it takes the second option, as before) `playMatchweek` first posts an `event` item titled `Assistant decided: <event title>` with the chosen option's label as its body, so the choice is on record in the Inbox; the digest lists each one with the outcome text from the event's own item, the nearest event item of the same title above the record and short of that week's result (a training setback may sit between them).

The modal is titled `Matchweeks 12 to 16` (`Matchweek 16` for a single week). It shows a strip of result chips (opponent badge, score, H or A, dashed for a cup or Europe tie, green for W and red for L), `3W 2D 0L · 9th to 6th, 11 points from 5 league matches` (`Still 6th` when unchanged, and `6th in the table` for a run from pre-season, when the table before it is only an order of clubs on no points), two confidence bars (before, then after), the groups `Decided for you`, Injuries, Suspensions, Transfers and Development at most `DIGEST_GROUP_CAP` (8) lines each with `and N more in the Inbox`, a line when the run was quiet, the Inbox's unread count, read live from the game exactly as the tab badge counts it (this run's items plus any left unread before it; `Nothing unread` when the run was simulated with the Inbox open, which marks the batch read as it lands) and a `To the dugout` button (Escape closes it too). Starting or resuming another game clears it. There is no digest when nothing was played, or when the run ended the season: the season summary covers that. A sacking ends the season too, so a run that ends in the sack goes to the summary without a digest.

## 11. The transfer market

`src/engine/market.ts` and `src/game/logic/marketOps.ts`.

**Windows.** The January window opens after matchweek 19 with 36 listings and 7 market days at price level 1.05. Two days pass automatically with each matchweek you play, and you can advance a day at a time from the Market tab; left alone it shuts after matchweek 23. Dynasty summers open a 40-listing, 10-day window at price level 1.0 that stays open through pre-season and closes at kick-off. Listings respect your era filters.

**Listings** are sampled from club-seasons across the eras, one to three players each, biased to the top of each squad, never a human you already own, and in a one-nation game (section 5) only players of that nation from club-seasons that hold one. Suitors: 1 to 3 for an 85+, 0 to 2 for a 78+, else 0 to 1.

**Asking price** starts from value × price level, then:

| Rule | Multiplier | Tag |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Overall 88+ | ×1.35 | World class premium |
| Overall 84 to 87 | ×1.15 | Star premium |
| Age ≤ 22 and overall ≥ 75 | ×1.15 | High potential |
| Age 33+ | ×0.70 | Veteran discount |
| Won the league that season | ×1.08 | Title winner |
| Last five ratings average ≥ 7.6 (own players) | ×1.15 | In form |
| Average ≤ 6.3 over 3+ games | ×0.85 | Out of form |
| Injured 6+ weeks | ×0.60 | Long-term injury |
| Injured | ×0.90 | Injured |

Prices round to the nearest £1M above 20, £0.5M above 5, else £0.1M.

**Each market day:**

- A listing with suitors has a 35 per cent chance of rising by `4% × suitors` (Bidding war).
- An 80+ player gains a suitor 8 per cent of the time.
- With two or more suitors and no bid from you, 12 per cent chance he signs for a league rival (Gone).
- On the last day, unwanted players (no suitors) drop 15 per cent 60 per cent of the time (Deadline discount).
- 60 per cent chance of one to three new listings.
- For each of your players, `desirability = (overall − 72) / 20 + 0.2 if aged ≤ 25 + 0.3 if morale < 45`; if positive, a `5% × desirability` chance of an offer at 75 to 110 per cent of his asking price from a league club. Accepting banks the fee and releases him; other open offers for him are withdrawn.

**AI signings are real.** When a listing goes to a league rival (two or more suitors, 12 per cent a day; one suitor, 4 per cent), the player-season joins that club's squad and state for the rest of the season, as does any player you sell to them.

**Loans.** Fringe players and youngsters (`loanable`: age ≤ 23 and overall ≤ 83, or overall ≤ 76) are listed with a loan fee of 12 per cent of asking (minimum £0.2M) about half the time. A loan signing pays the fee and full wages, carries `contract.loan`, and goes back to his club in the Dynasty summer.

**Part-exchange.** A bid may include one of your players. The selling club takes him only if he is within eight rating points of the man they lose, under 34 and not injured for four weeks or more, and credits 80 per cent of his asking price (60 per cent past thirty) towards theirs. The bid is judged on cash plus credit.

**Approaches.** Any player in the loaded database can be approached from the market tab (or the shortlist). The club quotes asking × premium by his standing in their squad: ×1.6 for a top-three player, ×1.4 top eight, ×1.25 top fourteen, else ×1.1, tagged Unsolicited approach; one of their top three rated 86+ is refused outright 30 per cent of the time (50 if they won the league). Approached players never sign elsewhere, and fringe ones may be loaned.

**Bidding.** You must have the fee in the budget and room under the wage cap, and fewer than 23 players. Contract length is your choice (one to four years; two at most for a 33-year-old), and so is the squad status he signs on, which sets his wage demand and which he may refuse (section 10, Squad status and playing time). At 97 per cent of asking or more: accepted. Between 85 and 97: accepted with probability `(ratio − 0.85) / 0.12 × 0.7` (+0.15 if nobody else is interested), otherwise a counter at 94 to 100 per cent of asking. Below 85: laughed out, asking +5 per cent, tagged Annoyed. Signings join the bench at familiarity 20, sharpness 65 on a three-year deal (one year at 32+). Signing for under 70 per cent of the opening price unlocks Dealmaker.

### In the record books

`src/engine/records.ts` is pure lookups over the real league tables already shipped in `u.tables` (the season tables of each pack plus England's historic 1992-93 to 2007-08 tables: the three 22-club seasons of 42 games until 1994-95 form their own peer group; points are net of any deduction, and `realChampion` names nobody for a season in `u.voidTitles`); nothing in it touches the RNG.

- **Complete seasons only.** `historicRows(u, leagueId)` returns every row of every complete real season, oldest first. `LeagueSeasonRow` carries no `played`, so a row's matches are `won + drawn + lost`; a season counts when every row adds up to `2 × (clubs − 1)`, which skips the season in progress (`u.currentSeason`, 2026-27 with four to seven matches played); seasons that ship without a table (the Bundesliga's 2008-09 and 2009-10, La Liga before 2012-13, Serie A before 2013-14) never reach `u.tables` (`buildLeague` drops empty tables), and an empty table would be skipped too. With the 2026-10-24 data that is 26 Premier League seasons (520 club-seasons of 38 matches), 14 La Liga, 13 Serie A and 16 Bundesliga seasons (288 club-seasons of 34 matches); Ligue 1, the Eredivisie and the Primeira Liga ship without tables and return nothing.
- **Same length only.** `benchmarkSeason(u, leagueId, row)` compares the user's season with the historic rows of the same `played`, so a 34-game Bundesliga campaign is never measured against a 38-game one and a sacked or otherwise unfinished season matches nothing. Five metrics: points, wins and goals scored (more is better), goals conceded and defeats (fewer is better). Each reports `rank` (1 + the historic rows strictly better), `of` (historic rows compared), `equal` (rows level with it) and `better` (the top three ahead of it, best first, ties broken by season then club id). `records` lists the metrics where the season stands alone at the top (`rank === 1` and `equal === 0`): Manchester City's real 2017-18 ranks first on points but ties itself, so it is not an outright record, while 101 points is. `seasons` is the number of distinct seasons compared and a league has a record book only from `RECORD_BOOK_MIN_SEASONS = 10` of them (`hasRecordBook`).
- **Real champions.** `realChampion(u, leagueId, season)` is the position-one row of a complete table, null for a missing table or the season in progress.
- **Summary panel.** "In the record books" sits under the tier banner when the league has a record book and the season was not cut short: one row per metric with the figure, its all-time standing ("5th best of 521", "Joint best", or a gold "League record" chip with a scroll icon), the three seasons ahead of it (hidden on a phone), and, behind the title's info button, how many seasons it is measured against and the real champions of the season you played in. Under Calibrated difficulty one line says these are benchmarks, not records, with the reason (the AI clubs were handicapped, section 13) behind an info button. It is distinct from the Stats tab's within-season "Season records" (streaks, biggest win).
- **Share text.** `recordShareLine` adds `📜 New Premier League points record: 101`, `📜 Joint-best points total in Premier League history` or `📜 5th-best points total in Premier League history` to `extraLines` for a top-ten points total; a mid-table season adds nothing.
- **Achievement.** `record-points` ("Record Books") unlocks in both `finishSeason` and `runQuickSeason` through `earnsPointsRecord` (`src/game/logic/records.ts`): Realistic difficulty, not sacked, `won + drawn + lost` equal to the season's matchweeks, and an outright points record in a league with a record book. Calibrated hundred-point seasons stay benchmarks.
- **Dynasty history** gains a "Real champions" column from each `SeasonRecord`'s `leagueId` and `leagueSeason`, a dash where no complete table exists.

### Season, game by game

`src/game/logic/gameByGame.ts`. The quick season has always played all 380 fixtures with the real match engine; `recordUserGames` now keeps the user's 38 (34 in an 18-club league) as `QuickResult.results`, one `QuickGameResult` per matchweek in the order they were played: `matchweek`, `opponentId`, `home`, `gf`, `ga`, the user's `scorers` (every `goal`, `penalty` or `own_goal` event on the user's side, ordered by minute, with the player-season id and name; an opponent's own goal is credited as "OG" with no id) and `position`, the user's league place after that matchweek.

- **Pure bookkeeping.** It runs after the fixture loop over fixtures that already carry their results, so it draws no random numbers: the final table, the RNG state and the top scorer of seed `game-by-game` (Burnley 2025-26, 4-3-3, auto-drafted) are pinned in a test against the values the engine produced before the recording existed (Arsenal 80 points, the user second on 79 with 24W 7D 7L and 87-44). The position is computed once per matchweek, not per fixture: 38 calls to `computeTable` over at most 380 fixtures.
- **The field is optional** and read with `?? []`, so a save or a leaderboard entry written before it existed still opens; nothing about the replay is stored.
- **The panel.** "Season, game by game" sits directly under the tier banner on the summary of a quick draft or the Daily, newest game first by default with a "Newest / Oldest" switch (the choice is a per-viewer convenience in `localStorage` key `touchline-gbg-order`, inside try/catch; `gamesInOrder`, pure and tested). Each row is tinted by the result (green win, amber draw, red defeat) and reads: a W, D or L badge, the opponent's badge and name with an H or A tag, the score as `gf-ga`, "MW n · 4th" (the matchweek and the position after it) and, on a second line, the scorers as "⚽ Haaland 12', 67' · OG 80'" (minutes grouped per scorer in order of his first goal, initials dropped). The row is a grid of 28 / 22 / 1fr / auto / auto px (plus 30 px for the Highlights button) with the scorers under it, so it holds at 390 px wide. The head-to-head lists stay in matchweek order.
- **Folded.** `GameByGame` takes `fold` to show only the first games with a "Show all 38 games" button (`shownGames` in `src/ui/screenText.ts`); while the replay runs every revealed game shows, and "Show fewer" folds it again. The quick summary does not fold its list: on a desktop it has its own box and scrolls inside it. The head-to-head lists are not folded (they scroll inside their panel).
- **The replay** (`useSeasonReplay`) is UI state only. Everything is shown on arrival; "Play the season" empties the list and reveals one game every `REPLAY_STEP_MS = 220` ms (8.4 s for 38 games), the strip and the position update as games land, the banner shows "Season in play…" with the running strip in place of the tier and record, and the tier pops in after the last game. When the banner shows its result the W, D and L and the points count up from zero over 0.4 s, and a title earns a gold flare behind the tier name (the perfect season adds a burst of confetti), the first time that result is shown in a visit and again after every replay; all of it is UI only and off under reduced motion. "Skip" shows the rest at once; `prefers-reduced-motion` offers no replay button and animates nothing. The head-to-head result shows both managers' lists (`H2HResult.results`, the two derbies against the other manager included) without the replay.
- **Share text.** `bestWinLine` adds `Best win: 5-0 v Chelsea (MW 23)` (or `3-0 at Chelsea`) to `extraLines`: the widest margin, then the most goals scored, then the earliest matchweek; a season without a win adds nothing.

### The quick season: kick-off, playback, January and Europe

`src/game/logic/quick.ts`, `src/game/logic/january.ts`, `src/game/logic/odds.ts`, `src/ui/screens/KickOff.tsx`, `src/ui/components/PreSeasonOdds.tsx`, `src/ui/screens/SeasonPlayback.tsx`. After the draft of a Quick Draft or the Daily the season is no longer instant.

- **Phases.** `finishDraft` calls `toKickoff`: the XI is set (`quickXI`, the draft's lineup with empty slots filled by `autoPickLineup`) and `Game.phase` becomes `kickoff`. `kickOff(game, u, skip)` plays the whole season (`runQuickSeason`) or, with `GameSettings.january`, the first half only (`openJanuary`, phase `january`); `closeJanuary(game, u, index | null)` takes one listing or passes and plays the season out. Phase `summary` then follows as before. `Phase` gained `kickoff` and `january`; older saves never hold either, and `normaliseGame` sends a save in `january` without its `quickJanuary` back to `kickoff`, from which the same season replays.
- **Squad complete and the pre-season odds** (`src/game/logic/odds.ts`, `SquadComplete`): the kick-off screen opens on a "Squad complete" panel. `oddsLeague` builds the league about to be played (the XI as `quickXI` fields it with the manager's tactics and captain, the AI clubs with the season's calibration) and works out every home-and-away pairing's expected goals once, with the engine's own pre-match sums (`pairingExpectation` in `match.ts`: strengths, tactical modifiers, marking, set pieces, aerial power and leadership, with no match-day variance and no team talk; `beginMatch` calls the same helpers, and draws exactly what it drew before). `sampleSeasons` then plays `ODDS_SEASONS = 2000` seasons from those numbers: each fixture draws both sides' match-day variance (log-normal with the engine's consistency spread) and Poisson goals, and the table sorts on points, goal difference, goals scored and club id as `computeTable` does. That is about a thousandth of the cost of playing every match through the engine, and over six seeds the mean expected points sit within a few points of the seasons the engine plays out (tested). The odds draw from `Rng.fromState(quickStartRng ?? rngState).fork('pre-season-odds')`, a fork of a copy, so the same squad and tactics always show the same odds and the season itself never moves; nothing is saved. The panel shows the projected finish (the median place), expected points (the mean), the chance to win the league, finish top 4, top 6 and top 10 and go down (bottom three), printed as "52.6%", "<0.1%", "0%" (`chanceLabel`), and the XI's Overall, Attack, Midfield, Defence and Goalkeeper ratings as bars (`squadBreakdown`, `barFill`: 50 empty, 95 full; numbers hidden when the draft hides ratings). The UI samples `ODDS_STEP = 250` seasons per idle moment (`usePreSeasonOdds`, `requestIdleCallback` where there is one) and caches the odds by `oddsKey` (the game id, start state, XI, bench, tactics, captain, league, difficulty and draft rules), so a tactics change works them out again after a short pause; the chance and unit bars grow in one after another once the odds land. Display only: `ENGINE_VERSION` stays `'4'`.
- **The tactics step**: folded under the Squad complete panel ("Tactics, captain and penalties"), the drafted XI on a read-only pitch, the captain and the penalty taker (selects over the XI, "Auto" by default), and the six team instructions with the Dugout's labels and help (mentality, tempo, pressing, defensive line, width, passing; `TeamInstructions` in `src/ui/components/TacticsControls.tsx`). The big "Simulate season" button plays with them; "Simulate with the default tactics" (shown with the tactics) puts `defaultTactics` back and clears the captain first. The quick season's user side is `quickUserInput`: the XI with `Game.tactics`, `Game.captainId` and `tactics.setPieces.penalties`, so every choice reaches the match engine. With the defaults it is exactly the input the instant season always used.
- **One stream, split at the halfway matchweek.** `generateFixtures` returns the fixtures in matchweek order, so playing matchweeks 1 to 19 and then 20 to 38 draws exactly what one loop draws. The window opens after `matchweeks / 2` (19 of 38, 17 of 34). `openJanuary` writes nothing to `Game.rngState`: once the window closes `runQuickSeason` plays the whole season again from the same state, the first half with the tactics and captain it kicked off with (`QuickJanuary.firstHalfTactics`, `firstHalfCaptainId`) and the second half with the current ones. Passing with unchanged tactics therefore gives the instant season figure for figure, the RNG state included (tested over three seeds, and against `runQuickSeason` called straight after the draft), and `ENGINE_VERSION` stays `'3'`.
- **The window** (`QuickJanuary`, saved on the game): the position after the first half, a budget of the XI's combined value times a share rising evenly from 8% for the leaders to 20% for the bottom side, at least £5M, in £0.5M steps (`januaryBudget`), and three to five listings (`januaryListings`). The listings shop for the three weakest slots by the drafted player's rating at the slot's position (`weakestSlots`, Prime-aware through `playerLookup`); candidates are every player-season the wheel could have offered (its leagues, era, one club or one nation) who plays that position naturally and whose player is not already in the squad (the XI or the bench, so a substitute never meets himself on the pitch). Every listing but the last is a signing, rated 3 to 15 above the man he replaces, with a fee of his value in £0.5M steps within the budget; the last is a straight swap, no fee, 0 to 5 above. Slots cycle through the three weakest and a slot with no candidate passes to the next, so a narrow wheel can offer fewer. Each listing is drawn from the 25 best candidates for its slot, one season per player (his best that fits the band and the budget, `januaryShortlist`, so one man's many seasons, all alike under Prime, never fill the list), all draws from a fork of a copy of the season's stream (`Rng.fromState(state).fork('january')`), which leaves the season itself untouched. ## 13. Difficulty calibration

This exists in code. `GameSettings.difficulty` is `'calibrated'` (default) or `'realistic'`.

- `aiRatingOffset` (`src/engine/season.ts`) auto-picks every AI club's best eleven, averages their overall team strength and subtracts `CALIBRATED_LEAGUE_AVERAGE = 75`, floored at 0 and rounded to 0.1. Realistic returns 0.
- The offset is subtracted from every unit (and the overall) of every AI club in every match, including quick seasons and pre-season friendly opponents. The board target is computed against the offset strengths. The Club tab shows "rivals play N rating points below their real strength".
- Rationale: a wheel draft rarely assembles eleven 85s, while a modern top club fields exactly that, and the engine stretches ratings above 80. Observed in the test suite at the time of writing, with no offset (realistic): an auto-drafted squad averaging 81.8 overall finished 19th with 32 points in 2025-26, and an elite XI beats a mid-table XI at home 88.8 per cent of the time, with equal sides splitting 42.8 / 28.0 / 29.2 home-draw-away.

## 14. Dynasty

`advanceToNextSeason` (`src/game/logic/dynasty.ts`) runs when you continue from the summary:

1. **Ageing.** Each player gets a new player-season (`${playerId}@dyn${year}`) one year older. A potential is estimated as `overall + max(0, 27 − age) × 1.3 + N(0, 1.5)`, then the common attribute delta by new age is:

| New age | Delta |
| --- | --- |
| ≤ 23 | min(growth room, N(2.2, 1.2)) |
| 24 to 27 | min(growth room, N(0.8, 1.0)) |
| 28 to 30 | N(−0.2, 0.8) |
| 31 to 32 | N(−1.4, 1.0) |
| 33 to 34 | N(−2.6, 1.2) |
| 35+ | N(−4.0, 1.5) |

   Physical attributes lead: at 30+ pace takes an extra −1.6, physical −0.8, dribbling −0.6; at 22 and under pace +0.8, physical +0.4, dribbling +0.3. Passing gains +0.6 and mentality +1.0 at 30+ (+0.3 otherwise). Goalkeeping gains +0.5 until 33, then −0.5. The depth attributes: stamina takes an extra −1.2 and jumping −1.0 at 30+ (+0.6 and +0.5 at 22 and under); strength follows the delta until 29, holds from 30 to 32 (the delta is cancelled, only the noise moves it) and falls with an extra −0.8 from 33; aggression and work rate are character, so the delta is cancelled at every age and only the noise moves them. Measured over 400 seeded summers on a 70-rated set: ageing to 31 moves stamina −2.5, jumping −2.4, strength 0.0, aggression and work rate 0.0; to 33 strength −3.4; to 21 stamina +2.9, strength +2.3, jumping +2.8. Each attribute also gets N(0, 0.6) noise and is clamped 1 to 99. Overall is recomputed from the primary position weights; value is `0.25 × 1.21^(overall − 60)` times an age factor (1.3 at ≤ 21, 1.15 to 26, 1.0 to 29, 0.72 to 31, 0.45 to 33, else 0.25); wage is `5 × value^0.85`.

2. **Retirements.** From 34: 30 per cent; from 36: 60; from 37: 90.
3. **Contracts** lose a year. At zero a player leaves if morale is under 50 or on a 25 per cent roll, otherwise renews for two to three years (one at 32+). Drafted players start on one to three years, signings on three. The squad status carries over, and the wage the new player-season computes from its value is multiplied by the status again.
4. State resets to condition 95, sharpness 60, morale at least 50, familiarity +10, no injury or cards.
5. **Bonus spins** `min(6, 2 + players lost)`. A summer draft opens with squad size = remaining squad + bonus spins and one re-spin. The surviving squad is seeded into it, best eleven on the pitch and the rest on the bench, so every bonus spin adds depth wherever the wheel lands; existing players are marked taken so the uniqueness rule holds.
6. **League.** The league season advances by one real year (after 2026-27 it wraps to 2008-09). You keep the same host club if it was in that division, otherwise a random club from that season. Budget and achievements carry over, the wage cap is recomputed for the new squad, and a new board target is set.
7. A summer window (section 11) is open until kick-off. The offseason modal lists who retired, who left, who improved and who declined.

## 15. Determinism and saves

All randomness runs through one `Rng` (xoshiro128** seeded by a cyrb53 hash) whose state is stored in the save after every action, so a seed replays identically and a daily seed is identical for everyone. A match paused at half time or at the 70th minute stores a forked stream for the rest of the match, so the week's other fixtures never share its numbers. A daily result is filed under the day its seed was set, not the day it was finished. The game is persisted with Zustand `persist` under the `localStorage` key `touchline-save-v1` (the active game, parked games, the shortlist, the head-to-head game, the profile and whether you were in a game), compacted and compressed as described below. Quick drafts and dailies share one save slot; Season and Dynasty each have their own, so starting a quick draft parks a running dynasty on the home screen instead of replacing it. On load, `normaliseGame` (`src/game/logic/save.ts`) fills in every field added since a save was written, for the active game and the parked ones, and the same path covers an imported file; the players a game created (`customPlayers`: dynasty ageing, development, wage changes) that predate the depth attributes get stamina, strength, aggression, jumping and work rate derived from their eight core attributes, age and height with the same `withDepth` the engine uses for an older data pack, so a dynasty saved before format 3 plays on without a `NaN` in its condition or its tactics. The default league must load; a saved game whose other leagues fail to load is parked with a toast rather than rendered against a half-loaded universe, league loads are serialised so two callers never overwrite each other, and a failed fetch is not cached. There is no backend.

### Save size: compaction, compression and a full storage

A browser gives a site about 5 MB of `localStorage` (5 million UTF-16 characters in Chrome), shared by the active game, every parked game, the head-to-head game, the shortlist and the profile. Measured before this change (in memory, `src/game/__tests__/save.test.ts`, and in the browser): one Season at full time was 4.40 million characters of JSON, 4.37 M of it the season (fixtures 3.98 M: the 342 AI-vs-AI results 3.34 M, of which events 1.06 M, player stats 1.33 M, condition loss 0.30 M and the team reports 0.50 M; the user's 38 results 0.64 M; the AI clubs' player states 0.22 M; the cup ties 0.15 M; news 10 K), and everything else (squad, customPlayers, history, awards, career records, the last match) under 50 K. A Season parked at matchweek 30 (3.47 M) plus a Dynasty was refused on its first season's Sim to end, so the save stayed at the pre-season and the season played after it was lost on reload.

Two things now keep it small, and neither changes a result:

- **Compaction** (`compactGame`, `src/game/logic/compact.ts`, run by `partialize` on the active and every parked game). After the week has moved on nothing reads more of a stored result than its score: the tables (Dugout, Summary, the opposition report, the digest), the fixtures list, the season records, the objectives, the briefing and the event context read `homeGoals` and `awayGoals`; the cup and Continental Cup lists add `penalties` and `winnerId`. The match screen shows `game.lastMatch` and `game.lastCupMatch`, which are kept whole, and the league statistics and awards come from the squad's and the AI clubs' player states. So a stored fixture or tie result keeps `homeTeamId`, `awayTeamId`, `homeGoals`, `awayGoals`, `xg` and (ties) `penalties`, with empty `events`, `homeStats` and `awayStats` (still a valid `MatchResult`), except the user's league matches of the current and the previous matchweek and the user's latest tie in each knockout, which stay complete. The match-day dashboard reveals the round's other scores goal by goal at the user's clock, live and in the replay, so the other fixtures of the previous, the current and the in-play matchweek, and the other ties of the round of the user's latest tie and of the tie in play, keep their goal events cut to minute, type, side and scorer (`text` empty, a few dozen bytes a goal): a reload in the middle of a match shows the same latest scores and live table (tested for league and cup). The game in memory keeps its detail until the page reloads; a season played on from a compacted save ends exactly as the one played on in memory (same table, statistics, awards, finances and generator state, tested through the next Dynasty summer). Unbounded lists are capped at the source: the inbox at 120 items (as before) and a transfer window's log at its newest 80 lines (`WINDOW_LOG_CAP`).
- **Compression** (`src/game/saveStorage.ts`). The persisted JSON is written as lz-string `compressToUTF16` (lz-string 1.5.0). On read, a value whose first character is `{` is the plain JSON every earlier build wrote and is parsed as it is; anything else is decompressed. A compressed JSON object never starts with `{`, so old saves (including ones with full match detail on every fixture) load through the same `merge` and `normaliseGame` and are compacted on their next write. Compressing takes about 0.1 s per 400 K characters, so writes are coalesced: a change schedules one write 250 ms later carrying the latest state (a steady stream of changes still writes every 250 ms), and a waiting write goes out at once when the page is hidden or closed.

After both, a Season at full time stores about 49 K characters (4.41 M before: 429 K once compacted, then about 9 to 1 compression); a two-season Dynasty at full time with a Season parked at matchweek 31 stores about 83 K (7.98 M before, 0.80 M compacted). In the browser the same Dynasty with a Season parked at matchweek 30 stored 80 K characters at the end of its first season and 97 K at the end of its second.

**A full storage.** When the browser refuses a write (`QuotaExceededError`), the storage catches it, the game carries on in memory and the stored save stays as it was; a toast says the storage is full and suggests exporting the save or discarding a parked game (shown on the first refused write and every twentieth after, until a write succeeds). **Export and import** (the Profile page): export writes the game in memory as plain, readable JSON (`{ state, version: 1 }`, compacted), so it works even when the storage is full; import accepts plain JSON from any build (an old uncompacted export included, with or without a byte-order mark) and the compressed text of a stored save, compacts it, stores it compressed and reloads; a file that is not a save, or that does not fit, leaves the current save untouched.

### Cross-league seeds, the data stamp and the engine version

`spin()` chooses a season uniformly, then a club-season from that season by weighted index over `u.clubSeasons`, so the order of that array is part of the seed's meaning. `mergeUniverses` (`src/engine/universe.ts`) therefore concatenates league packs in the order of the data index `leagues.json` (England, Spain, Italy, Germany, France, the Netherlands, Portugal), never in the order they happened to be loaded; a pack missing from the index goes last, in load order. Before this, opening Spain before Germany or the other way round gave the same European Nights seed different clubs on nine of eleven quick-draft spins (seed `K7Q2-MX9A`, same eleven seasons). England-only wheels, the Daily included, were never affected; cross-league seeds changed once, with this fix. `h2hSpin` draws from the same pool and inherits the order.

Every `LoadedUniverse` carries `generatedAt`, the `YYYY-MM-DD` stamp of the data packs read from `leagues.json` (`2026-10-25` today, the format 3 packs with the depth columns; a single pack built offline falls back to its own `players.json` stamp). `ENGINE_VERSION` (`src/engine/version.ts`, currently `'4'`: `'1'` was before the wheel twists added a draw to every third spin, `'2'` before the 70th-minute window split every match into three segments, `'3'` before the depth attributes changed every match's expected goals and card rolls) names the engine's random-number consumption and is bumped whenever a change alters how many draws a spin, a pick, a match or a season takes or the order it takes them in. Anything that replays a seed on another device stores both stamps beside the result so a mismatch is reported as unverified rather than failing silently. The quick season's January window and Continental Cup run (section 12) draw from forks of a copy of the season's stream, so neither changed a replayed outcome and neither bumped the version. Nor did the quick-season highlights: they replay the season from its kept start state and draw the reel's extra moments from a fork of a copy. Nor did the Quick Draft modes (section 5): they exist only under the new `modes` rule, Crazy's formation and surprises draw from streams seeded apart from the game's, and Speedrun's auto-pick is an ordinary spin and pick. Nor did pinning the Daily back to 2 re-spins: the budget moves no draw unless a re-spin is taken. Nor did the Quick Draft bench: it only exists under the new `benchPicks` rule, its spins after the XI draw from their own fork, and a season without anyone on the bench plays exactly as before. Nor did the power-ups: they exist only under the new `powerUps` rule, Re-roll season draws from its own seeded stream, Peek spins copies, and Swap, the armband and the lock draw nothing; a draft that uses none spins exactly as without the rule (tested). Nor did Gamble mode and the Player Upgrader: the Icons roll and the upgrade draw from streams seeded apart from the game's and exist only under the new `gamble` flag and `upgrader` rule; a draft without them draws exactly as before (the spin weight moved into `spinWeight` unchanged), and with Gamble on every spin before the Icons spin lands where the same draft without Gamble would (tested). The share text (`shareText`, section 12) takes an optional `extraLines` list, printed after the awards and before the seed line, so a mode can add one line without a new field each time.

### Offline

The site is hosted as a Render static site (build `npm ci && npm run build`, publish `dist`, auto-deployed from the repository); the GitHub Pages workflow was retired when the repository went private. `public/sw.js` is registered on production builds relative to the deploy path. The shell (`index.html`, the manifest) is served network-first and cached as a fallback; hashed assets, icons and league packs are cached on first use and served cache-first, and so are the Google Fonts stylesheet and font files (the one typeface, Sora), so the type still loads offline after one visit. A new build changes the asset names, so it is picked up on the next online visit; stale assets stay in the single cache until its version string is bumped.
